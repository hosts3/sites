How much does a life insurance agent cost in Miami, Florida?

Nothing. Our consultations, quotes, comparison guides and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. How much does term life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?

Rates depend on age, health, tobacco use and coverage length. As a reference point, a healthy 30-year-old Miami applicant can often qualify for $250,000 of 20-year term from approximately $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old may see $500,000 of 20-year term from roughly $58–$75 per month. We run real quotes across 8+ carriers before you decide anything.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Many term policies use accelerated underwriting and can be approved in 24 to 72 hours without a medical exam. Applications requiring a paramedical exam — typically larger face amounts or certain health histories — usually take two to four weeks. Final expense and guaranteed issue policies for ages 50–85 often issue in three to seven days with no exam at all.

Is a life insurance agent comparison guide really better than buying direct?

Yes, for most Miami families. Direct carriers only show you their own product. An independent insurance agency like ours is appointed with 8+ life carriers and 10+ health carriers, so you see competing prices, underwriting classes, riders and waiting periods side by side. Our team has completed more than 5,000 comparisons and policy reviews across Miami-Dade since 2011.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Miami-Dade?

Yes. We are AHIP Medicare certified annually and CMS-compliant for marketing and enrollment. Can I qualify for an ACA Marketplace subsidy in Miami?

Most of our Miami-Dade clients do qualify for premium tax credits. Restaurant, hospitality, salon and construction workers without employer coverage are the most common applicants we help.
