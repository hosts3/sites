How We Ranked These Miami Agencies

Every agency on this list was evaluated against six weighted criteria:

  • Licensing & compliance (20%) — Active Florida Department of Financial Services licenses (2-15 Life/Health/Annuity or 2-40 Health), plus current AHIP Medicare certification and CMS-compliant marketing.
  • Carrier access (20%) — Whether the agency is independent and appointed with multiple carriers, so quotes are compared rather than pushed from a single company.
  • Client ratings (20%) — Google, BBB, and word-of-mouth reputation, with weight given to volume of reviews, not just star averages.
  • Bilingual & cultural service (15%) — English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole support matters in a county where a large share of households prefer a language other than English at home.
  • Transparency (15%) — Free consultations, written quotes, and clear explanations of commissions paid by carriers rather than clients.
  • Post-sale service (10%) — Annual reviews, claims advocacy, and responsiveness after the policy is issued.

Before you enroll: verify any agent's license at no cost through the Florida Department of Financial Services licensee search, and confirm a Medicare plan's star rating and drug formulary on Medicare.gov. Rankings below reflect publicly available ratings and editorial scoring as of publication.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does life insurance cost in Miami for a healthy adult?

Sample rates from Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group: a healthy 30-year-old can get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage from roughly $18–$24 per month, while a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term from about $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 typically run $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. Your actual rate depends on health history, tobacco use, and the carrier's underwriting class.

Do the top rated life and health insurance agents charge a fee?

No. Reputable independent agents are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you. Consultations, quotes, and annual policy reviews should cost nothing. If an agent asks for an upfront fee to "shop" your Medicare or life insurance options, that is a warning sign — walk away and verify their license with the Florida Department of Financial Services.

Can one agent handle both my life insurance and my Medicare plan?

Yes, if the agency holds both a Florida 2-15 Life, Health & Annuity license and current AHIP Medicare certification. Handling both lines with one agency simplifies beneficiary coordination and annual reviews. Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group, for example, writes term life, final expense, Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, and ACA plans under one roof.

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Miami-Dade?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a private network plan, often with a $0 monthly premium and extras like dental or transportation — but you generally stay in-network and may need referrals. Medigap supplements Original Medicare, letting you see any provider nationwide who accepts Medicare, at a higher monthly cost (Plan G in Miami-Dade typically runs about $115–$210 per month depending on age). The right choice depends on your doctors, travel plans, and prescription costs.

Can I get ACA health insurance if I'm self-employed or a gig driver?

Absolutely — self-employed workers and 1099 contractors are exactly who Marketplace coverage is designed for. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits based on estimated annual income, and many end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies. An enrollment agent can help you estimate income correctly, since over- or under-reporting is the most common subsidy mistake.

What if I've been declined for life insurance before?

You still have options. Guaranteed-issue life insurance for ages 50–85 requires no health questions and no medical exam, though it typically includes a two-year graded benefit period. Final expense policies with simplified underwriting also approve many applicants with controlled diabetes, high blood pressure, or a prior cancer history. A good agent will pre-screen you with several carriers before submitting any formal application.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Accelerated-underwriting policies can be approved in 24–72 hours for healthy applicants under 55. Fully underwritten policies requiring a paramedical exam and medical records typically take three to six weeks. Simplified-issue and final expense policies usually issue within one to two weeks.

Do I need to live in Miami to work with these agencies?

Not necessarily. Life insurance agents licensed in Florida can serve clients anywhere in the state by phone or video — Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group is licensed statewide for life insurance. Health and Medicare appointments, however, are best handled in person or by video with an agent familiar with the specific provider networks in your county.
