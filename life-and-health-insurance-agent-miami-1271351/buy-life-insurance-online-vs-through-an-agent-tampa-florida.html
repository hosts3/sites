Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in Tampa, Florida?
Almost always the same price — the premium is filed with the state by the carrier, not set by the agent. The real difference is that an independent Tampa agent compares 8 to 18 carriers at once, corrects rate-class mistakes, and follows up with underwriters. In practice that saves our clients an average of $8–$40 per month on identical coverage.
How much does life insurance cost in Tampa, Florida right now?
As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can get $250,000 of 20-year term for roughly $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 per month. Final expense coverage runs $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 in benefits. Your actual rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use and coverage amount.
How long does it take to get approved and issued?
Accelerated underwriting with no medical exam typically issues in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term policies with labs and medical records usually take 2 to 4 weeks. Guaranteed issue final expense policies are frequently approved the same day you apply. We track every application and chase underwriting so nothing sits in a queue.
Do I need a medical exam to buy life insurance in Florida?
Not always. Many carriers now offer accelerated underwriting that uses prescription history, motor vehicle records and a health questionnaire instead of blood work — often up to $1,000,000 in coverage. If an exam is required, we schedule a paramedical professional to come to your home or office anywhere in Tampa Bay at no cost.
What happens if I already bought a policy online and want it reviewed?
We offer a free, no-obligation policy review for any coverage — including policies we did not sell. We compare your current premium, riders, cash value, conversion privileges and beneficiary designations against 8+ carriers, then tell you plainly whether to keep it, adjust it, or replace it. No cost, no pressure, no obligation to switch.
