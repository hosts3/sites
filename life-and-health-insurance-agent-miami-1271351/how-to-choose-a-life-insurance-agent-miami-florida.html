How do I choose a life insurance agent in Miami Florida without getting overcharged?

Start by confirming the agent is independent rather than captive to one carrier — an independent agent can quote Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, Transamerica, and John Hancock side by side, which is how you find the lowest rate for identical coverage. Second, verify the Florida license number on the Department of Financial Services website. Third, insist on a written quote in writing before you sign. Our consultations are 100% free, and our rate-shopping guarantee means we'll match a lower rate for identical coverage or explain exactly why the difference exists.

How much does life insurance cost in Miami, Florida in 2026?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old looking at $500,000 of 20-year term generally falls between $58 and $75 per month. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 runs about $32–$95 monthly depending on age and benefit amount ($5,000–$25,000). Miami rates are competitive because Florida residents are generally taller and leaner in underwriting tables than the national average — but tobacco use, height/weight, and driving record still move the number significantly.

Do you charge a fee for your services as an insurance agent?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, and enrollment help are completely free — there are no fees, no obligation, and no cost to the client. Agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you. This is why we can offer a free annual policy review for every client, including policies you purchased from a different agent or agency.

How long does it take to get a life insurance policy approved?

It depends on the product. Simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue final expense policies often go active in 24 to 48 hours with no medical exam. Fully underwritten term and permanent policies typically take 7 to 21 days, and sometimes up to 30 days if a medical records request or an APS is needed from your doctor. We track every application daily and will tell you the moment the carrier issues a decision.

Can I get life insurance in Miami with health problems like diabetes or high blood pressure?

Yes, in most cases. We work with carriers that specialize in diabetic and hypertensive applicants, and we know which ones will rate you Table B versus Table D for the same A1C. For ages 50–85 with significant health history, guaranteed-issue whole life requires no health questions and no exam, though the coverage amount is smaller and there's typically a 2-year graded benefit period. We'll show you both options side by side so you can decide.

How do I know if I need term or permanent life insurance?

Choose term if your goal is pure protection for a defined window — raising kids, paying off a 30-year mortgage in Kendall, or replacing income until retirement. Choose permanent whole life or IUL if you want coverage that never expires and builds tax-deferred cash value you can borrow against later. Many Miami clients use a mix: a large term policy for the mortgage plus a smaller permanent policy for final expenses. We'll model both scenarios with real numbers before you commit.
