Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in Fort Lauderdale?

In almost every case, the premium is identical or lower through an agent — because carriers pay agent commissions on top of the policy, not out of it. What changes is access: an independent agent can quote 8 carriers at once, including several (Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, Transamerica) that don't sell direct to consumers. Online engines also tend to display their most favorable underwriting class, which many applicants don't actually qualify for.

How much does life insurance cost in Fort Lauderdale, Florida in 2026?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Fort Lauderdale resident can get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old typically pays $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense plans run $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. Your exact rate depends on age, health class, tobacco use, and riders.

How long does it take to get a policy approved?

No-exam and simplified-issue policies are often approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term and permanent policies with a medical exam typically take 3 to 10 business days, though we've seen cases close in 2 days when records arrive quickly. Your agent tracks the case with the carrier daily and updates you by text or phone.

Do you charge anything for your help?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, and claims advocacy are all 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier you choose, never by you. There are no fees, no retainers, and no hidden charges — and no client is ever asked to commit during the first appointment.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. Guaranteed issue plans for ages 50–85 ask zero health questions, and many simplified-issue products only require a short health questionnaire. No-exam options are also available for mortgage protection and final expense coverage. If you've been declined before, call us — we'll tell you honestly which carriers will and won't work with your history.

Do you help with Medicare in Fort Lauderdale?

Yes. Our agents are AHIP Medicare certified annually and CMS-compliant for marketing and enrollment. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans, and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, and Cigna. I'm self-employed with 1099 income. Can I still qualify?

Absolutely — self-employed workers, gig drivers, and contractors are a large share of our Fort Lauderdale client base. Most carriers accept bank statements, 1099s, or tax returns as proof of income for life insurance. For health coverage, we'll run a free ACA subsidy check; many Broward households pay under $50 per month after premium tax credits.

Bring it in. Our free policy review covers existing coverage — even policies purchased through a different agency or a website. We'll check your death benefit against your current mortgage, income replacement needs, and family situation, verify your beneficiary designations are correct, and tell you plainly whether you're overpaying. Do you help when it's time to file a claim?

That's included at no cost. Our claims advocacy team calls the carrier on your family's behalf, gathers the death certificate and required forms, follows the file to payment, and gives your beneficiaries a single point of contact. We've walked Fort Lauderdale families through this dozens of times — you shouldn't have to navigate a call center while grieving.

What if I find a lower price somewhere else?

Our rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, we'll match it or explain in writing exactly why the policies differ — underwriting class, riders, cash value, conversion privileges, or carrier financial strength.
