How much does health insurance cost for a self-employed worker in Tallahassee, Florida?

It depends almost entirely on your income and household size, not your age alone. Most self-employed Tallahassee clients qualify for ACA premium tax credits, and many households end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies. Without subsidies, individual Marketplace plans in Leon County commonly run $280–$520 monthly in 2026.

Can a self-employed person in Florida qualify for ACA subsidies or Obamacare savings?

Yes — self-employed income counts. If your projected household income falls between 100% and 400% of the federal poverty level, you likely qualify for a premium tax credit. We help you document 1099 income, Schedule C profit and deductions correctly so your subsidy is maximized and protected at tax time.

Do you charge any fees for finding me a plan?

No. Consultations, quotes, subsidy calculations, policy reviews and claims help are 100% free. Carriers pay our commission directly, so nothing is added to your premium. You will never receive an invoice from our agency for enrollment assistance.

How fast can I get covered in Tallahassee?

ACA Marketplace coverage started today usually begins the first of the following month if you enroll before the 15th. We typically return a full comparison of 3–6 plans within 4 business hours, and many clients are fully enrolled within 48 hours. Life insurance approvals range from same-day to 3 weeks.

What if I have a pre-existing condition like diabetes or high blood pressure?

ACA Marketplace plans cannot deny you or charge more because of a pre-existing condition — that protection is guaranteed under federal law. For life insurance, we place many clients with diabetes, hypertension or a past cancer history using simplified-issue carriers, and guaranteed-issue final expense coverage requires no health questions at ages 50–85.

What life insurance options fit self-employed clients with no employer benefits?

Most choose 20- or 30-year term coverage: a healthy 30-year-old can often get $250,000 of 20-year term for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 for roughly $58–$75 monthly. Business owners with extra cash flow sometimes add Indexed Universal Life or a MYGA annuity for tax-advantaged accumulation.

Do you help with Medicare in Tallahassee, and what does it cost?

Yes. Our agents are AHIP and CMS-certified annually and compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap and Part D drug plans from Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Florida Blue and Simply Healthcare. Many Advantage plans carry a $0 monthly premium; Medigap Plan G in Florida typically runs $115–$210 monthly by age. Our help is always free.

What happens if I need to file a claim or my beneficiary needs help?

Call us before you call the carrier. We handle claims advocacy: we open the claim, gather medical records and paperwork, follow up with the carrier, and keep your family updated. Life insurance death benefits are typically paid to beneficiaries within 24–48 hours of a completed claim.

I'm leaving a job and losing employer coverage. How do I switch without a gap?

Losing employer coverage opens a 60-day Special Enrollment Period for Marketplace plans. Send us your termination date and we'll line up an effective date that starts the day after your group plan ends — no coverage gap. We also compare COBRA against a subsidized Marketplace plan, which is frequently thousands of dollars cheaper per year.
