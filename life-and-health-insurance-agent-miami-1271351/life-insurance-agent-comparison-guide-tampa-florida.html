How much does life insurance cost in Tampa, Florida in 2026?

It depends on your age, health and term length. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Tampa resident typically pays $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage, while a healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies run $32–$95 per month. Because we quote 8 life carriers simultaneously, you see the lowest available premium at your health class.

Is the life insurance agent comparison guide and consultation really free?

Yes — completely. Quotes, comparisons and annual policy reviews cost you nothing, and there is no obligation to buy. Insurance carriers pay our commission directly, so you never receive an invoice from Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group. There are no hidden fees and no cancellation charges.

How long does it take to get approved and issued?

Many applicants with accelerated underwriting are approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten policies that require a paramedical exam typically take 2 to 4 weeks. Final expense and guaranteed issue policies can be issued in as little as 24 hours, and Medicare enrollments can be effective the first of the following month.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify?

Not always. Applicants under 60 in good health often qualify for no-exam accelerated underwriting using prescription history and a phone interview. Final expense coverage from ages 45–85 has no-medical-exam options, and Guaranteed Issue policies for ages 50–85 require no health questions at all — though waiting periods may apply.

What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents one insurance company and can only offer that company's rates. An independent agent like ours is appointed with 18+ carriers — Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Mutual of Omaha, Americo, Transamerica, Foresters and more — so we compare underwriting rules and pricing across the market to find your best approval.

Can you help me review a policy I already own?

Absolutely, and it is one of our most requested services. We will review any existing life, Medicare or health policy — even one purchased from another agency — and show you whether the premium, coverage amount, living benefits or cash value structure still makes sense in 2026. If your current policy is better, we will tell you so.

What happens if the insurance company denies my application?

We do not stop at a denial. Because we work with multiple carriers, we re-shop your case with companies known for more lenient underwriting on your specific condition — diabetes, sleep apnea, prior cancer or heart history. Many clients we place were declined elsewhere before contacting us.

Do you offer bilingual service in Tampa?

Yes. Our 8 licensed agents and 3 support staff provide full service in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole by phone, video and in person. Every bilingual Medicare workshop we host is free to attend, and all policy documents can be explained line by line in your preferred language.

How does claims support work if something happens to me?

Your beneficiaries call our office, not an 800 number. We contact the carrier, order the required forms, submit the death certificate and track the claim until funds are released. Because carriers pay the agency a service fee, this advocacy costs your family nothing.
