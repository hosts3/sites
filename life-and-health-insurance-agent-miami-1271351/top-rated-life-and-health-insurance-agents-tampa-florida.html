Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Tampa, Florida

Straight answers on pricing, timelines, guarantees and credentials — the questions our agents hear most from Tampa Bay clients this year.

How much does life insurance cost in Tampa, Florida in 2026?

Most healthy Tampa applicants pay between $18 and $75 per month for term coverage. A healthy 30-year-old can qualify for $250,000 of 20-year term from roughly $18–$24 per month, while a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 monthly. Final expense policies run $32–$95 per month depending on age and death benefit.

Do you charge a fee for insurance quotes or consultations?

Never. Every consultation, quote and annual policy review is free with no obligation. Our agents are paid by the insurance carriers, not by clients — so you pay the same premium you would buying direct, but with an advocate in your corner.

Can I get Medicare help in Tampa if I am turning 65 this year?

Yes. Our AHIP-certified agents compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and Simply Healthcare. Many Tampa clients qualify for $0-premium Medicare Advantage plans. Enrollment help costs nothing.

How quickly will I get my quote?

We guarantee a response within 24 hours on every request, and most Tampa clients receive their first carrier comparison within 45 minutes during business hours (8:30 AM–6:30 PM EST). Life applications are frequently submitted the same day you approve them.

Do you offer no-medical-exam life insurance?

Yes. Guaranteed issue and simplified issue policies are available for ages 45–85 with no health questions and no exam, covering $5,000 to $25,000. They are ideal for final expense and burial costs, and approval is often instant or within 48 hours.

Can you help me qualify for ACA subsidies in Tampa?

Absolutely. Most Florida households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies on Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health or Florida Blue marketplace plans. Subsidy calculations and Florida KidCare enrollment help are always $0 cost to you.

What happens if my beneficiary's claim is denied?

We provide claims advocacy at no charge. Our agency calls the carrier directly, files the paperwork and follows up on your beneficiary's behalf until the claim is resolved. Most online-only agencies will not do this — it is one of the biggest reasons families choose a local agent.

Are you a captive agent tied to one insurance company?

No. We are an independent agency appointed with 15+ carriers, including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide, Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare and Humana. We shop the market rather than push a single brand.

Do you help small businesses in Tampa with employee benefits?

Yes. We build small group benefit packages for businesses with 5–50 employees — medical, dental, vision, group life and disability income coverage. Group quotes are free and typically delivered within two business days, including employee enrollment support.

What if I already own a life insurance policy?

We will review it free of charge, even if you bought it from another agency years ago. Our agents check your current rate, death benefit, riders and beneficiary designations, then tell you honestly whether to keep it, adjust it or replace it. No pressure and no obligation to switch.

What credentials and licenses do your agents hold?

Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) or 2-40 (Health Insurance Only) — completes annual continuing education, and maintains AHIP Medicare plus CMS Fraud, Waste & Abuse certification. We have been A+ BBB accredited since 2015.

Do you offer bilingual or evening appointments?

Yes. We provide phone support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole, and offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15–Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1–Jan 15). Call +1-540-352-6249 to schedule.
