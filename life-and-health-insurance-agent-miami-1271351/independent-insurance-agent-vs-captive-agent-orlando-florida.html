What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent in Orlando, Florida?

A captive agent is employed by a single insurance company and may only sell that one carrier's products — if their company's rate is high or their underwriting declines you, there is nowhere else to go. An independent agent like Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group is appointed with 18+ carriers including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Mutual of Omaha and Transamerica. That means we can compare rates side by side, place you with the carrier most likely to approve your health history, and re-shop your coverage as you age. Our loyalty is to you, not to one insurer's quota.

Does it cost anything to work with an independent insurance agent in Orlando?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Our commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you, and the premium you pay is the same whether you enroll directly with the carrier or through our agency. There are no application fees, no service charges and no hidden costs — we will put that in writing before you apply.

How much does term life insurance cost in Orlando, Florida in 2026?

Based on 2026 Central Florida averages, a healthy 30-year-old can typically secure $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for approximately $18–$24 per month. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 generally ranges from $32 to $95 monthly, depending on age and the death benefit you choose ($5,000–$25,000).

How quickly can I get approved for life insurance in Orlando?

Timelines depend on the product. Accelerated and simplified-issue policies with no medical exam are frequently approved in 24–72 hours. Guaranteed issue final expense coverage for ages 50–85 can be issued in as little as one business day because there are no health questions. Fully underwritten term and permanent policies — which require labs and medical records — usually take 2–4 weeks. We track your case daily and update you at every stage.
