Nothing. Consultations, quotes, annual policy reviews and claims help are 100% free, and there is no obligation to buy. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier after a policy issues — never by the client — so the premium is the same whether you buy directly from the carrier or through us.

How fast can I get a life insurance quote in Tampa?

Most Tampa clients get side-by-side quotes from 5–8 carriers within 45 minutes during business hours (Mon–Fri 8:30 AM–6:30 PM, Sat 9 AM–2 PM). Outside those hours, we back everything with a 24-hour response guarantee on every quote request and question.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. Accelerated underwriting allows many healthy applicants up to $1,000,000 to be approved with no exam — just a health questionnaire and database check. Simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue policies from ages 50–85 require no exam and, in most guaranteed-issue cases, no health questions at all. Final expense approvals typically come back in 24–72 hours.

How do I know if an insurance agent is legitimately licensed in Florida?

Verify any agent through the Florida Department of Financial Services license search. Our agency holds Florida License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health), carries A+ BBB accreditation since 2015, and is a member of NABIP. We will send our license numbers in writing before you sign anything.

What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents a single insurance company, so you see one price and one underwriting decision. An independent agent like Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group holds appointments with 18+ carriers — including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Transamerica, Americo and John Hancock — so we can shop rates and find a second or third path to approval if one carrier declines.

How much life insurance do I actually need in Tampa?

A practical benchmark is 10–15 times your annual income, plus the remaining mortgage balance and any college or elder-care obligations. For a Tampa household earning $70,000 with a $350,000 mortgage and two children, that often lands between $750,000 and $1,250,000. We calculate this with you line by line rather than guessing.

Do you help with Medicare in Tampa, and which plans do you offer?

Yes. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant for Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) Plans G and N, and standalone Part D drug plans. We represent Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and Doctor's Health Plan, and we review your plan every fall during Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7).

What if I already have a policy — can you still help?

Absolutely, and this is one of our most popular services. Our free, no-obligation policy review covers policies you did not purchase through us. We check the rate, riders, cash value, carrier rating and beneficiary wording, then tell you in writing whether you're overpaying or properly covered. Most reviews are returned within one business day.

Will my premiums increase over time?

With level term life insurance, your premium is locked for the entire term — 10, 15, 20 or 30 years — and cannot increase because of age or health changes. Whole life and IUL premiums are also fixed for life. Final expense policies are typically fixed as well. Only annual renewable term or yearly-renewable products increase, and we don't recommend those unless there's a specific reason.

Do you charge extra for claims support?

No. Claims advocacy is included at no cost. When a claim arises, our team contacts the carrier, orders the death certificate requirements, submits the forms and follows up on your behalf until funds are released. We also track the claim timeline and keep your beneficiary updated at every step.

Can you help self-employed and gig-economy workers in Tampa?

That's a core part of our practice. 1099 contractors, rideshare and delivery drivers, salon and restaurant workers, and construction crews often have no employer life, health or disability benefits. We build individual life, disability income and ACA Marketplace health coverage that functions like a self-funded benefits package — and ACA subsidy help is always free.

What happens during the first appointment, and will I be pressured?

The first appointment is education, not sales. We explain how underwriting, premiums and beneficiary rules work, answer your questions, and review your existing coverage. No client is ever asked to commit during the first appointment — you'll receive written quotes and decide on your own timeline. Appointments run 15–25 minutes by phone or video.
