How We Ranked These Benefits

Scoring scale: 5.0 = transformative financial impact · 4.5–4.9 = significant, repeatable savings · 4.0–4.4 = meaningful convenience and protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are the benefits of top rated life and health insurance agents really worth it if I can buy online?

Online platforms are efficient for simple, healthy-applicant term life policies. They are far weaker for Medicare plan selection, ACA subsidy maximization, underwriting-sensitive health conditions, small group benefits, and claims advocacy. The strongest single advantage is that an independent agent can compare up to 18 carriers — the exact same policy usually costs the same either way, but you gain expert matching and free lifetime service.

2. How much does it cost to work with Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, subsidy calculations, policy reviews, and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Commissions are paid by the insurance carrier. There is no fee, no retainer, and no pressure to commit during a first appointment.

3. What does term life insurance actually cost in Miami?

A healthy 30-year-old can often get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can typically get $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally run $32–$95 monthly depending on age and the coverage amount selected.

4. Can I get Medicare help if I am not yet 65 but retiring early?

Yes. Early retirees generally bridge to Medicare with an ACA Marketplace plan and premium tax credits, which is often far less expensive than COBRA. The agency also helps with Part B enrollment timing, IRMAA questions, and the transition from Marketplace coverage to Medicare at 65.

5. What if my health history has made life insurance difficult to get?

Guaranteed Issue life insurance is available for ages 50–85 with no health questions, and final expense plans often require no medical exam for applicants 45–85. For controlled conditions, agents route the application to the carrier with the most favorable underwriting guidelines for that specific diagnosis.

7. Do I have to be a client for a free policy review?

No. The free no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies you already own, even if they were purchased elsewhere. The agency will compare your current coverage, confirm beneficiaries are current, and if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, they will match it or explain the difference in writing.

8. Is service available outside Miami-Dade County?

Yes. Health and Medicare appointments are handled in person or by video across Broward County (Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Fort Lauderdale) and Monroe County (Key Largo, Islamorada). Life insurance is licensed statewide across all of Florida, and phone support is available in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole.
