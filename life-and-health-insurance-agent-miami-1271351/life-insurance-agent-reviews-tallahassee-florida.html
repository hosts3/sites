How much does life insurance cost in Tallahassee, Florida?

For a healthy 30-year-old, $250,000 of 20-year term coverage typically runs about $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58 to $75 per month. Final expense coverage for ages 45 to 85 generally runs $32 to $95 per month depending on age and how much coverage you choose. Pricing is set by the carrier based on age, health and tobacco use — not by your ZIP code.

Do you charge a fee for quotes or consultations?

Never. Every consultation, quote and annual policy review is 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are paid by the insurance carriers — not by you — so your premium is exactly the same as if you applied directly. That means our advice is genuinely independent and you can walk away at any point without owing anything.

How fast can I get approved for a policy?

Fully underwritten term and whole life policies usually issue in 2 to 4 weeks, depending on how quickly medical records arrive. Guaranteed issue and final expense products with no medical exam can be approved and issued in as little as 24 to 72 hours. Many Tallahassee clients have coverage in force the same week they call us.

How quickly will you respond to my call or quote request?

What makes an independent insurance agent different from buying direct?

A captive agent or online website can only show you their own products. As an independent insurance agency we are appointed with multiple carriers, including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare and Humana. You get one application conversation and several competing offers — which is how most clients save $300 to $700 per year on identical coverage.

Can I get life insurance in Florida if I have health problems?

Very often, yes. We work with carriers that accept controlled diabetes, high blood pressure, elevated cholesterol, sleep apnea and even a history of cancer in remission. For harder cases, guaranteed issue whole life is available to ages 50 to 85 with no health questions and no exam, with graded benefits in the first two years. We will tell you upfront which category you realistically qualify for.

What is your rate-shopping guarantee?
