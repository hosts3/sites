Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Miami

These are the questions our Miami agents hear most often. If yours is not here, call +1-540-352-6249 — we answer 24/7, and there is never a charge for advice.

How much does term life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?

Rates depend on age, health, tobacco use and the amount of coverage. As a current reference: a healthy 30-year-old Miami resident can often secure $250,000 of 20-year term life insurance for approximately $18 to $24 per month, while a healthy 45-year-old typically pays $58 to $75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term coverage. We compare eight life carriers at once, so you see the real range rather than a single company's price.

Are your consultations and quotes really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation. We charge no consultation fees, no policy-review fees and no markup on your premiums. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carriers. You can walk away from any appointment without buying anything, and we will still send you the written comparison for your records.

Do you offer services in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Sí, of course. Our Little Havana office provides full service in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole by phone, in person and by video appointment. Three of our eleven team members are dedicated bilingual support staff, and our free Medicare workshop on the first Wednesday of each month is presented in both Spanish and English.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Often, yes. We offer simplified-issue plans with a few health questions answered by phone, and guaranteed-issue life insurance for ages 50 to 85 with no health questions and no exam at all. Guaranteed-issue policies typically have a two-year graded benefit period, and we always explain that clearly before you enroll so there are no surprises for your family.

Do you help with Medicare enrollment in Miami-Dade?

Absolutely. We are AHIP certified annually and CMS compliant, and we compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and more. We check your doctors and medications against each plan's formulary before recommending it, and we never charge for the appointment.

How quickly will I get a quote or a callback?

We guarantee a response within 24 hours on every request, and most quote requests submitted before 4:00 PM EST receive a same-day callback — often within 45 minutes during business hours. Our offices are open Monday through Friday 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM by appointment, with evening video appointments until 8:00 PM Monday through Thursday during enrollment season.

What happens if my doctor or prescriptions are not covered?

That is exactly why we run a provider and prescription check before you enroll rather than after. If your medications or physicians are not on a plan's formulary or network, we simply do not recommend that plan. If your health needs change mid-year, we will re-shop your coverage during the next valid enrollment window at no cost and walk you through switching.

Do you handle insurance claims for beneficiaries?

Yes. Our claims advocacy service is included with every policy we place. When a claim arises, our staff contact the carrier, submit the required documentation and follow up until the benefit is paid — so your family is not left navigating a call center during an already difficult time. We also help update beneficiary designations any time your family situation changes.

What health insurance options do self-employed Miami workers have?

Self-employed workers, 1099 contractors, gig-economy drivers and restaurant or salon employees without employer coverage can enroll in ACA Marketplace plans during Open Enrollment (November 1 to January 15) or with a Special Enrollment Period after a qualifying life event. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies are applied.

Do you write policies outside of Miami?

Yes. Life insurance is licensed statewide in Florida, so we regularly help clients in Broward County (Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Fort Lauderdale), Monroe County (Key Largo, Islamorada) and elsewhere in the state by video. In-person health and Medicare appointments are available throughout Miami-Dade, or by video anywhere in Florida.

What is included in the free policy review?

We examine your death benefit, premium structure, cash value performance, riders, carrier financial ratings and beneficiary designations — including policies purchased from another agency or directly from a carrier. You receive a written summary of gaps, overlaps and potential savings, plus a clear recommendation. There is no cost and no obligation to change anything.

Can you help small businesses in Miami with employee benefits?

Yes. We design and administer small group benefits for teams of 5 to 50 employees, including medical, dental, life, short-term disability and voluntary products.
