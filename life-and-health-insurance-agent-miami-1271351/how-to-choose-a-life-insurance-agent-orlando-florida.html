Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Orlando, Florida

Updated for 2026 — the questions Orlando clients ask us most often, answered honestly.

How much does it cost to work with an insurance agent in Orlando?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you, so using an agent costs the same as buying direct — and you get comparison shopping across 18+ carriers plus ongoing claims support.

How do I choose a life insurance agent in Orlando, Florida?

Check four things: (1) an active Florida Department of Financial Services license, (2) independence from a single carrier so they can shop rates, (3) written response-time and policy-review promises, and (4) real reviews from Florida clients. Ask about claims advocacy — that is where agents earn their keep.

How quickly can I get covered?

Simplified-issue and final expense policies can be approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term life typically takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on exam scheduling and medical record retrieval. We follow your file daily and respond to every client question within 24 hours, guaranteed.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify for life insurance?

Not always. Many Orlando clients qualify for no-exam term life up to $1,000,000 based on health questionnaires and prescription history. Guaranteed issue final expense coverage for ages 50–85 requires no health questions at all, though benefit amounts are lower (typically $5,000–$25,000).

What does a free policy review include?

We review any life or health policy — including ones you did not buy through us — and check your death benefit, premium, cash value, riders, and beneficiary designations. You get a written summary and a side-by-side comparison showing whether a competing carrier would cost less for identical coverage.

Are you licensed to sell insurance in Orlando and across Florida?

Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). We are licensed statewide, so life insurance can be written for Orlando residents, and we are AHIP Medicare and CMS-compliant for health and Medicare enrollments.

How much life insurance do I actually need?

A common starting point is 10 to 15 times your annual income, plus your mortgage balance and final expenses, minus existing coverage and savings. An Orlando household earning $70,000 with a $250,000 mortgage often lands between $750,000 and $1,000,000 of term coverage.

Do you help with Medicare and ACA enrollment in Orlando?

Yes. We enroll Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement (Medigap), and Part D drug plans annually during Annual Enrollment, and we help with ACA Marketplace plans and subsidy calculations during Open Enrollment (November 1 – January 15). Many Central Florida households pay under $50 a month after credits.

What if I find a lower rate somewhere else?

Our rate-shopping guarantee applies: if you find a lower premium for identical coverage, we will match it or explain in writing exactly why the difference exists. We would rather keep an honest client for 14 years than win a single policy with a hidden catch.

Can you help if I have a health condition like diabetes or high blood pressure?

Frequently, yes. We work with carriers that specialize in controlled diabetes, hypertension, and past cancer history, plus guaranteed issue products when standard underwriting is not available. We do a pre-screen before submitting so you do not get an avoidable decline on your record.

What languages do you support for Orlando clients?

Our team provides bilingual support in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole by phone, video, and in person. Documents can be explained line by line in your preferred language so nothing is signed without full understanding.

What happens if I need to file a claim?

Call +1-540-352-6249 and our agency handles it. We contact the carrier, request the forms, help your beneficiary complete them correctly, and follow the claim until the benefit is paid — typically within 10 to 30 days for clean claims.
