Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Miami

Straight answers from licensed Florida agents — updated with current 2026 rates, enrollment dates and underwriting timelines.

How much does an affordable best life insurance policy for young adults cost in Miami?

For a healthy 30-year-old in Miami-Dade, a $250,000 20-year term policy typically runs $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old buying $500,000 of 20-year term usually pays $58 to $75 monthly. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use, driving record and term length — and our agency quotes eight life carriers at once so you land in the best underwriting class you qualify for.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance in Miami?

Often, no. Many of our carriers offer accelerated underwriting with no medical exam for applicants ages 18 to 55 who answer a short health questionnaire. Approvals can come through in 24 to 72 hours instead of weeks. For ages 50 to 85, we also offer guaranteed-issue policies that ask no health questions at all, though death benefits are typically limited during the first two years.

How long does it take to get approved and issued?

No-exam accelerated policies are commonly issued in 1 to 3 business days. Fully underwritten policies requiring labs and medical records generally take 3 to 6 weeks. We contact the carrier every 48 hours on your behalf and update you by text, email or phone — you never have to chase the insurance company yourself.

Is your consultation really free, and how do you get paid?

Yes — every quote, consultation and annual policy review is 100% free with no obligation. Agent commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier, not by you, so working with our agency costs you nothing extra. No client is ever asked to commit during a first appointment, which is part of why we hold a 4.9-star rating.

Can you review a life insurance policy I already own?

Absolutely. Our free no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies you did not buy through us. We verify death benefit, premium structure, cash value performance, riders and beneficiary designations — then tell you honestly whether to keep it, convert it, or shop for better value. Roughly 1 in 4 reviews we complete uncovers a beneficiary error.

Yes, and the help is $0-cost. We enroll clients in ACA Marketplace plans every year and calculate premium tax credits based on household income and size. Most Miami-Dade households we serve qualify for financial assistance, and many end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies. We also handle Florida KidCare enrollment for children at no charge.

How much coverage should a young adult in Miami actually buy?

Most working young adults in Miami should start with 10 to 15 times annual income — commonly $250,000 to $500,000 — then add for a mortgage, student loans and future childcare. A 20-year or 30-year level term policy delivers the most protection per dollar. If your budget is tight, we will show you what $20 or $30 a month realistically buys before you commit to anything.
