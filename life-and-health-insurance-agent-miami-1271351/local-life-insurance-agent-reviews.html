Fully underwritten policies usually issue in 2–4 weeks. Accelerated-underwriting policies with no medical exam can be approved in as little as 24–72 hours. In most cases, yes. We work with carriers that accept controlled diabetes, high blood pressure, sleep apnea, and even a past cardiac event — often at standard or slightly rated pricing. For ages 50–85 who prefer not to answer medical questions, we offer guaranteed issue whole life policies with no exam and no health questionnaire.
