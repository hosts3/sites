How much does term life insurance cost in Jacksonville, Florida?

Rates depend on age, health and coverage amount. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Jacksonville resident can often get $250,000 of 20-year term for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 monthly. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 typically runs $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of benefit. Every quote we provide is free and there is no obligation.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Many final expense and simplified-issue policies are approved the same day, and fully underwritten term policies usually take 2 to 4 weeks. If you complete a no-exam application and your health history is straightforward, we frequently see policies issued in 5 to 10 business days. We track underwriting daily and notify you the moment a decision is released.

Do you charge a fee for your services as an insurance agent?

Never. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free, and our commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier — not by you. You pay the same premium whether you buy through our agency or directly from the insurer. That means independent advice costs you nothing extra.

What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents one carrier, so they can only offer that company's products. An independent insurance agency like ours is appointed with 20+ carriers, including Mutual of Omaha, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, Florida Blue, Humana and UnitedHealthcare. That lets us match coverage to your health profile and budget instead of forcing you into a single company's rate table.

Can I get life insurance in Jacksonville without a medical exam?

Yes. We offer no-exam term life, guaranteed issue whole life for ages 50–85 with no health questions, and simplified-issue final expense plans. These options cost slightly more per thousand dollars of coverage but are ideal if you have diabetes, high blood pressure, a prior cancer diagnosis, or simply want to avoid labs and a paramedical exam.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap plans in Jacksonville?

Absolutely. Our agents are AHIP-certified annually and CMS-compliant in Medicare marketing and enrollment. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plans across Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Cigna, Wellcare and more. Many Part C plans in Duval County have a $0 monthly premium, and Plan G Medigap typically runs $115–$210 per month.

Can you help me qualify for ACA subsidies or Florida KidCare?

Yes, and there is no cost for that help. Most Jacksonville households qualify for premium tax credits on ACA Marketplace plans, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies. We also assist with Florida KidCare applications for children at no charge, including documentation review and follow-up with the state.

Is life insurance available statewide if I move outside Jacksonville?

Yes. Our agency is licensed to write life insurance in all 50 states and is licensed in Florida for both life and health (License 2-15 and 2-40). If you move from Jacksonville to another part of Florida or out of state, your policy travels with you — and we can continue servicing it by phone or video, including beneficiary updates and annual reviews.

What happens if I need to file a claim or my beneficiary needs help?

Claims advocacy is included at no charge. Our office contacts the carrier on your behalf, submits the required forms and death certificate, and follows the claim until funds are disbursed. In recent Jacksonville cases, benefits have been paid in as few as 11 days. Your beneficiary never has to negotiate with an insurance company alone.

Are you licensed, insured and background-checked?

Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40, and every agent on our team holds an active Florida license with annual continuing education. We are A+ BBB accredited since 2015, members of NABIP, and carry professional liability coverage.

Do you offer appointments outside normal business hours?

We do. Standard hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM EST and Saturday 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM by appointment. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15), we add evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM. Bilingual support is available in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

What if I find a lower price for identical coverage somewhere else?

Bring us the quote and we will either match it or explain, line by line, why the coverage is not actually identical — waiting periods, riders, cash value, conversion privileges and carrier financial ratings all affect real value. Our rate-shopping guarantee means you will never wonder whether you overpaid.
