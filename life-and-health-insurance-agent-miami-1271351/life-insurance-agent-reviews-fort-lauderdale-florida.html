How much does term life insurance cost in Fort Lauderdale, Florida?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old non-smoker typically pays $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term, and a healthy 45-year-old pays about $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 runs roughly $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. Your exact rate depends on age, health history and tobacco use.

Is working with an insurance agent free, or do I pay a fee?

It is completely free. Consultations, quotes, comparisons and annual policy reviews cost you nothing, and commissions are paid by the insurance carrier — not the client. You pay the same premium whether you buy through us or contact the carrier directly. There are no hidden fees and no obligation to purchase.

How fast can I get approved for life insurance in Fort Lauderdale?

Many clients are approved in 24–72 hours with no-exam accelerated underwriting. Fully underwritten policies requiring bloodwork and medical records typically take 3 to 10 business days. Our average quote turnaround is about 90 minutes during business hours, and we back it with a 24-hour response guarantee on all client questions.

Do you offer life insurance with no medical exam?

Yes. We have no-exam term options for qualified applicants up to $1,000,000, plus guaranteed-issue whole life for ages 50–85 with no health questions at all. Guaranteed-issue plans have a graded benefit period (usually two years), so we explain that trade-off clearly before you enroll.

What insurance carriers do you represent in Fort Lauderdale?

We are independent and appointed with 18+ carriers, including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health, Doctors' Health Plan and Simply Healthcare for health and Medicare, plus Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life for life coverage.

Does it cost more to use an independent agent than buying direct?

No — carrier pricing for a given policy is filed with the state and identical whether you buy direct or through an appointed agent. Using an independent agent simply means one person compares multiple carriers for you. We also offer a rate-match guarantee: find a lower rate for identical coverage and we'll match it or explain the difference.

Do you help with Medicare in Fort Lauderdale, and when should I enroll?
