Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Port St. Lucie, Florida

Real questions from self-employed Treasure Coast residents, answered in plain language.

How much does health insurance cost for a self-employed person in Port St. Lucie, Florida?

It depends almost entirely on your income and household size, because ACA premium tax credits are income-based. Most self-employed Port St. Lucie households we enroll end up paying between $0 and $150 per month after subsidies, and many pay under $50. The full price of a silver plan before subsidies in St. Lucie County typically runs $450–$700 per month for an individual, so the subsidy is usually the single largest savings opportunity available to you.

Do I need to be employed by a company to qualify for ACA Marketplace coverage?

No — and this is the most common misconception we correct. Self-employed workers, 1099 contractors, gig-economy drivers, freelancers and small business owners are all eligible to enroll in ACA Marketplace plans during Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15) or within 60 days of a qualifying life event such as losing employer coverage, moving, marriage or a new baby.

Are your consultations and quotes really free?

Yes, 100% free with no obligation and no cost to you. Insurance carriers pay our commission, not our clients. That means our advice costs you nothing whether you enroll through us or decide to think about it for six months. We never ask a client to commit during the first appointment.

How quickly can I get coverage after I call?

We respond to quote requests within 24 hours — usually under 2 hours during business hours. ACA and Medicare Advantage enrollments generally take effect the first day of the following month. Medicare Supplements can be effective as soon as your application is approved. Life insurance depends on underwriting: fully no-exam policies can issue in 24–72 hours, while policies requiring a medical exam typically take 2–4 weeks.

Will you help me if my income changes mid-year and my subsidy is wrong?

Absolutely, and it matters. Subsidies are based on projected annual income, so a strong quarter in your business can create a repayment surprise at tax time. We help you update your income estimate with the Marketplace, recalculate your credit, and switch plans if needed — free of charge, every year, for as long as you're our client.

Do you offer Medicare help for people turning 65 in Port St. Lucie?

Yes. We are AHIP Medicare-certified annually and CMS-compliant in our marketing and enrollment. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans and Medicare Supplement (Medigap) options, enroll your Part D prescription drug plan, and verify your doctors and pharmacies are in-network before you sign. Many Medicare Advantage plans in St. Lucie County have a $0 monthly premium; Plan G supplements typically run $115–$210 per month depending on age.

How does this compare to buying a plan online myself?

You can absolutely buy online — but you'll be comparing prices without comparing provider networks, formulary coverage, or whether a plan's deductible structure penalizes the prescriptions you take. Independent agents see all carriers in one system, are paid the same commission regardless of which plan you choose, and know which St. Lucie County hospitals and physician groups contract with each network. That's the value difference.

Both. We place term life, whole life, indexed universal life, final expense, guaranteed issue, mortgage protection and annuities through Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life. Can you help my employees if I run a small business in Port St. Lucie?
