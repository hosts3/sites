How much does life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?

A healthy 30-year-old in Miami typically gets a $250,000, 20-year term policy for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old about $58–$75 per month for $500,000. Final expense coverage usually runs $32–$95 monthly. Final rates depend on age, health history, tobacco use, and the coverage amount you select — and we quote multiple carriers at once so you see the real spread.

Is your health insurance agent service really free?

Yes — consultations, quotes, subsidy calculations, and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, never by you. Your premium is identical whether you enroll through Biscayne Bay or directly with the company, so working with us costs you nothing extra.

How long does term life insurance approval take?

Fully underwritten policies usually issue in 3–6 weeks. Accelerated-underwriting products can finish in 24–72 hours for qualified applicants, and guaranteed issue policies for ages 50–85 are often approved the same day with no medical exam. We track every application and update you weekly until the policy is delivered.

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a network-based plan, often with a $0 monthly premium plus dental, vision, and OTC allowances. Medigap supplements Original Medicare instead, lets you use any Medicare-accepting provider nationwide, and Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month in Miami-Dade. We compare both side by side using your actual doctors and prescriptions.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. We offer simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue life insurance with no medical exam and, in some cases, no health questions at all for ages 50–85. Coverage generally ranges from $5,000 to $25,000 for final expense products, and many applicants are approved the same day they apply.

Do you help with ACA Marketplace subsidies?

Absolutely. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies are applied. We calculate your credit at no charge, compare Ambetter, Florida Blue, Molina, Oscar, and Doctor's Health Plan networks, and verify that your doctors and medications are covered before enrollment.

I'm self-employed with no employer benefits. Can you help me?

That is our specialty. We work with gig drivers, 1099 contractors, restaurant and hospitality staff, salon professionals, and construction workers across Miami-Dade. You can enroll in an ACA plan with subsidy help, add disability income insurance to protect your income, or — if you employ 5–50 people — set up small group benefits.

What happens if my insurance claim is denied?

We advocate for you at no cost. Our agency files carrier paperwork, calls the claims department directly, and assists beneficiaries through the full appeal process — including documentation, follow-ups, and escalation when needed. Our no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review examines your death benefit, cash value, beneficiary designations, riders, and current rate — including policies purchased elsewhere.
