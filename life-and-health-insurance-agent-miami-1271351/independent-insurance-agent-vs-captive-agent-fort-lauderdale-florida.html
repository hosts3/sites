Independent Insurance Agent vs Captive Agent — What Fort Lauderdale Clients Need to Know

The independent insurance agent vs captive agent Fort Lauderdale Florida comparison comes down to one question: who does your agent actually work for? A captive agent is employed by a single insurance company and can only present that company's rates. An independent agent is contracted with many carriers and is paid by whichever one you choose — which means the recommendation can finally be about you.

Comparison Point Captive Agent Independent Agent (Biscayne Bay)
Carriers available One company only 18+ A-rated carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock and Transamerica
Rate shopping You get a single quote — no comparison Side-by-side quotes from every carrier that fits your health profile
Underwriting outcomes Declined means you start over elsewhere We pre-screen your health history and route you to the carrier most likely to approve at the best rate class
Who pays the agent One carrier's payroll The carrier you select — your premium is identical either way
Annual policy review Rarely offered outside the captive product line Free every year, even for policies we did not sell
Claims assistance You call the carrier's 800 number We call the carrier, file the paperwork and follow the claim for your beneficiary

What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent in Fort Lauderdale, Florida?

A captive agent is employed by one insurance company and can only sell that company's products. An independent agent like Biscayne Bay is appointed with 18+ carriers, so we compare rates and underwriting guidelines side by side and recommend the plan that actually fits your health history and budget.

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in Fort Lauderdale can qualify for $250,000 of 20-year term life coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old typically pays $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense coverage ranges from about $32 to $95 per month.

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carriers, never by the client — so your premium is the same whether you work with us or buy the same policy direct.
