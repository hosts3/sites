How much does life insurance cost in Port St. Lucie, Florida in 2026?

Rates depend on age, health class, term length, and coverage amount. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally run $32–$95 per month. We quote at least three carriers side by side so you can see the real spread before deciding.

How fast can I get approved and covered?

Accelerated-underwriting products can issue in as little as 24 to 72 hours for healthy applicants. Fully underwritten policies with labs and medical records typically take 2 to 4 weeks. Final expense and guaranteed-issue plans usually issue within 7 to 10 business days. Your agent tracks underwriting daily and calls you the moment there's an update.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify?

Not always. Several carriers we represent offer no-exam term life up to $500,000 for qualified applicants, and guaranteed-issue final expense coverage for ages 50–85 requires no health questions at all. If a medical exam is required, the carrier pays for it and a licensed examiner can come to your Port St. Lucie home or office.

What is final expense insurance, and who qualifies?

Final expense — also called burial insurance — is a small whole life policy, usually $5,000 to $25,000, designed to cover funeral costs, medical bills, and small debts. It is available to ages 45–85 with simplified or guaranteed issue underwriting, so conditions like diabetes, COPD, or a prior heart event often do not disqualify you. Premiums never increase and the policy never expires.
