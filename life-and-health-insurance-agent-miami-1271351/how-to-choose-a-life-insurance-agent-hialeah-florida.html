Frequently Asked Questions About Life & Health Insurance Agents in Hialeah, Florida

These are the questions we hear most often from Hialeah, Miami Lakes and Little Havana residents — answered without sales talk.

How do I choose a life insurance agent in Hialeah, Florida?

Look for four things: an active Florida license (2-15 or 2-40) you can verify on the DFS website, appointments with multiple carriers instead of one, a written quote with real monthly numbers, and a free policy review with no obligation. Independent agents who represent 8+ carriers can shop your health history instead of rejecting you on one company's rules.

How much does life insurance cost in Hialeah, Florida?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Hialeah resident typically pays $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage, and a healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 run about $32–$95 monthly. Your actual rate depends on age, health, tobacco use and coverage amount.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify for life insurance?

Not always. Term policies above $250,000 usually require a free paramedical exam at your home or office. Policies under $250,000 are often approved with health questions only, and our guaranteed-issue final expense plans for ages 50–85 require no health questions and no exam at all — approval is based on age and coverage amount.

How long does it take to get approved and issued?

Simplified-issue and final expense policies are frequently approved the same day, with coverage active in 24–48 hours. Fully underwritten term life typically takes 3–10 business days in Florida, assuming no additional medical records are requested. We track your application with the carrier and update you at every stage.

Are your consultations and quotes really free?

Yes — 100% free, with no fees and no obligation. We are compensated by the insurance carrier when a policy is issued, not by you. That means quotes, policy reviews, subsidy calculations and annual check-ins cost Hialeah clients nothing, whether or not you ever purchase coverage through our agency.

Can you help me with Medicare when I turn 65?

Absolutely. Our agents are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant annually. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and others. Many Advantage plans are $0 monthly premium in Miami-Dade, and Medigap Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month.

What if I've been declined for life insurance before?

Being declined with one carrier does not mean you are uninsurable. Carriers underwrite conditions like sleep apnea, diabetes, high blood pressure and past cancer very differently. We routinely re-shop declined clients across 8 life carriers and frequently place coverage at standard or slightly rated rates — or move you to a guaranteed-issue product.

Do you have Spanish and Haitian Creole speaking agents?

Sí — and wi. Our team of 8 licensed agents is supported by 3 bilingual staff members, and we conduct consultations in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. Our main office in Little Havana (2740 SW 8th Street, Suite 210) hosts a free bilingual Medicare workshop on the first Wednesday of every month.

What is your 24-hour response guarantee?

Every quote request, question and service call receives a response within 24 hours — most within 2 business hours. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15–Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1–Jan 15) we extend evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM EST, and our phone line stays available 24/7.

Will you review a policy I already own?

Yes, and it is completely free — even if you bought it from another agent or company. We check your death benefit against current 2026 rates, verify your beneficiary designations are correct, and look for gaps like missing living benefits or an outdated term. Many Hialeah clients discover they are overpaying or underinsured.

How do ACA subsidies work for Hialeah families?

Subsidies are based on household income and family size, not assets or credit. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our Hialeah clients pay under $50 per month after the credit is applied. We calculate your estimated subsidy for free and help enroll — including Florida KidCare for children.

Why use an independent agency instead of buying online or from a captive agent?

Online quoting tools show you one company's rate. A captive agent can only sell one brand. As an independent agency with 12+ carrier appointments and 14+ years in South Florida, we compare underwriting guidelines, riders and renewal rates across companies — and we advocate for you with the carrier if a claim is ever filed.
