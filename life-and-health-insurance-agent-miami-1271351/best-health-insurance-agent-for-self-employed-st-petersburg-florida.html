Real questions from self-employed workers, gig drivers, small business owners and retirees across Pinellas County. Still not sure? Call +1-540-352-6249.

How much does it cost to work with a health insurance agent in St. Petersburg, Florida?
Nothing — and that is not a marketing line. Our consultations, plan comparisons, enrollments and annual policy reviews are free with no obligation. Agents are compensated by the insurance carrier, so your premium is identical whether you enroll on your own or with us.
Can self-employed workers still get affordable health insurance in 2026?
Yes. Most self-employed Pinellas County households qualify for ACA Marketplace premium tax credits, because subsidies are based on projected income rather than employment status. Many of our clients end up paying under $50 per month after credits, and some qualify for a $0-premium Bronze plan.
What happens if I underestimate my income and have to repay a subsidy?
You could owe part of the credit back at tax time, so accuracy matters. During your free assessment we help you project 1099, gig-platform, seasonal and cash-flow income realistically, and we can adjust your plan or subsidy mid-year if your earnings change.
How fast can I get a quote and complete enrollment?
Most quote requests are answered within 2 business hours, and ACA Marketplace enrollments are frequently finished the same day. Accelerated-underwriting life policies can issue in 24 hours, while fully underwritten policies with a medical exam typically take 2–4 weeks.
Do you sell only health insurance, or life and Medicare too?
We are a full-service independent agency. That includes ACA Marketplace health plans, Medicare Advantage and Medigap, Part D drug plans, term and whole life, indexed universal life, final expense, disability income, fixed indexed and MYGA annuities, and small group benefits for 5–50 employees.
What is your response-time guarantee?
Every quote request, policy question and claim inquiry receives a response within 24 hours — in practice, most are answered within 2 hours during office hours. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) we also offer evening video appointments until 8:00 PM.
Can you review a policy I bought from a different agent?
Yes, and it costs you nothing. Our free Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies you did not purchase through us. We examine your rate class, coverage amount and beneficiary designations, then give you a written comparison against current 2026 carrier options — including the option to keep what you have.
Do you provide bilingual service for St. Petersburg clients?
We do. Our licensed agents and support staff serve clients in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole by phone, video or in person, so nothing about deductibles, networks or beneficiary rules gets lost in translation.
Which insurance carriers can you shop on my behalf?
Health and Medicare clients can choose from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina Healthcare, Oscar Health, Doctor's Health Plan and Simply Healthcare. Life coverage is shopped across Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life.
What credentials should I verify before hiring an insurance agent in Florida?
Confirm an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — 2-15 for life, health and annuities, or 2-40 for health only. Our founder holds both, completes annual continuing education, is AHIP Medicare certified, is a NABIP member and has been BBB A+ accredited since 2015.
Do you help with claims, or does the carrier handle everything?
Claims advocacy is included at no charge. We file the carrier paperwork, follow up with the claims examiner, supply documentation and keep beneficiaries informed until the benefit is paid — usually without the family making a single call themselves.
