Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in St. Petersburg, Florida

Updated for 2026. These are the questions St. Petersburg clients ask us most often — answered honestly and in plain English.

How much does it cost to work with a life insurance agent in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Nothing, ever. Consultations, quotes, carrier comparisons and annual policy reviews are 100% free. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier after a policy is issued — not by you — so the premium you pay is identical whether you buy direct or through us. Nobody on our team will ever ask for a fee or a deposit.

How quickly can I get a life insurance quote in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Most clients receive a written quote the same day — typically within 45 minutes during business hours, with a 24-hour response guarantee on all requests. Simple term policies frequently approve in 24 to 72 hours through accelerated underwriting, while fully underwritten permanent policies usually take two to four weeks.

What does a life insurance agent actually do for me?

We compare 8+ A-rated carriers against your age, health history and budget, explain underwriting and pricing in plain language, complete the application with you, chase medical records and requirements, and advocate for your beneficiaries at claim time. You get one point of contact instead of five different call centers.

How do I choose a life insurance agent in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Verify the agent's Florida license (2-15 or 2-40) on the state DFS portal, confirm they represent multiple carriers rather than one company, ask directly how they get paid, and request a written side-by-side quote. If an agent cannot answer those four questions clearly, keep looking.

Do you offer no-medical-exam life insurance?

Yes. Final expense and guaranteed issue policies for ages 45 to 85 often require no exam and no health questions, with coverage ranging from $5,000 to $25,000. Premiums typically run $32 to $95 per month depending on your age and the coverage amount. Many of these policies issue within the same week.

Can you help me with Medicare in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Yes. Our AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant agents compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and more. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Pinellas County carry a $0 monthly premium, and Medigap Plan G generally runs $115 to $210 per month depending on age.

I already have a life insurance policy. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Our free policy review covers existing policies — even ones we did not sell. We examine the death benefit, premium structure, cash value, riders and beneficiary designations, then tell you plainly whether keeping it makes sense or if a replacement would genuinely serve you better. There is no charge and no obligation to switch.

Do you help with ACA Marketplace subsidies in Florida?

Yes. We calculate your premium tax credit, compare Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health, Simply Healthcare and Doctor's Health Plan, and walk you through enrollment step by step. Most Florida households qualify for substantial credits, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies. Enrollment help costs nothing.

Are you licensed to write insurance in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Yes. We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only), are AHIP Medicare certified annually with Fraud, Waste & Abuse training, members of NABIP, and Better Business Bureau A+ accredited since 2015. Life insurance is licensed statewide across all of Florida.

What happens at claim time if something happens to me?

We handle it for your family. Our agency files the claim with the carrier, follows up until it is paid, answers your beneficiaries' questions and prepares the paperwork — at no charge, whether the policy was written last month or 12 years ago. That advocacy is the single biggest reason clients stay with an independent agency.

Do you charge more than buying directly from an insurance company?

No. Life insurance rates are filed with the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation, so the premium for identical coverage is the same whether you buy direct or through an agent. Working with us adds free comparison shopping, application support and claims advocacy at no additional cost to you.

Do you speak Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Yes. Our team offers bilingual phone support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. St. Petersburg clients can be served entirely by phone or secure video appointment, and we schedule evening video calls Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare Annual Enrollment (October 15 – December 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (November 1 – January 15).

What is the best time of year to review my life or health coverage in Florida?

Life insurance can be reviewed any month — rates and health changes make January through March an especially good window to lock in coverage. Health and Medicare decisions have fixed timing: Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7, and ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 to January 15. We send reminders to every client before each window opens.
