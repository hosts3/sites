Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Miami

Updated for 2026. These are the questions our Miami agents hear most often — answered honestly, with real numbers.

How much does life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?

It depends on age, health and coverage amount. A healthy 30-year-old in Miami-Dade typically pays $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage, while a healthy 45-year-old pays about $58–$75 per month for $500,000. Final expense plans run $32–$95 per month depending on age and benefit amount.

How do I choose a life insurance agent solutions that actually fit my family?

Start with three checks: is the agent independent (appointed with multiple carriers), are consultations free with no client-paid fees, and will they explain underwriting before recommending a product? Ask for a side-by-side carrier comparison in writing. If an agent can only show you one company's rates, you are talking to a captive agent — not an independent insurance agent.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance?

Not always. Many carriers now offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for applicants under 55 with clean health histories, producing a decision in 24–72 hours. If an exam is required, a paramedical professional comes to your Miami home or office at no cost to you. Clients with significant health history may qualify for simplified-issue or guaranteed-issue products instead.

Does working with an independent insurance agency cost me more?

No. Carriers pay agent commissions, not you. Your premium is the same whether you buy directly or through an agency — but an independent agency can show you 8+ carriers and find the lowest rate for your exact profile. We charge $0 for consultations, quotes, policy reviews and claims help.

How long does it take to get approved and receive my policy?

No-exam cases are typically approved in 24–72 hours and issued within 3–7 business days. Cases requiring a medical exam or medical records usually take 2–4 weeks. We track every application and give you status updates, and our documented response guarantee is 24 hours on any question.

Can you help me with Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Miami-Dade?

Yes. We are AHIP Medicare and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified annually and CMS-compliant. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement Plan G — typically $115–$210 per month in Miami-Dade — and Part D drug plans, checking every one of your prescriptions against each formulary. Many Advantage plans are available at $0 monthly premium.

I'm self-employed with no employer benefits. What are my options?

This is one of our most common Miami client profiles — gig drivers, restaurant staff, salon professionals, contractors and small business owners. You can combine an ACA Marketplace health plan (most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, with many paying under $50/month) with a term life policy for income replacement, and add disability income insurance if you have no paid sick leave.

Do you charge any fees for consultations, quotes or policy reviews?

Never. Consultations, quotes, annual policy reviews and claims assistance are 100% free with no obligation. We also review policies you bought elsewhere at no charge — including whole life and IUL contracts — and tell you plainly if you already have good coverage.

Can I get coverage if I have health conditions or I'm over 65?

In most cases, yes. We specialize in approvals for clients with diabetes, high blood pressure, COPD and prior cardiac events. Guaranteed issue life insurance is available ages 50–85 with no health questions, and final expense coverage is designed specifically for ages 45–85 with simplified underwriting. We will tell you upfront which carriers are realistic before you apply.

What happens if a claim is denied — do you help my family?

Yes. Claims advocacy is included with every policy we place. Our agency makes the carrier calls, gathers death certificates and medical documentation, and files the paperwork on behalf of your beneficiaries. We do not hand your family a phone number and walk away — recent client claims have been paid within 11 days of filing.

Do you serve clients outside Miami-Dade County?

Life insurance is licensed statewide — we serve clients anywhere in Florida by video or phone. Health, Medicare and group benefits appointments are handled in person or by video across Miami-Dade, plus Broward (Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Fort Lauderdale) and Monroe County (Key Largo, Islamorada).

What languages does your team speak?

Our 8 licensed agents and 3 support staff provide full service in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. All documents, Medicare workshops and policy explanations are available in Spanish, and our Little Havana office hosts a free bilingual Medicare workshop on the first Wednesday of every month.
