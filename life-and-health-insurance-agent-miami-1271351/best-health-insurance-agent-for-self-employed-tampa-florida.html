Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Tampa, Florida

Updated for 2026 Open Enrollment. These are the questions our Tampa clients ask most often — answered plainly, without sales spin.

How much does health insurance cost for a self-employed person in Tampa, Florida?

It depends almost entirely on your projected income and household size. Unsubsidized Silver plans in Hillsborough County commonly run $420–$780 per month for one adult in 2026, but most self-employed Tampa clients qualify for premium tax credits that reduce that to well under $50 per month. We calculate your subsidy for free using the same income figures you will report on your 1099 return.

Do self-employed workers qualify for ACA subsidies in 2026?

Usually yes. Subsidies are based on your estimated household income for the coverage year, not your tax filing status from last year. Because 1099 income can fluctuate, we help you set a realistic projected figure and show you exactly what happens to your credit if you earn more or less than expected — so you avoid a surprise at tax time.

What makes your agency the best health insurance agent for self employed Tampa Florida clients?

Three things: we are independent, so we compare 10+ carriers instead of selling one; we have 14+ years and 5,000+ Florida enrollments behind us since 2011; and our consultations are free with commissions paid by the carrier, not you. Add A+ BBB accreditation, a 4.9-star rating from 300+ reviews, and a 24-hour response guarantee, and you can see why clients stay with us for years.

How long does it take to get covered?

Most Marketplace enrollments are completed the same day, and coverage typically starts the first of the following month — or immediately if you enroll during Open Enrollment before the 15th. Life insurance underwriting generally takes 2 days for simplified issue and 2–4 weeks for fully underwritten policies requiring a medical exam.

Do you charge any fees for your help?

No. Consultations, quotes, subsidy calculations, policy reviews, and claims advocacy are 100% free. Agent commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier. There is no fee, no obligation, and no cost to you at any point in the process — and we will tell you in writing if that ever changes.

Can you help if I have a pre-existing condition or was previously denied?

Yes. ACA Marketplace plans cannot deny you or charge more because of a pre-existing condition, and guaranteed issue life policies for ages 50–85 require no health questions and no medical exam. We also specialize in placing clients who have been declined elsewhere, including final expense and simplified issue term coverage.

Which carriers and doctors can I keep with my Tampa plan?

We are appointed with Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar, Doctor's Health Plan and Simply Healthcare for health and Medicare, plus Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber for life. Before recommending anything, we verify that your Tampa doctors — including Tampa General, AdventHealth, and Moffitt — are in-network.

Do you also write life insurance, or only health plans?

Both. Our Florida License 2-15 covers Life, Health and Annuity, so we write 10/15/20/30-year term, whole life, indexed universal life, final expense, mortgage protection with living benefits, disability income, and fixed indexed or multi-year guaranteed annuities — in addition to ACA, Medicare and small group health.

What if I need coverage outside Open Enrollment?

You may still qualify through a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying life event such as losing employer coverage, moving, marriage, divorce, or the birth of a child. We will review your situation, document the event properly, and explore alternatives like short-term or group options if no SEP applies.

What happens if a claim is denied?

Our claims advocacy is included at no charge. Our office makes the carrier calls, files the appeal paperwork, and follows the case until it resolves. In 2025 alone we resolved dozens of claim and billing disputes for Tampa Bay clients without them ever having to sit on hold.
