Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Miami

Straight answers to the questions our Miami clients ask most — written by licensed agents, not a marketing team.

How much does life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?

For a healthy 30-year-old Miami resident, $250,000 of 20-year term coverage typically runs about $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can expect roughly $58 to $75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies for ages 45 to 85 generally range from $32 to $95 monthly. Rates depend on age, health history, tobacco use and coverage amount — we confirm your exact number in writing after a free consultation.

Do you charge a fee for quotes, consultations or policy reviews?

Never. Every consultation, quote, carrier comparison and annual policy review is 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier, not by you, so our advice costs you nothing — and you are never asked to commit during a first appointment.

How fast can I get approved for a life insurance policy?

Fully underwritten policies usually issue in 2 to 4 weeks from application. Accelerated and no-medical-exam products — including many final expense and guaranteed issue plans — can issue in as little as 24 to 72 hours. We respond to every new quote request within 24 hours, and typically within 2 to 4 business hours.

Are you an independent insurance agency or tied to one company?

Fully independent. We are appointed with more than 20 carriers, including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide, Gerber Life, Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health, Doctors Health Plan and Simply Healthcare. That means we compare options side by side instead of steering you toward one brand.

Can you help me enroll in Medicare if I just turned 65?

Yes. Our team is AHIP certified annually and CMS compliant, and we help Miami-Dade clients compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Miami are available at a $0 monthly premium. We host a free bilingual Medicare workshop at our Little Havana office on the first Wednesday of each month.

What if I have a health condition like diabetes or high blood pressure?

You still have options. Many carriers offer simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue products for ages 50 to 85 with no health questions at all. For clients with well-managed diabetes or hypertension, we often still secure fully underwritten coverage at standard rates — we simply submit to the carriers most likely to approve your specific profile.

Can you review a policy I already own that you didn't sell me?

Absolutely, and it is free. Bring your current policy documents and we will review the death benefit, premium, riders, cash value, exclusions and beneficiary designations. Yes. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies.
