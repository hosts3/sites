Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Miami, Florida

Straight answers to the questions our Miami-Dade clients ask most often. Still unsure? Call +1-540-352-6249 — real people, not a call center.

How much does life insurance cost in Miami, Florida?
A healthy 30-year-old in Miami can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can usually get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58 to $75 per month. Final expense coverage generally runs $32 to $95 per month for $5,000 to $25,000 of permanent protection. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use, height and weight, and which carrier we match you with — which is exactly why we quote several at once.
Do you charge a fee for your insurance consultations or quotes?
Never. Every consultation, quote, needs analysis and annual policy review is 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by you, so there is zero cost to work with us — and zero cost to walk away.
How fast will I get a call back or a quote?
Our average callback time is under 45 minutes during business hours (Mon–Fri 8:30 AM–6:30 PM, Sat 9:00 AM–2:00 PM by appointment), and we guarantee a response to every request within 24 hours. Quotes for term life or ACA plans can often be prepared while you are still on the phone.
What is an independent insurance agent, and why does it matter in Miami?
An independent agent is not contracted to just one insurance company. We are appointed with more than 20 carriers — including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Mutual of Omaha, Transamerica, Foresters and Nationwide — so we can compare plans side by side and recommend what genuinely fits your budget, doctors and prescriptions rather than what a single company happens to sell.
Can you help me during Medicare Annual Enrollment?
Yes — it is one of our busiest seasons. Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7, and during that window we add evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM, plus Saturday sessions by appointment. We are AHIP Medicare certified annually and fully CMS-compliant for marketing and enrollment.
Can I get covered if I have health problems or take medications?
In most cases, yes. We offer guaranteed issue and final expense life insurance for ages 50 to 85 with no health questions and often no medical exam, plus simplified issue options for conditions like controlled diabetes or high blood pressure. We screen your health profile before applying so you do not waste a declined application.
Do I really need a medical exam to get life insurance?
Not always. Multiple carriers on our shelf offer accelerated underwriting or fully simplified issue, which can mean approval in 24 to 72 hours without bloodwork or a paramedical visit. If your age, coverage amount or health history does require an exam, we schedule it at your home at no cost to you.
Will you help my family file a claim when the time comes?
Yes, and this is included at no charge. Claims advocacy means our office contacts the carrier, secures the required forms, helps obtain the death certificate and follows the claim through to payment. We assist beneficiaries in English, Spanish or Haitian Creole — even for policies purchased elsewhere.
Can I get affordable ACA health insurance in Miami-Dade?
Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies — some near $0. ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15. Our help with ACA subsidy applications and Florida KidCare enrollment is completely free, and we double-check your income estimate to protect you at tax time.
Do you also write auto insurance and home insurance?
Our license and specialty is life, health, Medicare, annuities and small group benefits. For auto insurance and home insurance we refer clients to vetted local Miami partners, and we are glad to review your bundled property coverage as part of your free annual policy review so nothing overlaps or lapses.
Are you licensed and insured to sell in Florida?
Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida DFS License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). We are A+ BBB accredited since 2015, NABIP members, and carry 4.9 stars across 300+ Google reviews. Life insurance is available statewide across Florida; health and Medicare appointments are handled throughout Miami-Dade, Broward and Monroe County.
What happens at the first appointment — will I be pressured to buy?
Never. Our first appointment is education-first. We explain how life insurance underwriting, Medicare Advantage versus Medigap, and ACA subsidy calculations actually work, show you real numbers from multiple carriers, and answer your questions. No client is ever asked to commit during the first appointment, and there is no fee to keep exploring.
Do you speak Spanish or Haitian Creole?
Sí — and wi. Our 3 bilingual support staff and several agents provide full service in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole, including enrollment meetings, policy explanations, claim assistance and our free monthly Medicare workshop in Little Havana. Contact us and tell us which language you prefer.
