Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Port St. Lucie, Florida

Straight answers to the questions Treasure Coast clients ask us most — pricing, timelines, coverage details and credentials.

How much does life insurance cost in Port St. Lucie, Florida?
As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in Port St. Lucie can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can secure $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58 to $75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 run from about $32 to $95 per month depending on age and the coverage amount. Your exact rate depends on health history, tobacco use and family medical history.
Do I have to pay for an insurance consultation or quote?
No — our consultations, quotes and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. We are compensated by the insurance carrier, not by you, so you never receive an invoice from our agency. If a policy is not the right fit, we will tell you that and you owe nothing.
How fast can my life insurance policy be approved?
Final expense, simplified issue and guaranteed issue policies are frequently approved in 24 to 72 hours with no medical exam. Fully underwritten term, whole life and IUL policies usually take 2 to 4 weeks because carriers must order medical records and lab results. We track every order and update you weekly so nothing sits idle.
Can you help me compare Medicare Advantage and Medigap plans in St. Lucie County?
Yes. We are AHIP certified and CMS compliant, and appointed with Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and more. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medigap Plan G and Part D prescription drug plans side by side — including whether your specific doctors and hospitals in St. Lucie County are in network.
Do I need a medical exam to qualify for coverage?
Not always. Guaranteed issue life insurance for ages 50–85 requires no health questions and no exam. Many final expense and simplified issue policies use only a short health questionnaire and a prescription-history check. Larger term and IUL policies generally do require a paramedical exam, which we arrange at your home at no cost to you.
What if I already have a policy from another agent?
Bring it in. We offer a completely free policy review of coverage you purchased anywhere — including policies from another agency. We check your death benefit, premium, riders, cash value and beneficiary designations against current market options, then tell you honestly whether switching makes financial sense or whether you should keep what you have.
How quickly do you respond to quote requests?
We guarantee a written response within 24 hours on every quote request and client question. Our average callback time is under 2 business hours during office hours. Bilingual phone support is available in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole, Monday through Friday 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM and Saturday by appointment.
Do you meet clients in person in Port St. Lucie or only by phone?
Both. We schedule in-person appointments throughout Port St. Lucie, St. Lucie West, Tradition, Fort Pierce and Stuart. If you prefer, everything can be handled by phone or secure video — including evening appointments until 8:00 PM Monday through Thursday during Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15).
What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?
A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only present that company's products, at that company's price. An independent agency like ours is appointed with 18 carriers, so we compare multiple life, health and Medicare options side by side and recommend the one that actually fits your health profile and budget — not the one with the biggest commission.
How do I compare life insurance agents in Port St. Lucie, Florida?
Verify the agent's Florida Department of Financial Services license number and lines of authority, ask how many carriers they are appointed with, confirm that consultations are genuinely free, check their Google and BBB ratings, and ask specifically about claims support. This life insurance agent comparison guide Port St. Lucie Florida page walks through every criterion we recommend residents use.
Are there life insurance policies for people with health conditions?
Yes. We work with carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Americo, Gerber Life and Foresters Financial that specialize in simplified issue and guaranteed issue coverage for clients with diabetes, COPD, sleep apnea, heart conditions or a prior cancer diagnosis. Many clients who were declined elsewhere still qualify for coverage through us.
Do you provide claims support for beneficiaries?
Yes — it is one of our core promises. When a claim is filed, our agency calls the carrier, tracks the paperwork, follows up on missing documents and stays involved until your beneficiary receives the benefit. Families should never have to navigate a claims department alone during a difficult time.
