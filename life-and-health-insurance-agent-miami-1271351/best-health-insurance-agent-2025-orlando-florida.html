Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Orlando, Florida

These are the questions our agents hear most from Orlando callers, updated for 2026 enrollment rules. If yours isn't here, call +1-540-352-6249 and ask — there's no charge for the answer.

How much does it cost to work with a health insurance agent in Orlando, Florida?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Our agency is paid by the insurance carriers, never by you, and your premium is identical whether you enroll on your own or through us. We also review policies you bought elsewhere at no charge.

How fast can I get a quote from a licensed Orlando insurance agent?

Most callers get a live phone quote in 15 to 30 minutes during business hours, and every written comparison is delivered within our 24-hour response guarantee. Life insurance quotes often come back the same afternoon because we pre-screen health conditions with underwriters before submitting anything.

What's the best health insurance option for self-employed and 1099 workers in Orlando?

It depends on your income. If you're between 100% and 400% of the federal poverty level, an ACA Marketplace plan with premium tax credits is almost always cheapest — many Central Florida households pay under $50 a month after subsidies. Above that range we compare off-exchange plans, HSA-eligible high-deductible coverage and short-term options. We run all three side by side so you can see the real numbers.

When is Medicare enrollment in Florida and what happens if I miss the deadline?

Your Initial Enrollment Period runs seven months around your 65th birthday. Annual Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7 each year, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs January 1 to March 31. If you miss a deadline, you may face late-enrollment penalties or have to wait for the next window — so we recommend starting a review 90 days before you turn 65. We keep evening appointments open until 8:00 PM during enrollment season.

Can I get life insurance in Florida without a medical exam?

Yes. We write simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue policies for ages 45 to 85 with no medical exam — and in many cases no health questions at all. Final expense coverage typically runs $32 to $95 per month for $5,000 to $25,000 of coverage. Even fully underwritten term policies can now be approved in 48 hours with accelerated underwriting if you're healthy.

Do you only serve Miami, or do you work with clients in Orlando too?

We're headquartered in Little Havana with a satellite office in Kendall, but our life insurance agents are licensed statewide in Florida and our health and Medicare team serves Orlando by secure video and phone appointment. That means Orlando clients get the same carrier access, bilingual service and claims advocacy as our Miami clients — without a drive across the state.

Which insurance carriers do you represent in Central Florida?

Health and Medicare: Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina Healthcare, Oscar Health, Doctor's Health Plan and Simply Healthcare. Life: Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life. Being appointed with multiple carriers is what lets us compare rather than push.

What happens if my claim gets denied or delayed?

Call us before you call the carrier. Our claims advocacy team handles the carrier calls, paperwork and follow-up on your behalf, and we escalate denials through the proper appeal channels. For beneficiaries filing a life insurance claim, we do the work end to end — that's the point of having an agent instead of a website.

How do you get paid, and will I pay more by using an agent?

Carriers pay us a commission out of their marketing budget once a policy is issued — it does not come out of your premium and it does not increase your rate. If you ever find identical coverage at a lower price, we'll match it or explain in writing exactly why the difference exists.

I already have a life insurance policy. Is a free policy review worth it?

Almost always. We check whether your coverage amount still matches your mortgage, income and dependents, whether a newer carrier could beat your current rate, and whether your beneficiary designations are current. We review policies purchased anywhere, including through an employer or a captive agent. Roughly one in three reviews finds a savings opportunity or a coverage gap.

Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). Our agents complete annual continuing education, are AHIP Medicare and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified each year, and follow CMS-compliant marketing and enrollment rules. We've been A+ BBB accredited since 2015 and you can verify any license through the Florida DFS license lookup.
