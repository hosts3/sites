How much does life insurance cost in Jacksonville, Florida in 2026?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in Jacksonville can get $250,000 of 20-year term life coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 typically run $32–$95 per month depending on age and face amount. These are real quotes from the carriers we represent, not averages.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance in Florida?

Not always. Many of our carriers offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for healthy applicants under 60 — you answer health questions and they pull prescription and motor vehicle records. Guaranteed issue final expense policies for ages 50–85 require no exam and no health questions at all.

How fast can I get approved and covered?

Fully accelerated, no-exam policies are frequently approved the same day or within 24–72 hours. Applications requiring a paramedical exam typically take 3–6 weeks end to end. We guarantee a written response within 24 hours of any quote request, and our average turnaround is under 4 business hours.

Do you charge a fee for your help?

Never. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, Medicare comparisons and enrollment assistance are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers — not by you — so working with an independent agent costs the same as buying direct, but with far more comparison.

Can you help me choose Medicare coverage in Jacksonville?

Yes. We're AHIP certified annually and CMS compliant. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap Plan G and N) and Part D prescription drug plans from Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna and Cigna. Many Advantage plans in Duval County have a $0 monthly premium, and we host a free bilingual Medicare workshop on the first Wednesday of each month.

Do you help with ACA Marketplace and Obamacare subsidies?

We do, at no cost. Most Florida households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies are applied. We help you estimate income correctly so you don't owe subsidy repayments at tax time, and we assist with Florida KidCare enrollment for children.

What if I have health issues, or I've been declined before?

That's a large part of what we do. We pre-screen your health history across 18 carriers to find the company that will rate you most favorably — one carrier declines COPD while another approves it at a standard rate. If fully underwritten coverage isn't possible, we'll place guaranteed issue coverage with no health questions.

Will you review a life insurance policy I already own?

Yes, and it's free. Our no-obligation policy review covers policies purchased anywhere — not just through us. We check your death benefit against your current mortgage and income needs, verify beneficiary designations, review cash value performance on permanent policies, and tell you if a newer carrier rate would save money.

What happens if my family needs to file a claim?

We act as your claims advocate at no charge. Our office calls the carrier, submits the required documents and follows up weekly on your beneficiary's behalf. Recent claims we've handled have been paid in as little as 17 days. Claims support is included with every policy we place.

Are you licensed and credentialed in Florida?

Yes. We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). We're AHIP Medicare certified annually, CMS compliant, members of NABIP, and BBB A+ accredited since 2015. All agents complete annual continuing education.

Do you offer appointments in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Sí — and wi. Our bilingual support team serves clients in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole by phone, video and in person. We can explain underwriting, Medicare rules and ACA subsidy calculations in whichever language you're most comfortable making financial decisions in.

Why Choosing the Right Life and Health Insurance Agent Matters in Jacksonville, Florida

Households near the Intracoastal and in low-lying areas around the Trout River and Julington Creek carry different property and financial exposure than a family in Riverside or Avondale — and the right life insurance amount usually reflects that.

Northeast Florida also has a distinctive workforce: Naval Air Station Jacksonville and Mayport personnel, thousands of healthcare employees at Mayo Clinic and Baptist Health, logistics and distribution workers at the port, and a large population of self-employed contractors and gig drivers who have no employer life insurance or group health benefits. For those households, an independent agent who can shop ACA Marketplace subsidies and term life at the same appointment is worth far more than a single-carrier website.

With the Annual Enrollment Period running October 15 through December 7 and ACA Open Enrollment from November 1 through January 15, the timing of your decision can be worth hundreds of dollars a year. Our team is CMS compliant, AHIP certified each year, and we do not sell one carrier's product — we compare Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Cigna and more before recommending anything.

On the licensing side, every agent who works with you holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — our agency operates under License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). Life insurance is licensed and available statewide across Florida, so if you move from Jacksonville to Orlando, Tampa or back to South Florida, your coverage and your agent move with you.
