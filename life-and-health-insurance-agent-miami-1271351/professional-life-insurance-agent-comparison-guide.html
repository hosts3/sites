Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Miami

The questions Miami clients ask us most often — answered directly. Still unsure about something? Call +1-540-352-6249 and ask.

How much does a life insurance agent cost in Miami?

Nothing. Our consultations, carrier comparisons and quotes are 100% free with no obligation. Commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you — so working with a licensed agent costs the same as buying direct, except you get multiple carriers compared side by side.

What does a professional life insurance agent comparison guide actually include?

You receive one written table showing each carrier's monthly premium, coverage amount, term length, riders, waiting periods and exclusions for your exact age and health profile. We build it from live quotes across 8+ A-rated carriers appointmented with our agency, then walk you through it line by line.

How fast can I get approved for life insurance in Miami?

Final expense and simplified-issue policies are often approved in 24–72 hours with no medical exam. Fully underwritten term and whole life policies usually take 2–4 weeks depending on exam scheduling and medical record retrieval. We follow every case daily and push carriers on turnaround.

How much does term life insurance cost in Miami-Dade?

Healthy 30-year-olds commonly qualify for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage at roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can often get $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 per month. Final expense coverage runs about $32–$95 monthly for $5,000–$25,000. Rates depend on age, health and tobacco use.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. We offer guaranteed issue and simplified-issue products for ages 45–85 that require no exam and, in some cases, no health questions at all. Coverage amounts for these products typically range from $5,000 to $25,000. Ask us which no-exam option matches your health history.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Miami?

Yes. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant, appointed with Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and Simply Healthcare. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Miami-Dade have a $0 monthly premium, and Medicare Supplement Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month based on age. Enrollment assistance is free.

Do you charge for ACA Marketplace or Florida KidCare enrollment help?

No — it is completely free. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many pay under $50 per month after subsidies. We help with Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health, Doctors Health Plan and Florida KidCare applications, income verification and annual re-certification.

What licenses and certifications does your agency hold?

We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health). Our team is AHIP Medicare and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified annually, a member of NABIP, and A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015, with a 4.9-star Google rating from 300+ reviews.

Do you provide emergency or after-hours help?

Yes. Quote requests and client questions receive a response within 24 hours, and most are answered within three business hours. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15–Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1–Jan 15) we offer evening video appointments Monday–Thursday until 8:00 PM. Our phone line is monitored 24/7.

What happens if I find a cheaper policy somewhere else?

We will match it or explain exactly why the difference exists — different underwriting class, rider structure, conversion privileges or carrier financial strength. Our rate-shopping guarantee exists so you never have to guess whether you got a fair deal. Free annual policy reviews keep it that way.

Do you help beneficiaries file claims?

Yes, at no charge. When a client passes, we call the carrier, confirm the required documents, submit the death certificate and follow the claim until funds are released. Most beneficiary claims we handle in South Florida are paid within 10–15 days.

Which areas of Miami do you serve?

We serve all of Miami-Dade County, including Little Havana, Hialeah, Coral Gables, Kendall, Doral, Brickell, Little Haiti, Aventura, Miami Beach, Homestead, Cutler Bay, Palmetto Bay, Sweetwater and Westchester, plus Broward and Monroe counties. Life insurance is licensed statewide across Florida.

Do you offer small group employee benefits for Miami businesses?

Yes. For businesses with 5–50 employees — restaurants, salons, contractors, retail shops and professional offices — we build group health, dental, vision and disability packages, handle carrier submissions and run on-site enrollment meetings for your staff in English or Spanish.

Is my consultation really no-pressure?

Yes. No client is ever asked to commit during a first appointment. We educate first — how Medicare works, how ACA subsidies are calculated, how life insurance underwriting classes affect your rate — and you decide afterward. Call +1-540-352-6249 whenever you are ready.
