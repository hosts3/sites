What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent in St. Petersburg, Florida?
A captive agent represents one insurance company and can only sell that company's products. An independent insurance agent is appointed with multiple carriers, so we can compare rates, underwriting rules and networks across companies at once. The practical difference is choice: if one carrier declines you or prices you high, we simply quote another.
Does hiring an independent insurance agency cost me anything?
No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, subsidy calculations and Medicare enrollment help are 100% free. Agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you, and your premium is identical whether you buy directly from the carrier or through our office.
How much does term life insurance cost in St. Petersburg, Florida?
As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old typically pays $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage, and a healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000. Final rates depend on age, tobacco use, prescriptions and family medical history.
How long does it take to get a life insurance policy issued?
No-exam policies are frequently approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten policies that require labs or medical records usually issue in 2 to 4 weeks. We track every application daily and update you at each milestone until the policy is delivered.
