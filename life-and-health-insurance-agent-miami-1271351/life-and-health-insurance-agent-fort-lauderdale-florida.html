How much does a life and health insurance agent cost in Fort Lauderdale, Florida?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, and enrollment help are 100% free — no fees, no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by you, so the premium you pay is identical whether you work with us or apply directly. You can learn more on our services page.

What does term life insurance cost for a healthy 40-year-old in Fort Lauderdale?

As of 2026, a healthy 40-year-old typically pays roughly $30–$45 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. A healthy 45-year-old pays about $58–$75 monthly for the same coverage, and a healthy 30-year-old around $18–$24 for $250,000. Final rates depend on tobacco use, medical history, and the carrier's underwriting class.

How fast can I get covered?

Final expense and guaranteed-issue policies can be issued in 24 to 48 hours. Simplified-issue life policies usually take 2 to 5 business days. Fully underwritten term and permanent policies may take 2 to 6 weeks if a medical exam or records are requested. ACA Marketplace coverage can be effective as early as the first of the next month.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance?

Often, no. We have access to no-exam and accelerated-underwriting programs that use prescription history, motor vehicle records, and a phone interview instead of bloodwork. For coverage up to $500,000, many Fort Lauderdale applicants qualify with no exam at all — and if an exam is required, we schedule it in your home at no cost.

Are you an independent agency or do you work for one insurance company?

We are fully independent. Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group is appointed with 18+ carriers including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, Transamerica, John Hancock, and Nationwide. Because we aren't tied to one brand, we can honestly tell you when a plan isn't right for you.

What licenses and certifications do your agents hold?

Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) or 2-40 (Health) — and completes annual continuing education. Our Medicare team is AHIP certified each year and trained on CMS marketing and enrollment compliance. Our agency has been A+ BBB accredited since 2015.

Can you help me review a policy I already own?

Yes, and it's free even if you bought the policy elsewhere. Our no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review checks your death benefit against today's costs, verifies your beneficiaries are current, and looks for gaps or duplicate coverage. Many clients discover they're paying for a rider they didn't need or that a 2015-era policy now costs more than a new one.

Do you offer emergency or same-day appointments?

Yes. We keep same-day and next-day appointment openings for urgent situations — a hospital discharge, a surgery scheduled, a new baby, or a mortgage closing deadline. We also offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare Annual Enrollment (October 15 – December 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (November 1 – January 15).

How does a Medicare Supplement compare to Medicare Advantage in Broward County?

Medicare Advantage plans typically have $0 monthly premiums but use networks and copays. Medigap Plan G costs roughly $115–$210 per month in Miami-Dade and Broward but lets you see any Medicare-accepting provider nationwide with very few out-of-pocket costs. The right choice depends on how often you travel, which doctors you want to keep, and your prescription list — we compare both in one appointment.

Can I qualify for ACA subsidies in Fort Lauderdale?

Most Broward County households do. Premium tax credits are based on household size and projected income relative to the federal poverty level, not on your assets. Many of our clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies, and some qualify for cost-sharing reductions that lower deductibles too. We estimate your subsidy before you enroll — at no cost.

What happens if my claim is denied or delayed?

We advocate on your behalf. Our agency contacts the carrier directly, submits the required documentation, and follows the claim through to resolution — you don't have to navigate hold queues or paperwork alone. Beneficiaries get the same support, including help filing death claims and completing affidavits for final expense policies.
