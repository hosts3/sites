How much does life insurance cost in Fort Lauderdale, Florida in 2026?

Rates depend on age, health, coverage amount and term length. As a real benchmark, a healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month, while a healthy 45-year-old can often get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 usually runs $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. We give you exact carrier quotes in writing — never an estimate.

Is there any cost or fee for using your insurance agency?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Our commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier, not by you, and your premium is the same whether you buy through us or directly from the insurer — the difference is that you get comparison shopping and ongoing service.

How fast can I get covered, and how long does the process take?

Health and Medicare enrollments can often be completed the same day you call, with coverage effective the first of the following month. Term life insurance with accelerated underwriting is frequently issued in 3–10 business days; fully underwritten policies requiring a medical exam may take 3–6 weeks depending on how quickly records arrive from your physician. We provide a written 24-hour response guarantee on every request.

Do you help with Medicare if I'm turning 65 in Broward County?

Yes — Medicare is one of our specialties. We are AHIP Medicare certified annually and CMS-compliant, and we compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement plans such as Plan G (typically $115–$210 per month in South Florida), and standalone Part D drug plans. Many Medicare Advantage plans are available at a $0 monthly premium. We verify your doctors and prescriptions are covered before recommending anything.

Can I get ACA Obamacare coverage with a subsidy if I'm self-employed in Fort Lauderdale?

Very likely, yes. Most Broward and Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many self-employed clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies are applied. Enrollment help is free, and ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15 each year — though a Special Enrollment Period may apply if you lose coverage, move or have a qualifying life event.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

In many cases, yes. We have access to no-exam and accelerated underwriting programs through carriers like Mutual of Omaha, Americo, Foresters and Gerber Life, and our Guaranteed Issue Life Insurance option for ages 50–85 requires no health questions at all. Approval is based on age, coverage amount and a short health questionnaire, which makes it a good fit for clients managing diabetes, COPD or a prior cancer diagnosis.

What makes an independent insurance agent better than buying online?

An independent agent does not work for one carrier. We are appointed with 18+ companies including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Mutual of Omaha and Transamerica, so we can place your case where it is priced and underwritten most favorably. You also get a human who handles carrier calls, claim paperwork and annual reviews — things an online quote engine will never do for your family.

Do you offer bilingual service in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Sí — and Kreyòl tou. Our 8 licensed agents and 3 support staff provide bilingual phone, in-person and video support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. We host a free bilingual Medicare workshop at our Little Havana office on the first Wednesday of every month, and we prepare documents and explain every clause in the language you are most comfortable with.

What is your claims support promise if something happens to me?

When a claim is filed, our agency becomes your family's advocate. We call the carrier, request the forms, help your beneficiary complete them correctly, track the claim status and follow up until the benefit is paid. There is no charge for this service, and it is important for clients, because a single paperwork error is one of the most common reasons a life insurance claim is delayed.

Do you guarantee you will find the lowest rate?

We back our shopping with a rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower rate for identical coverage from a licensed carrier, we will either match it or explain in detail why the difference exists — underwriting class, riders, conversion privileges or cash value features. We also offer a free annual policy review, including policies you bought somewhere else.

Can you help small businesses in Fort Lauderdale with employee benefits?

Yes. We build small group employee benefit packages for businesses with 5–50 employees, including health, dental, vision, life and disability options. We also advise owners on executive life insurance and annuities. Group plan designs are customized around your budget per employee and your hiring goals, and we handle the carrier paperwork and employee enrollment meetings.
