Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Tallahassee, Florida

The questions we hear most often from Leon County families and business owners — answered plainly, with real numbers.

What is the real difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent in Tallahassee, Florida?
A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only sell that company's products — State Farm, Allstate and similar models. An independent insurance agent is appointed with many carriers and can quote your exact situation across all of them, then recommend the best fit. In Tallahassee, Florida that typically means more coverage options, more underwriting flexibility for health conditions, and a lower premium for the same benefit.
How much does life insurance cost in Tallahassee in 2026?
Rates are based on age, health and coverage amount, not on your zip code — so Tallahassee pricing matches the rest of Florida. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage from roughly $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term from roughly $58–$75 per month. We run personalized quotes free so you see your actual number, not an average.
Do you charge a fee for your insurance services?
No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are always free, with no obligation and no hidden fees. Our commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier you choose — never by you. That is why we can compare 18+ carriers without it costing you a dollar more than buying direct.
How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?
Simplified-issue and final expense policies often issue in 24 to 72 hours because there is no medical exam. Fully underwritten term and whole life policies typically take 2 to 4 weeks depending on how quickly medical records come back. We monitor underwriting daily and will push carriers on your behalf if a case stalls.
Can you help me with Medicare if I am turning 65 in Tallahassee?
Yes. We are AHIP Medicare-certified annually and CMS-compliant in our marketing and enrollment. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and others, and we verify that your Tallahassee doctors and prescriptions are covered before you enroll. Plan G Medigap rates in Florida typically run $115–$210 per month depending on age.
Do I need a medical exam to qualify for coverage?
Not always. Many of our carriers offer no-exam simplified issue on term, final expense and mortgage protection policies, with decisions based on a short health questionnaire. Guaranteed-issue life insurance for ages 50–85 has no health questions at all, though coverage amounts are smaller and there is typically a two-year graded benefit period. We will tell you honestly which path gives you the best value.
I am self-employed with no work benefits. What are my options?
You are one of our most common client types in Tallahassee — gig drivers, contractors, restaurant and salon workers. We usually pair an ACA Marketplace health plan (most Florida households qualify for premium tax credits, and many pay under $50 per month after subsidies) with a level term life policy sized to your income and debts. We also handle Florida KidCare enrollment for dependent children at no cost.
