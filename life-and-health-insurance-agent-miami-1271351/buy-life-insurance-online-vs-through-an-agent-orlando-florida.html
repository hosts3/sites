Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Orlando, Florida

Straight answers to the questions our Orlando clients ask most — updated with 2026 rates and enrollment windows.

Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in Orlando, Florida?

Premiums are filed by the carrier and regulated by the state, so the rate for identical coverage is the same whether you apply online or through us. The difference is service: we compare 8 carriers at once, catch underwriting issues before they become declines, and handle claims for your family. Our consultations are free because carriers pay the commission — not you.

How much does term life insurance cost in Orlando in 2026?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage from about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term from roughly $58–$75 per month. Final rates depend on age, health history, tobacco use, and the carrier's underwriting class.

How long does it take to get approved and issued?

No-exam policies using accelerated underwriting can be issued in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten policies involving labs and medical records usually take 2 to 6 weeks. We respond to every carrier request within 24 hours, which is often what shortens the timeline most.

Do you charge a fee or commission to the client?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, claims assistance, and Medicare or ACA enrollment help are all 100% free. There is no obligation to purchase, and clients are never asked to commit during the first appointment. Our compensation comes entirely from the insurance carriers.

Can I qualify for life insurance without a medical exam?

Often, yes. Accelerated underwriting uses prescription history, motor vehicle records, and a health questionnaire instead of bloodwork. If you have significant health conditions, guaranteed issue final expense coverage is available for ages 50–85 with no health questions, generally between $5,000 and $25,000 of coverage.

What is the difference between term life and whole life insurance?

Term life covers you for a set period — 10, 15, 20, or 30 years — at the lowest monthly cost, and pays a benefit only if you pass away during that term. Whole life and IUL are permanent, never expire, and accumulate cash value you can borrow against. Most Orlando families start with term and add permanent coverage later.

What credentials and licenses does your agency hold?

We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). Our agents complete AHIP Medicare certification and Fraud, Waste & Abuse training annually, and we have been BBB A+ accredited since 2015 with a 4.9-star rating across 312 reviews.

Can you help if I already have a policy with another company?

Absolutely — and it is free. Our no-obligation policy review works on policies you did not buy through us. We check your death benefit against current needs, verify beneficiary designations, and tell you honestly whether your existing coverage is still competitive or overpriced. No pressure to switch.

Do you offer same-day or emergency help?

Yes. Call +1-540-352-6249 and a licensed agent responds during business hours, with a 24-hour response guarantee on every quote request. For active claims or a death benefit that needs to be filed urgently, we prioritize the case and handle carrier calls and paperwork on your behalf.

When can I enroll in Medicare or ACA Marketplace coverage?

Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7, and ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15. Outside those windows you may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period after a move, job loss, marriage, or birth. We offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during both enrollment periods.

Do you serve clients in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Sí — and wi. Our team includes bilingual support staff and agents who serve clients in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole. We explain Medicare, ACA subsidies, and life insurance underwriting in the language you are most comfortable making financial decisions in.

Is an online life insurance quote actually binding?

No. Online calculators produce estimates based on the health class you self-select. A binding offer only comes after the carrier reviews your application, prescriptions, and sometimes a paramedical exam. That is why two people who "got the same quote online" frequently end up with very different final premiums.
