How much does self-employed health insurance cost in Cape Coral, Florida?

As of 2026, most self-employed Cape Coral clients qualify for ACA premium tax credits. After subsidies, many households pay $0 to $50 per month. Without a subsidy, a silver plan for a 40-year-old in Lee County generally runs $420 to $560 per month. We calculate your exact credit for free before you enroll.

Do you charge a fee to help me compare or enroll in a plan?

Never. Consultations, quotes, subsidy calculations, enrollment and your annual policy review are 100% free with no obligation. Carriers pay our commission, so your cost is identical whether you enroll alone or with an agent in your corner.

Can a 1099 contractor or gig driver get health coverage in Cape Coral?

Yes. Rideshare drivers, realtors, salon and restaurant workers, tradespeople and independent consultants all qualify for Marketplace plans. Enroll during Open Enrollment (November 1 – January 15) or within 60 days of a qualifying life event such as a move, marriage, birth or loss of other coverage.

How quickly can I get a quote and be enrolled?

Most Cape Coral clients receive three to five plan comparisons within 24 hours and complete enrollment the same week. Our written 24-hour response guarantee covers every quote request and follow-up question, and urgent calls are answered in under two hours during business hours.

Are you licensed and credentialed in Florida?

Yes. We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Only). Our agents complete annual continuing education and AHIP Medicare certification, we are CMS-compliant for Medicare marketing, and we have been BBB A+ accredited since 2015.

What life insurance do self-employed people usually choose?

Most choose 20-year level term because it protects the mortgage and income years at the lowest cost. Healthy 30-year-olds see $250,000 from roughly $18–$24 per month; healthy 45-year-olds see $500,000 from about $58–$75 per month. Clients who want cash value often add an IUL or whole life policy.

Do you offer life insurance with no medical exam?

Yes. Final expense and guaranteed issue policies for ages 45 to 85 require no exam and no health questions. Coverage ranges from $5,000 to $25,000 and typically costs $32 to $95 per month, with approvals often issued in 24 to 72 hours.

Can you help during Medicare Annual Enrollment?

Absolutely. Annual Enrollment runs October 15 – December 7, and we add evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Lee County carry a $0 monthly premium, and Medicare Supplement Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month depending on age.

I already have a policy I bought from someone else. Will you still review it?

Yes, and it is free. We review policies you did not purchase through us — including final expense and term policies from other agencies. Our rate-shopping guarantee means if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, we will match it or explain exactly why the difference exists.

What happens if a claim is denied or a beneficiary needs help?

Our claims advocacy team handles the carrier calls and paperwork on your behalf — including for beneficiaries after a loss. We stay on the file until it is resolved, and that support is included with every policy at no extra charge. There is no fee and no time limit.

Do you meet Cape Coral clients in person, or is it all by video?

Both. We meet clients at home, at their business or at a local coffee shop by appointment, and we also offer statewide video appointments for life insurance. Bilingual phone support is available in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole, and we are available 24/7 for urgent questions.

Is there a referral bonus for sending friends and family?

Yes. You receive a $50 gift card for every referral that results in an issued policy, and we give the person you refer a $50 thank-you card as well. Family members can be referred even if they live elsewhere in Florida — life insurance is licensed statewide.
