Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Jacksonville, Florida

Updated for 2026 — the questions Jacksonville clients ask us most often, answered straight.

How much does life insurance cost in Jacksonville, Florida?

A healthy 30-year-old nonsmoker can get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies range from $32 to $95 monthly. Rates depend on age, health history, tobacco use and face amount — and we quote up to eight carriers so you see the lowest real number, not an average.

Do you charge a fee for your insurance services?

Never. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, Medicare comparisons and ACA enrollment help are all 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by clients, so our recommendation costs you nothing out of pocket — and no client is ever asked to commit during the first appointment.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Fully underwritten term and permanent policies typically issue in 2 to 6 weeks after the medical exam and records review. Accelerated-underwriting and simplified-issue products — including most final expense and guaranteed issue plans — can be approved and active in 24 to 72 hours. We track every application daily and update you at each stage.

Can I get life insurance if I have health problems or I'm over 65?

Yes. Guaranteed issue life insurance is available from ages 50 to 85 with no health questions asked and no medical exam required. Final expense and burial coverage is available from ages 45 to 85 with simplified underwriting. For more serious conditions we shop carriers that specialize in impaired risk rather than simply declining the case.

Is an independent insurance agent better than a captive agent?

An independent agent contracts with multiple carriers, so we can put eight companies' rates side by side in a single appointment. A captive agent represents one brand only, which means you see one price no matter how uncompetitive it is. Independence also matters at claim time, because we can escalate on your behalf without worrying about protecting a single carrier relationship.

How does Medicare enrollment work in Jacksonville, Florida in 2026?

Your Initial Enrollment Period runs seven months — three before your 65th birthday month, the birthday month, and three after. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medigap Plan G and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and Molina, then check every prescription against each formulary. Our agents are AHIP certified annually and fully CMS compliant.

Can you help me qualify for ACA subsidies or Florida KidCare?

Absolutely, at zero cost to you. Most Jacksonville households qualify for premium tax credits, and many families end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies are applied. We complete the HealthCare.gov application, help document your income correctly, and handle annual redetermination. We also submit Florida KidCare applications for children at no charge.

What exactly is a free policy review, and do I need one?

A free policy review means we examine a policy you already own — even one purchased from another agency — and check the premium, term length, riders, cash value, beneficiary designations and carrier rating. Many Jacksonville clients discover they are paying 30–40% above current market rates, or that a beneficiary is a deceased ex-spouse. The review takes about 25 minutes and carries no obligation to change anything.

Do you offer same-day or emergency appointments?

We answer the phone 24/7 at +1-540-352-6249 and reply to quote requests within 24 hours — typically under two business hours. Same-day and next-day appointments are frequently available for hospital discharges, hospice or terminal-illness situations, and urgent Medicare enrollment deadlines. Applications signed before 3:00 PM Eastern are submitted to the carrier the same day.

Do you help with claims, or is that my family's responsibility?

We handle claims advocacy for you. When a policyholder passes, our office calls the carrier, orders certified death certificates, completes the claimant statement, submits medical records if requested, and follows up with the beneficiary until funds are disbursed. Beneficiaries should never have to navigate a claims department alone during a difficult time.
