Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Fort Lauderdale, Florida

Straight answers about pricing, timelines, guarantees and credentials — the same answers we give on the phone.

How much does life insurance cost in Fort Lauderdale, Florida in 2026?
Rates in Broward County run slightly below the national average. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally run $32–$95 monthly. Your exact rate depends on age, tobacco use, health history and coverage amount — we quote all carriers at once.
What exactly is an independent life insurance agent, and why does it matter?
An independent agent is contracted with many insurance companies instead of one. We are appointed with Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life for life insurance, plus eight health and Medicare carriers. That means we can show you the lowest legitimate price for your health profile rather than steering you to a single company's product.
How long does it take to get approved and issued?
Fully underwritten policies typically take 3–6 weeks, but accelerated and no-exam underwriting often issues in 2–10 business days. Guaranteed-issue final expense policies can be active in as little as 48 hours. Medicare and ACA enrollments are usually completed in a single appointment with coverage effective the first of the following month.
Do you charge any fees for your help?
No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, subsidy calculations and enrollment assistance are 100% free — no fees and no obligation. Agent commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier, not by you, and the commission does not change the premium you pay. You would pay the exact same rate buying directly from the carrier.
Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?
Yes. We have access to no-exam and accelerated-underwriting products with coverage up to $500,000 that use prescription history, motor vehicle records and a short phone interview instead of bloodwork. We also offer guaranteed-issue final expense coverage for ages 50–85 with no health questions at all — though these policies include a graded benefit period, typically 24 months.
What are your credentials and licenses?
Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). Our agents complete annual continuing education and AHIP Medicare certification covering Fraud, Waste & Abuse compliance. We are A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015, members of NABIP, and rated 4.9 stars across 300+ Google reviews.
Do you offer emergency or same-day appointments?
We do. If you have a hospital discharge, a coverage gap, a lapsed policy, or a Medicare enrollment deadline approaching, call +1-540-352-6249 and tell us it is urgent. Our team triages urgent requests the same business day and can often complete a video or phone appointment within a few hours. Phone support is available 24/7.
How do you compare to buying directly from a carrier online?
Buying direct limits you to one company's underwriting and one price. As an independent agency we shop 18+ carriers at once, which is exactly why a life insurance agent comparison guide matters in Fort Lauderdale — the same applicant can be rated Preferred with one carrier and Standard with another, a difference of hundreds of dollars a year. We also handle claims advocacy after the policy is issued, which direct online purchases do not.
Do you help with Medicare Advantage, Medigap and Part D?
Yes. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant for Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans. Many Broward County Medicare Advantage plans carry a $0 monthly premium, while Plan G supplements generally run $115–$210 per month depending on age. We check every plan against your actual doctors and prescriptions before recommending anything.
Can you help me qualify for ACA subsidies or Florida KidCare?
Absolutely, and at no cost to you. Most Miami-Dade and Broward County households qualify for premium tax credits; many families end up paying under $50 a month after subsidies. We calculate your projected income credit, handle the marketplace application, and assist with Florida KidCare enrollment for children at $0 additional cost.
What happens if my beneficiary needs to file a claim?
Call our office first. Our team contacts the carrier, requests the correct claim forms, helps your beneficiary complete them, and follows the file until it pays — at no charge and with no time limit. Claims advocacy is one of the four core service promises we make to every client, regardless of when the policy was purchased.
Will you review a policy I already own from another agent?
Yes, and it is completely free with no obligation to switch. We review the death benefit, premium schedule, riders, cash value performance and beneficiary designations, then tell you plainly whether your current coverage is still competitive. Many clients keep their existing policy — we simply add a free annual review to keep it accurate as their family changes.
