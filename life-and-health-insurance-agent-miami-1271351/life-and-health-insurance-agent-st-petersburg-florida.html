How much does life insurance cost in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Pricing depends on age, health and coverage amount. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month, while a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally run $32–$95 monthly.

Is the consultation or quote really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation and no hidden fees. Our agency is paid by the insurance carrier, not by you, so you never receive an invoice for advice, quotes or a policy review. There is no charge even if you decide not to buy anything.

How long does it take to get approved for a life insurance policy?

Fully underwritten policies typically take 2–10 business days after the application and any required medical exam. Accelerated and no-exam underwriting can issue coverage in as little as 24–72 hours for qualified applicants. Final expense and guaranteed-issue products are usually the fastest, often issued within 3–5 days.

Do you help St. Petersburg residents with Medicare Advantage and Medigap?

Absolutely. We are AHIP-certified annually and contracted with Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and others. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Pinellas County are available at a $0 monthly premium, while Medigap Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month depending on age and carrier.

Can I get ACA Marketplace coverage with a subsidy if I live in Pinellas County?

Most St. Petersburg households qualify for premium tax credits based on income and household size. Many of our clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies. ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15, though a Special Enrollment Period may apply after a qualifying life event such as a move, marriage, or job loss.

What if I have health issues — can I still get coverage?

Often yes. We work with carriers that specialize in higher-risk cases. Guaranteed-issue life insurance for ages 50–85 requires no health questions and no medical exam, and final expense products frequently accept clients with diabetes, high blood pressure or a prior cancer diagnosis. We will tell you honestly which carriers will look at your situation.

Do you sell coverage from more than one insurance company?

Yes — we are an independent agency with appointments at 18+ carriers, including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar, Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica and Gerber Life. That means you get side-by-side comparisons rather than a single company's pitch.

What licenses and certifications does your agency hold?

Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). We are AHIP Medicare and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified annually, CMS-compliant in our marketing, members of NABIP, and A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015.

Do you help after a claim is filed?

Yes. Claims advocacy is included at no cost. Our staff calls the carrier, tracks the claim status, and completes the required paperwork on behalf of beneficiaries. Families should not have to navigate a death claim or a denied medical claim alone during an already difficult time.

Can I meet by phone or video instead of in person?

Yes. Life insurance consultations are available by video anywhere in Florida, and health and Medicare appointments can be handled by phone or video if you prefer. Evening video appointments run Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare and ACA Open Enrollment. Bilingual support is available in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

Will you review a policy I already own from another agency?

Yes — and it is free, including policies not purchased through us. Bring your declarations page and we will benchmark your premium, death benefit, riders and cash value against current 2026 market rates. If your existing policy is competitive, we will tell you to keep it.

What neighborhoods near St. Petersburg do you serve?

We serve all of Pinellas County, including St. Petersburg, Gulfport, St. Pete Beach, Treasure Island, Tierra Verde, Pinellas Park, Kenneth City, Largo, Seminole, Clearwater, Safety Harbor, Bay Pines and Madeira Beach. Life insurance is also licensed and available statewide across Florida.
