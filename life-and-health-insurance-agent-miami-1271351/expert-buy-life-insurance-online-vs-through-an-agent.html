Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Miami

Straight answers on pricing, timelines, credentials and the online-versus-agent decision.

Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent?

For the same carrier and the same policy, the premium is identical — carriers pay agent commissions, so working with a licensed agent costs you nothing extra. The real savings come from underwriting placement: our Miami agents match your health profile to the carrier most likely to approve a preferred class, which often beats a generic online quote by 10–25%. Clients regularly find $5 to $30 in monthly savings on identical term coverage after we re-shop an online quote.

How much does a consultation with your Miami insurance agency cost?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, policy comparisons and annual reviews are 100% free with no obligation and no hidden fees. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier after a policy is issued, never by the client. You can walk away from any appointment without owing us a penny.

How fast can I get life insurance approved in Florida?

Simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue final expense policies are frequently approved the same business day, and we've had policies issued in 48 hours. Fully underwritten term life with a paramedical exam typically takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on how quickly medical records arrive from your physician's office. We track every file daily and follow up with carriers so nothing stalls.

What does "expert buy life insurance online vs through an agent" actually mean for my family?

It comes down to three things: who verifies the rate is real, who handles a denied claim, and who answers when you have a question in year seven. Online portals are great for comparing price. A licensed agent adds underwriting strategy, claims advocacy and an annual policy review — the parts that matter when something goes wrong rather than when everything is easy.

Do you charge extra for Medicare, ACA or final expense enrollment help?

No. Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ACA Marketplace and Florida KidCare enrollment assistance are all provided at zero cost to you. We're AHIP Medicare certified annually and CMS-compliant in our marketing and enrollment practices, so you get accurate plan information without pressure or upselling.

What if I already bought a policy online — can you still help me?

Absolutely. Our free no-obligation policy review covers policies you purchased anywhere, including direct-to-consumer websites. We'll confirm your beneficiary designations are correct, check whether your rate class was accurate, and tell you honestly whether you're overpaying. If you are, we'll show you the alternative — and if you're not, we'll tell you that too.

Are your agents licensed and insured in Florida?

Yes. Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — License 2-15 for Life, Health and Annuity, and License 2-40 for health-only — and completes annual continuing education plus AHIP Medicare certification. Our agency has been A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015 and carries full professional liability coverage.

Can I get an evening or weekend appointment in Miami?

Yes. Standard office hours are Monday through Friday 8:30 AM – 6:30 PM and Saturday 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM by appointment at our Little Havana and Kendall offices. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15) we also hold video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM. Phone support is available 24/7 in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

What if my health history triggers a denial when I apply online?

This is one of the strongest reasons to work with a local agent. Declines and rated offers are common online because applicants self-select the wrong carrier. We re-shop declined cases to simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue carriers — including options with no medical exam for ages 50 to 85 — and we've placed coverage for clients with diabetes, prior cardiac events and controlled cancer histories.

Do you only serve Miami, or the whole state of Florida?

We write life insurance statewide across all of Florida through virtual appointments, and we serve health and Medicare clients in person or by video throughout Miami-Dade County with secondary coverage in Broward and Monroe counties. Offices are located in Little Havana and Kendall, and our agents travel to clients in Hialeah, Coral Gables, Doral, Homestead, Aventura and Miami Beach regularly.

What happens to my family if I pass away — who files the claim?

We do. Claims advocacy is included with every policy at no charge. Our team contacts the carrier, requests the required forms, submits the certified death certificate and follows up with the claims examiner until the benefit is paid. In a recent Miami-Dade case, that process took 11 days from first call to payout — with the beneficiary never having to speak to a call center.

How do I know your quotes are real and not teaser pricing?

Every quote you receive from us is generated through carrier illustration systems using the health information you provide, and we disclose the rate class it assumes — preferred plus, preferred, standard or table rated. We also honor a rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower published rate for identical coverage, we will match it or explain in writing exactly why the difference exists.

Do you offer anything to new clients in 2026?

Yes. Every new client receives a free no-obligation life insurance policy review, a $50 gift card referral bonus for each referral that results in an issued policy, and complimentary $0-cost enrollment help for Florida KidCare and ACA subsidies. We also host a free bilingual Medicare workshop at our Little Havana office on the first Wednesday of every month.
