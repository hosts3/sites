Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Tallahassee, Florida

Updated for 2026 — the questions our Tallahassee clients ask most before they choose a policy.

How much does life insurance cost in Tallahassee, Florida?
Pricing depends on age, health and coverage amount. In 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Tallahassee resident can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month, while a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 usually run $32–$95 per month. Every quote we provide is free and itemized.
Do you charge a fee for comparing insurance carriers?
Never. Consultations, comparisons and quotes are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by clients — so you pay exactly the same premium whether you work with us or call the carrier directly. You simply get comparison shopping as a free benefit.
How quickly can I get a quote and get covered?
Most Tallahassee, Florida quote requests receive a side-by-side carrier comparison within 24 hours — and often under 60 minutes during business hours if you call +1-540-352-6249. Once you choose a plan, no-exam applications are frequently approved in 2 to 10 business days, with coverage effective the day the first premium is drafted.
Do I need a medical exam to qualify for coverage in Florida?
Not always. We represent carriers that use accelerated underwriting, which can approve healthy applicants with no exam and no bloodwork. For applicants ages 50–85 with health conditions, we also offer guaranteed issue final expense policies that ask no health questions at all — approval is based on age and residency only.
Can you help Florida state employees and FSU/FAMU staff supplement FRS coverage?
Yes, and this is one of our most common Tallahassee requests. FRS group life coverage is often capped at one or two times salary, which may not cover a mortgage plus college costs. We compare affordable term life and IUL policies that fill the gap, and we coordinate the beneficiary designation so it works alongside your FRS benefits.
What's the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?
A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only sell that company's products. As an independent insurance agency, we're appointed with 8+ life carriers and 10 health carriers, so we can show you multiple options side by side. That's the entire premise of a life insurance agent comparison guide — and it's why clients often save on identical coverage.

Both. In-person appointments in downtown Tallahassee are available by request, and life insurance is licensed statewide across Florida.

Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health). Our agents complete annual continuing education, hold current AHIP Medicare certification with Fraud, Waste & Abuse training, and operate CMS-compliant Medicare marketing. Can you review a policy I already own from another agency?
Absolutely — and there's no cost for it. Our free Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies you purchased anywhere, including ones bought years ago at a higher rate. You call us first. Our agency handles the carrier calls, claim forms and paperwork on behalf of beneficiaries — we don't hand you a phone number and wish you luck. That claims advocacy is included for every client at no additional cost, for the entire life of the policy.

Yes. 1099 contractors, rideshare drivers, restaurant and hospitality workers, salon professionals and construction crews in Leon County often have no employer benefits at all. What if I find a lower rate somewhere else?
Tell us. Our rate-shopping guarantee says that if you find a lower premium for identical coverage from an equally rated carrier, we'll either match it or explain in writing exactly why the difference exists — for example, a different underwriting class, rider structure or benefit period.
