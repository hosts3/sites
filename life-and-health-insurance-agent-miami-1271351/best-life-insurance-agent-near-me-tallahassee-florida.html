Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Tallahassee, Florida

The questions we hear most from Big Bend families and business owners this year.

How much does life insurance cost in Tallahassee, Florida?

Rates depend on age, health and coverage amount. In 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in Tallahassee can often qualify for a $250,000 20-year term policy for about $18–$24 per month, while a healthy 45-year-old may pay roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term coverage. Final expense and burial policies for ages 45–85 typically range from $32 to $95 per month.

Are your consultations and quotes really free?

Yes — every quote, policy review and consultation is 100% free with no obligation and no hidden fees. Our agents are paid by the insurance carriers, never by the client. You are never asked to commit during your first appointment, and there is zero cost if you decide not to buy.

How fast can I get covered with a life insurance policy?

Guaranteed issue and final expense policies can be issued in as little as 24 to 48 hours with no medical exam. Fully underwritten term and whole life policies typically take 2 to 4 weeks, depending on how quickly medical records and labs come back from your physician. We track every case daily and push carriers for updates.

Do you help Tallahassee residents with Medicare at 65?

Absolutely. We are AHIP Medicare certified every year and CMS-compliant in our marketing and enrollment. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap Plans G, N and high-deductible F) and Part D prescription drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare, Molina and Doctor's Health Plan — at no cost to you.

What makes an independent insurance agent better than a captive agent?

A captive agent works for one carrier and can only offer that carrier's products. As an independent insurance agency, we represent 12+ A-rated carriers and can legally shop your case across all of them. That means better pricing on healthy cases, more options when you have health conditions, and an honest answer when one carrier declines you.

Can I get life insurance if I have diabetes, COPD or a past heart attack?

Often, yes. We work with carriers that specialize in impaired-risk underwriting, including guaranteed issue products for ages 50–85 that ask no health questions at all. During your free assessment we review your medications and diagnosis dates, then match you with the carrier most likely to approve you at the best rate class.

Do you offer no medical exam life insurance?

Yes. Many of our carriers offer accelerated underwriting or fully simplified issue policies with no exam, no blood draw and no fluids. Approvals can come back in 24 hours. Coverage amounts typically range from $25,000 to $500,000 depending on age and health history, with larger amounts available through traditional underwriting.

What is a free policy review, and do you charge for it?

It is a no-obligation review of your existing life, health or Medicare coverage — including policies you bought through another agent or employer. We check for overpricing, duplicate coverage, missing living benefit riders, incorrect beneficiaries and outdated guarantees. There is no charge, and there is no requirement to switch anything.

What credentials and licenses do you hold in Florida?

Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group has held Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only) since 2011. All agents complete annual continuing education, maintain AHIP Medicare certification, and the agency has been A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015. We are also NABIP members.

Are you available for urgent or same-day situations?

Yes. We keep phone lines open 24/7 and hold a 24-hour response guarantee on every quote request. Terminal illness, hospice and time-sensitive final expense cases are prioritized for same-day submission whenever the carrier's underwriting hours allow. Call +1-540-352-6249 and tell us it's urgent.

Do you sell auto insurance and home insurance too?

Our specialty is life insurance and health insurance along with Medicare, ACA Marketplace plans, annuities, disability income and small group employee benefits. For auto insurance quotes and home insurance, we can refer you to trusted independent property and casualty agencies in Tallahassee that we've worked with for years.

How do you handle a claim after I pass away?

Claims advocacy is included with every policy at no extra cost. When you pass away, your beneficiary calls +1-540-352-6249 and we contact the carrier directly, order the certified death certificate requirements, complete the claim forms and follow up weekly until the funds are released — typically within 2 to 6 weeks for a clean claim.

Do you offer service in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Sí — and wi. We provide bilingual phone and video support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. Several members of our staff speak all three languages, so you can discuss Medicare, ACA subsidies or underwriting questions in the language you're most comfortable with.
