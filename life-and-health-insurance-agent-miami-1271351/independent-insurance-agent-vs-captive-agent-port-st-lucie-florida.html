Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Port St. Lucie, Florida

The questions we hear most from Treasure Coast clients — answered straight, with real numbers.

What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent in Port St. Lucie, Florida?

A captive agent works for a single insurance company and can only sell that company's products at that company's rates. An independent agent is appointed with many carriers — in our case 18+, including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Mutual of Omaha and Transamerica — so we compare coverage and pricing side by side, then recommend what actually fits your budget. Same license, same state exam, dramatically more options.

Does it cost more to use an independent agent than to buy direct?

No — and this surprises most people. Insurance carriers pay our commission out of the premium they already charge, so the price is identical whether you enroll through us or directly with the carrier. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and ACA or KidCare enrollment help are all 100% free with no obligation.

How fast can I get a life insurance quote in Port St. Lucie?

Most term life quotes go out the same day, often within 45 minutes during business hours. Our written 24-hour response guarantee covers every quote request and client question. Once you apply, fully underwritten policies typically issue in 7–14 days, while no-exam final expense plans can be approved in as little as 24–48 hours.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. We write simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue final expense policies for ages 45–85 with no medical exam and no health questions on some plans, with coverage from $5,000 to $25,000 and premiums usually $32–$95 per month. Certain term and whole life products also offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for healthy applicants under 60.

What happens if I already have coverage through another agent?

Nothing bad — we'll review it for free, even though you didn't buy it from us. If your current policy is competitive, we'll tell you to keep it. If it isn't, we'll show you the numbers side by side. There's never any pressure to cancel, and we'll never ask you to drop coverage before a replacement policy is issued and in force.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Port St. Lucie?

Yes, and we're AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant. Many Medicare Advantage plans in St. Lucie County carry a $0 monthly premium, while Medigap Plan G generally runs $115–$210 depending on age. We verify your prescriptions and doctors against each plan's 2026 formulary before making any recommendation.

Can you help me find a cheaper ACA Marketplace plan?

Frequently, yes. Most St. Lucie County households qualify for premium tax credits, and we calculate your subsidy before comparing networks from Ambetter, Oscar, Molina, Cigna and Simply Healthcare. Many families we enroll end up paying under $50 per month after credits are applied. Our enrollment assistance costs you nothing.

We serve all of St. Lucie County — including St. Lucie West, Tradition, Riverland, PGA Village, Torino and Fort Pierce — plus Jensen Beach, Stuart, Palm City, Hobe Sound, Vero Beach, Sebastian and Okeechobee. There are no fees and there are no hidden costs. Carrier commissions fund our work, and every quote you receive shows the exact premium the carrier charges. If you ever find a lower rate for identical coverage, our rate-shopping guarantee means we'll match it or explain precisely why the difference exists.

What are the enrollment deadlines I should know for 2026?
