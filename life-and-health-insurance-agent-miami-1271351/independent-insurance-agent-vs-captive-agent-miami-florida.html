Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Miami, Florida

Straight answers from a licensed Miami agent — updated with 2026 carrier and enrollment information.

What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent in Miami, Florida?

A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only sell that company's products. An independent agent like Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group is appointed with 18 carriers — Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Mutual of Omaha, Transamerica and others — and compares them side by side. Independent agents are paid by the carrier, so your price is the same and you see the whole market instead of one brochure.

How much does life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?

Rates in Miami-Dade are close to national averages for most ages. A healthy 30-year-old typically pays about $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage, and a healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 monthly for $500,000. Final expense coverage runs about $32–$95 per month. Tobacco use, height/weight and medical history move those numbers, which is why we quote multiple carriers.

Do you charge a fee for your services or for quotes?

Never. Consultations, quotes, carrier comparisons, applications and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Our commission is paid directly by the insurance carrier and does not change your premium — buying through us costs the same as buying direct, but you get an advocate for the life of the policy.

How fast can I get covered?

ACA health plans and Medicare Advantage enrollments can often be effective the first of the following month. Final expense and guaranteed issue life policies are frequently approved the same day. Fully underwritten term life typically takes 2–4 weeks depending on the medical exam and carrier requirements. We respond to every quote request within 24 hours, usually under 45 minutes during business hours.

Can you help me enroll in Medicare Advantage, Medigap or Part D in Miami?

Yes. We are AHIP Medicare certified annually and CMS-compliant in our marketing and enrollment. We compare every Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plan available in your Miami-Dade ZIP code, verify your doctors and prescriptions, and confirm whether a $0 premium Advantage plan or a Plan G supplement (typically $115–$210 monthly here) fits you better.

Do I qualify for ACA subsidies or Obamacare savings in Miami-Dade County?

Most Miami-Dade households we enroll qualify for premium tax credits, and many pay under $50 per month after subsidies. Eligibility depends on household size and estimated 2026 income, not on assets. We run your numbers before you apply so there are no repayment surprises at tax time, and we help with Florida KidCare enrollment at no cost.

What if I have health problems — can I still get life insurance?

Very often, yes. Beyond fully underwritten policies, we offer simplified issue and guaranteed issue life insurance for ages 50–85 with no health questions at all. Depending on your conditions we may also place you with a carrier that treats diabetes, sleep apnea or past cardiac events more favorably. Individual carrier underwriting guides decide this, and independence is what lets us shop them.

Do you offer bilingual service in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Sí — and yes. Our team of 8 licensed agents and 3 support staff provides phone and in-person support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. This matters in Little Havana, Hialeah, Little Haiti and Sweetwater, where understanding policy exclusions and waiting periods in your own language prevents costly mistakes later.

What areas of South Florida do you serve?

Our primary service area is Miami-Dade County — Miami, Little Havana, Hialeah, Coral Gables, Kendall, Doral, Brickell, Little Haiti, Aventura, Miami Beach, Homestead, Cutler Bay, Palmetto Bay, Sweetwater and Westchester. We also serve Broward (Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Fort Lauderdale) and Monroe County, and life insurance is available statewide across Florida.

What happens if my claim is denied or my family needs to file?

Claims advocacy is included with every policy. We call the carrier on your behalf, gather and submit the required forms, track the claim status and follow up until the benefit is paid. We do this for beneficiaries too — one recent Coral Gables family received a $150,000 Mutual of Omaha benefit in 11 days with our office handling the paperwork.

Will you review a policy I already own from another agent?

Absolutely, and it is free. Our no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review examines your existing coverage — even a policy you purchased elsewhere — and checks the rate, the death benefit, the beneficiary designations, the conversion privileges and whether a newer carrier would approve you at a better class. If your current policy is already the best fit, we will tell you that.
