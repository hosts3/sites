How much does life insurance cost in Cape Coral, Florida?

Rates depend on your age, health history, and coverage amount. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in Cape Coral typically pays about $18 to $24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term life coverage. A healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58 to $75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense coverage generally runs $32 to $95 per month. We quote multiple carriers at once so you see the real spread, not a single company's rate.

Do you charge a fee for insurance consultations or quotes?

Never. Every consultation, quote, policy comparison, and annual review is completely free with zero obligation. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carriers, not by you, so you pay no service fees and no broker fees at any point in the process.

How fast will I get a quote after I call?

We guarantee a response within 24 hours on every quote request, and most Cape Coral clients receive a full multi-carrier comparison the same business day. Our average turnaround is under 4 business hours. During Medicare Annual Enrollment and ACA Open Enrollment, we extend hours and respond even faster.

Can you help me with Medicare if I live in Cape Coral?

Yes. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant, and we compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap), and Part D prescription drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, and additional carriers. Cape Coral clients can meet in person, by phone, or by video. We verify your physicians and medications are in-network before you enroll.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify for life insurance?

Not always. Many of the carriers we represent offer no-exam and accelerated underwriting programs that use prescription and medical database checks instead of a blood draw. Our Guaranteed Issue policies for ages 50 to 85 require no health questions at all, and final expense coverage for ages 45 to 85 also has no-medical-exam options.

What exactly is your rate-shopping guarantee?

If you find a lower rate for identical coverage — same carrier, same face amount, same term, same health class — from another licensed agent, we will match that rate or explain in plain language precisely why the difference exists. Because we are independent, we have no financial incentive to steer you toward one company.

How long does life insurance approval take?

Fully underwritten policies typically take 2 to 4 weeks from application to approval. Accelerated and no-exam policies are frequently approved in 24 to 72 hours. Final expense and guaranteed issue policies often issue within 1 to 2 weeks. We monitor every application daily and follow up with the carrier so nothing stalls.

Can you review a life insurance policy I already own?

Absolutely — and it costs you nothing. Our free Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies purchased anywhere, including from other agents or directly online. We check your coverage amount against your current mortgage, income, and debts, verify your beneficiary designations are current, review any riders, and confirm the pricing is still competitive in 2026.

Do you help with ACA Marketplace subsidies and Florida KidCare?

Yes. Most households in Lee County and Miami-Dade qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies are applied. We also provide completely free enrollment assistance for Florida KidCare. There is no charge for subsidy calculations or Marketplace enrollment help.

What happens if my beneficiaries need to file a claim?

We provide full claims advocacy at no cost. Our agency contacts the carrier, submits the required paperwork, orders any necessary documentation, and follows up on your family's behalf until the benefit is paid. You will never be handed a phone number and told to figure it out alone after a loss.

Are your agents licensed to sell insurance in Florida?

Yes. Every agent on our team holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and 2-40 (Health Insurance) — and completes annual continuing education plus AHIP Medicare certification. We have operated since 2011, are A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015, and hold a 4.9-star rating from 300+ client reviews.

How are you different from buying insurance through a website?

An online quote engine cannot explain underwriting classes, Medicare network rules, or how ACA subsidies are calculated for your household. It also cannot answer the phone at claim time. Our licensed agents teach you how coverage works before recommending anything, compare 18+ carriers, and stay with you for the life of the policy.
