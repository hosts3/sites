Updated for 2026. These are the questions Tampa Bay clients ask us most often — pricing, timelines, credentials and how coverage really works.

How much does life insurance cost in Tampa, Florida?

Rates depend on age, health and coverage amount. A healthy 30-year-old Tampa resident can usually get $250,000 of 20-year term life for about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old typically pays $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies run roughly $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage, and Guaranteed Issue options for ages 50–85 with no health questions start slightly higher.

Are your consultations and quotes really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation and no hidden fees. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by you, so you never pay for a consultation, a quote, a policy review or a beneficiary update. We also review policies you bought from another agency at no charge.

How fast can I get approved and covered?

Guaranteed issue and many final expense policies can be approved the same day you apply. Fully underwritten term life typically takes 2–4 weeks, and simplified-issue products often finish in 3–7 days. We guarantee a response to every quote request within 24 hours, and most Tampa clients hear back within 45 minutes during business hours.

Do I need a medical exam for life insurance?

Not always. For larger term and whole life policies, carriers usually require a paramedical exam and bloodwork. But our final expense, simplified issue and guaranteed issue products for ages 45–85 often require no exam and no health questions at all. We will tell you upfront which path fits your health history and budget.

What licenses and credentials do your agents hold?

Every agent on our team holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and/or License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). We complete annual continuing education and are AHIP Medicare + Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified each year, and our agency follows CMS-compliant Medicare marketing and enrollment rules. We are also A+ BBB accredited since 2015 and NABIP members.

Which insurance carriers do you represent in Tampa?

For health and Medicare we work with Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina Healthcare, Oscar Health, Doctor's Health Plan and Simply Healthcare. For life insurance we represent Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life. Because we are independent, we can compare all of them side by side.

Can you help me enroll in Medicare when I turn 65?

Absolutely. We help Tampa residents compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans against their actual doctors and prescriptions. Many Medicare Advantage plans available in Hillsborough County have a $0 monthly premium, while Medigap Plan G typically ranges from $115 to $210 per month depending on age. Our free bilingual Medicare workshop runs the first Wednesday of each month.

What if I already have life insurance — can you still help?

Yes, and this is one of our most popular free services. We perform a no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review that checks whether your current premium is competitive, whether your death benefit still matches your mortgage and income needs, and whether your beneficiaries are correctly named. How do you help my family if a claim is filed?

We act as your advocate. Our agency handles the carrier calls, submits the required paperwork and follows up until the claim is paid — your beneficiaries never have to navigate a call center alone. This claims support is included with every policy at no additional cost, whether the policy was issued in 2011 or 2026.

Do you offer emergency or same-day appointments?

We keep same-day and next-day slots open for urgent situations, including hospital discharge planning, terminal diagnoses and time-sensitive Medicare enrollment deadlines. Our phone line at +1-540-352-6249 is answered 24/7, and evening video appointments are available Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15–Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1–Jan 15).

Bring it to us. Our rate-shopping guarantee means that if you find a lower premium for identical coverage, we will either match it or explain in writing exactly why the two policies differ — different health class, benefit period, rider structure or carrier rating. We would rather earn your long-term trust than win a single sale.
