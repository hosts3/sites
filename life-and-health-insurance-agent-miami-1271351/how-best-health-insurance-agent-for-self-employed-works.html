A skilled agent helps you project income, choose a benchmark silver plan that maximizes cost-sharing reductions, and avoid repaying thousands at tax time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a health insurance agent for self-employed workers cost me?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, Marketplace enrollments, Medicare enrollments and annual policy reviews are 100% free — no fees, no obligation. Agents are compensated by the insurance carrier, not by the client. If anyone asks you for a fee to enroll in an ACA or Medicare plan, walk away.

How does an agent estimate my ACA subsidy if my income changes during the year?

The Marketplace uses your projected MAGI — for a sole proprietor, that's generally Schedule C net profit, not gross revenue. Your agent builds the projection from last year's return plus what you expect this year, then flags the 400% federal poverty level threshold so you can report changes within 30 days. It is not tax advice, so loop in your CPA before filing.

Can I get health insurance if I'm a 1099 gig driver in Miami?

Yes. Rideshare, delivery and independent contractor income qualifies you for the ACA Marketplace, and most Miami-Dade applicants qualify for premium tax credits. Many households end up paying under $50 per month. You'll need your 1099s, last year's return, and a reasonable projection of this year's net income.

Do I need life insurance if I'm self-employed and single?

It depends on who absorbs your debts and final expenses. If you have a mortgage, business loan, co-signed debt or family relying on your income, term coverage is inexpensive relative to the risk — often $18–$24 per month for $250,000 at age 30. If you have genuinely no dependents and no shared debt, a policy may not be a priority, and a good agent will tell you so.

When should a self-employed person in Miami start Medicare planning?

Six months before your 65th birthday. Your Initial Enrollment Period begins three months before your birthday month, and since you have no employer plan to grant a Special Enrollment Period, missing it can trigger permanent Part B and Part D late-enrollment penalties. Medicare supplement options are also easiest to obtain during that first window.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Photo ID, Social Security numbers for everyone applying, last year's tax return or Schedule C, current 1099s, a list of doctors and prescriptions with dosages, and any existing policy documents. If you're reviewing Medicare, bring your red, white and blue Medicare card. Appointments are available at the Little Havana and Kendall offices or by video.

How fast can my coverage start?

ACA Marketplace coverage generally starts the first of the month after enrollment (or two months out if you enroll late in the month). Life insurance is not instant — fully underwritten term policies commonly take two to six weeks, while guaranteed issue and some final expense products can be issued in days without a medical exam.

I already bought a policy somewhere else. Will you still review it?

Yes. The free Life Insurance Policy Review and annual health plan check include policies purchased elsewhere, and there's no obligation to switch. If your current plan is genuinely better for your situation, you'll be told that directly — the rate-shopping guarantee means the agency matches a lower identical-coverage rate or explains exactly why the difference exists.
