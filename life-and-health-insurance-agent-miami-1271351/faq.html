How much does a life and health insurance agent cost in Miami?

What does a life and health insurance agent actually do for me?
A licensed agent compares plans across multiple carriers — we work with 10+ including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Mutual of Omaha and Transamerica — explains how underwriting and ACA subsidies really work, submits your application, corrects errors before they cause a denial, and then advocates for you at claim time, including handling carrier calls for beneficiaries.
How quickly can I get a life insurance quote in Miami?
Most Miami clients receive a personalized term life quote within 24 hours, and often the same business day. Call +1-540-352-6249 with your age, coverage amount and general health history, and we will send side-by-side carrier comparisons. Do I need a medical exam to qualify for life insurance?
Not always. Healthy applicants under 50 usually get the lowest rates through fully underwritten coverage, which may include a paramedical exam and bloodwork. Our final expense and guaranteed issue products for ages 45 to 85 offer no-medical-exam and even no-health-question options for applicants managing diabetes, COPD or prior cardiac events.
When can I enroll in Medicare in Miami-Dade County?
Your Initial Enrollment Period is the seven months around your 65th birthday (three months before, your birthday month, and three months after). The Annual Enrollment Period runs October 15 – December 7 every year, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs January 1 – March 31. We offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Annual Enrollment.
Can self-employed and 1099 workers in Miami get ACA Marketplace coverage?
Yes, and this is one of our most common requests. Gig drivers, salon workers, restaurant staff and 1099 contractors across Miami-Dade frequently qualify for premium tax credits based on estimated household income. Many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies, and our ACA and Florida KidCare enrollment assistance costs you nothing.
What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap?
Medicare Advantage (Part C) frequently has a $0 monthly premium but uses a provider network and per-service copays. Medicare Supplement Plan G carries a monthly premium — typically $115 to $210 in Miami-Dade depending on age — but lets you see any provider who accepts Medicare, with highly predictable out-of-pocket costs. We run both scenarios against your actual prescriptions and doctors before you choose.
How much does term life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?
Current 2026 sample rates for healthy non-smokers: a 30-year-old can secure $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month, and a 45-year-old can secure $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 per month. Final expense insurance for ages 45–85 runs $32–$95 monthly depending on age and the $5,000–$25,000 benefit amount you select.
