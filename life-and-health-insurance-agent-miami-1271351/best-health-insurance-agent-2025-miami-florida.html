What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

An independent insurance agent like Biscayne Bay Life & Health represents many carriers — Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Mutual of Omaha, Transamerica and others — so we can compare rates and benefits side by side. A captive agent can only sell products from one company, which limits your options and often your savings.

Nearly 70% of Miami-Dade residents speak a language other than English at home, and a huge share of our workforce — restaurant staff, salon professionals, rideshare drivers, contractors and 1099 consultants — has no employer-sponsored benefits at all.
