We typically deliver term life quotes within 2 hours during business hours. Fully underwritten policies usually take 2–4 weeks, while no-exam and simplified-issue plans can be approved in as little as 24–72 hours. Guaranteed-issue final expense policies for ages 50–85 with no health questions can sometimes be issued the same day.

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a private network plan, often with a $0 premium but copays and prior authorizations. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) pays alongside Original Medicare, has higher monthly premiums but nearly no copays and lets you see any Medicare-accepting provider nationwide. We show you both side by side so the trade-off is clear.
