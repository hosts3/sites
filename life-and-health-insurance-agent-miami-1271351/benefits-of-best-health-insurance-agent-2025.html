Frequently Asked Questions

Do I pay anything for an insurance agent's help?

No. Consultations, quotes, enrollment help, and annual policy reviews are free. Agents are compensated by the insurance carrier after a policy is issued, so your premium is the same whether you enroll alone or with an agent's help. In Florida, an agent cannot charge you a separate fee for Medicare or ACA Marketplace enrollment.

Is an independent agent better than calling a carrier directly?

An independent agent represents you across 10+ carriers rather than one brand. That means plan comparisons, underwriting pre-screening, and claims advocacy from someone whose job is matching you to the right policy — not defending a single product line. If you find a lower rate for identical coverage, a good agency will match it or explain the difference in writing.

When is the 2025 enrollment window in Florida?

Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 – December 7, 2025. ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 – January 15, 2026. Outside those windows you generally need a qualifying life event — marriage, birth, job loss, or a move — to enroll. Medicare Advantage enrollees may also use the Open Enrollment Period from January 1 – March 31 to switch or drop a plan.

What does term life insurance cost in Miami in 2025?

Typical starting rates: a healthy 30-year-old can get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage from roughly $18–$24 per month; a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term from roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 generally runs $32–$95 monthly. Tobacco use, driving record, and family health history all move the number.

Can I get life insurance if I have a health condition?

Usually yes — it just depends on which carrier. Some insurers specialize in diabetes, COPD, or prior cancer histories at standard rates. If traditional underwriting declines you, guaranteed-issue life insurance for ages 50–85 requires no health questions at all, though most policies apply a graded death benefit during the first two years.

What are the benefits of best health insurance agent 2025 Life and Health Insurance agent relationships for self-employed workers?

Self-employed workers, gig drivers, and 1099 contractors have no employer benefits department. An agent fills that gap: ACA subsidy calculations, disability income coverage to protect earning power, term life to cover business debt, and a single contact for claims. It is one of the highest-value services available to Miami's independent workforce.

Do I need to meet in person, or can everything be done by phone?

Either works. Offices in Little Havana and Kendall are open Monday–Friday 8:30 AM–6:30 PM and Saturday by appointment; evening video appointments run Monday–Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare and ACA open enrollment. Life insurance is licensed statewide across Florida, so relatives in other counties can be served virtually.

How do I verify an agent's credentials?

Ask for the Florida Department of Financial Services license number — a 2-15 license covers life, health, and annuities; a 2-40 covers health only. You can verify any Florida license on the DFS website in under a minute. Reputable agencies also publish their BBB rating, review counts, and carrier appointments openly.
