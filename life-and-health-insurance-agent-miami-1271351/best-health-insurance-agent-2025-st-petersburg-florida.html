Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in St. Petersburg, Florida

Updated for 2026 — including the current ACA Open Enrollment window (November 1 – January 15) and Medicare Annual Enrollment (October 15 – December 7).

How much does it cost to work with a health and life insurance agent in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Nothing — consultations, quotes and annual policy reviews are 100% free to you. Agents are compensated directly by the insurance carriers, so your premium is identical whether you enroll through us or purchase direct. You simply get an advocate at no extra cost, plus help with claims and appeals.

How fast can I get a life insurance quote in St. Petersburg?

Most clients receive side-by-side quotes from 6–10 carriers within 24 hours, and often the same business day. Fully underwritten policies usually issue in 2–4 weeks, while no-exam final expense policies typically issue in 3–7 days depending on the carrier.

Do you charge a fee for ACA Marketplace or Medicare enrollment help?

No. Florida KidCare, ACA Marketplace and Medicare enrollment assistance is completely free. Many Florida households qualify for premium tax credits that bring a Marketplace plan under $50 per month, and many Medicare Advantage plans in Pinellas County carry a $0 monthly premium.

Can you review a policy I already own from another agent?

Yes — and it is free. Our no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies from any carrier or agency. We check death benefit adequacy, beneficiary designations, rate class, riders and cash value performance, then tell you plainly whether switching makes financial sense.

What licenses and credentials do your agents hold in Florida?

Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) or 2-40 (Health), completes annual continuing education, and is AHIP Medicare plus Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified each year. We are CMS-compliant for Medicare marketing and enrollment.

Do you offer bilingual insurance services in St. Petersburg?

Yes. Our team serves clients in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole by phone, video and in person. Bilingual support staff are available during all office hours — which matters when you are comparing benefit summaries, drug formularies or underwriting questionnaires.

How quickly do you respond to quote requests and client questions?

We guarantee a response within 24 hours on every request, and our average callback time is under 4 hours during business hours. Existing clients also have 24/7 access to our claims and billing line at +1-540-352-6249.

What happens if I need help filing a claim or appealing a denial?

We handle the carrier calls, forms and follow-ups for you. Our team has supported hundreds of Florida families through life claims, Medicare appeals, prior authorizations and prescription exceptions — you will never have to fight a carrier alone.

What if I find a lower rate for the same coverage somewhere else?

Our rate-shopping guarantee states we will either match the lower rate or explain exactly why the difference exists — different underwriting class, benefit period, deductible or exclusion. You will never be pressured into keeping an inferior policy.

Do I need a medical exam for life insurance in Florida?

Not always. Healthy applicants under 50 can often qualify for up to $1,000,000 in term coverage through accelerated underwriting with no exam. Guaranteed Issue life insurance is available to ages 50–85 with no health questions at all, though face amounts are lower and pricing is higher.

Which insurance carriers do you represent in Pinellas County?

Health and Medicare: Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina Healthcare, Oscar Health, Doctors Health Plan and Simply Healthcare. Life: Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, Transamerica, John Hancock, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life.

Can you help small businesses in St. Petersburg with employee benefits?

Yes. We build small group benefits for businesses with 5 to 50 employees, including medical, dental, vision, life and disability options. Group quotes typically take 3 to 5 business days, and we handle carrier paperwork, enrollment meetings and year-round service at no cost to the employer.
