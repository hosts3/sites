What is the difference between a Medicare Advantage plan and a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a private network plan — many cost $0 per month but use copays and networks. Medigap supplements Original Medicare and lets you see nearly any provider nationwide, with Plan G typically running $115–$210 per month in South Florida. We model both against your actual doctors and prescriptions before recommending anything.
