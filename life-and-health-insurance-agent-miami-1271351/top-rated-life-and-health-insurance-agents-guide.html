What to Look For in a Top-Rated Life & Health Insurance Agent

1. Independence vs. Captivity: The Single Biggest Factor

An independent agent contracts with many carriers and can place you with the one that fits your health history, budget, and goals. A captive agent works for one brand and can only sell that brand's products. When you search for the top rated life and health insurance agents in Miami, you are almost always looking for independent — because independence is what creates real choice.

Expert Tips & Mistakes to Avoid

  • Never buy life insurance without a carrier comparison. The same $500,000 policy can differ by hundreds of dollars a year between carriers based on your build, blood pressure, and family history.
  • Do not cancel a Medicare Supplement to "save money" on Advantage without checking whether your doctors are in-network next year. Provider networks change every January.
  • Name real human beneficiaries, not your estate. Naming "estate" can push proceeds through probate and cost your family months of delay.
  • Re-report your income to the Marketplace. ACA subsidies in Miami are based on projected income — a raise, a new gig, or a lost job must be reported within 30 days.
  • Ask for the graded benefit disclosure in writing before buying guaranteed issue or final expense coverage.
  • Review every policy annually. Rates change, health changes, and a free annual review catches overpayments most people never notice.
  • Work with someone who answers the phone in a crisis. The value of an agent shows up on the worst day, not the day you sign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does using an agent cost more than buying insurance online?

No. Agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, and premiums are filed with the state, so the price is the same whether you buy direct or through an agent. What changes is that you get a licensed professional who compares multiple carriers, handles underwriting, and advocates for your beneficiaries at claim time.

How much life insurance do I need in Miami?

A common starting point is 10–15 times your annual income, adjusted for mortgage balance, dependents, and existing savings. A Miami household earning $60,000 with a $300,000 mortgage typically needs $600,000–$750,000. A free needs analysis takes about 20 minutes and costs nothing.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. Simplified issue and guaranteed issue policies are available, including coverage for ages 50–85 with no health questions. Final expense plans for ages 45–85 also offer no-exam options. Rates are higher than fully underwritten term, so it pays to compare both paths.

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Miami-Dade?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a private network plan — many carry $0 monthly premiums in Miami but use provider networks and prior authorizations. Medigap (Medicare Supplement) pays alongside Original Medicare with no networks; Plan G rates in Miami-Dade typically run $115–$210 per month. The right choice depends on your doctors, travel plans, and prescriptions.

Do I qualify for an ACA subsidy as a self-employed worker in Miami?

Most Miami-Dade households do qualify for premium tax credits based on projected household income and family size. Many self-employed clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies. Enrollment assistance, including income projection help, is provided at no cost.

What if I already have a policy I bought years ago?

You should still get a free policy review. Agencies routinely find outdated beneficiaries, duplicate coverage, and rate classes that no longer fit. A review is free even if the policy was not purchased through that agency, and there is never an obligation to switch.

How fast can coverage start?

Health and Medicare plans usually take effect the first of the following month after enrollment. Fully underwritten life policies can take 2–6 weeks; simplified and guaranteed issue policies often issue in 24–72 hours. ACA plans have defined open enrollment and special enrollment windows.

Yes. Full service is available in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole — in-person at the Little Havana and Kendall offices, by phone, or by video appointment anywhere in Florida for life insurance.
