Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Orlando, Florida

Straight answers to the questions young Orlando adults ask us most. Still unsure? Call +1-540-352-6249 — we're available 24/7.

How much does life insurance cost for a young adult in Orlando, Florida?

Rates are based on age, health and coverage amount. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Orlando resident can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies run $32–$95 per month. Every quote we provide is free and specific to your health profile.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify for a policy?

Often, no. Many of the carriers we're appointed with use accelerated underwriting, which means no blood draw and no exam for applicants between 18 and 55. You answer a short health questionnaire by phone, and a decision frequently comes back within 24 to 48 hours. Larger face amounts or complex health histories may still require an exam.

Am I too young to actually need life insurance?

If anyone relies on your income — a spouse, a child, or even a co-signer on a student loan — the answer is usually no, you're not too young. Buying at 25 or 30 locks in a much lower rate for the entire term. Waiting until 40 can double or triple the premium for the exact same coverage.

What is the best life insurance policy for young adults in Orlando, Florida — term or whole life?

For most young adults, a 20 or 30-year level term policy delivers the most death benefit for the lowest premium. If you want lifelong coverage, tax-deferred cash value growth, or a policy that stays in force after the term ends, an Indexed Universal Life or whole life policy may fit better. We show you both side by side so you can compare total cost honestly.

How long does it take to get approved and get my policy?

No-exam and simplified issue applications are often approved the same day or within 48 hours, with the policy delivered electronically or by mail within a week. Fully underwritten policies that require medical records typically take two to four weeks. Our team follows up with the carrier on your behalf so your file never sits idle.

I'm a gig worker or self-employed. Can I still qualify?

Absolutely. Self-employed workers, 1099 contractors, rideshare drivers and hospitality employees make up a large share of our Orlando clients. Because you have no employer group benefits, an individual life policy plus optional disability income coverage is often the only protection protecting your paycheck and your family.

Do you charge a fee for consultations or quotes?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy comparisons and annual policy reviews are all completely free. Agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you, so your premium is identical whether you work with us or apply directly. There is no obligation at any point in the process.

What information do I need handy to apply?

You'll need a government-issued photo ID, your Social Security number, your current address, the name and date of birth of each beneficiary, a rough estimate of your annual income, and a list of any prescription medications you take. That's usually enough for us to run accurate quotes during the very first call.

Can I get coverage if I have a health condition?

In many cases, yes. Carriers we represent offer simplified issue, guaranteed issue and final expense products designed for people with diabetes, high blood pressure, prior cancer history or other conditions. Guaranteed issue coverage for ages 50–85 requires no health questions at all, though face amounts are smaller and premiums are higher.

What happens if my beneficiary needs to file a claim?

We act as claims advocates. Our agency makes the carrier calls, gathers the required documents and tracks the claim until it's paid — so your family isn't navigating paperwork during an already difficult time. We've supported claims for clients across Miami-Dade and Central Florida since 2011.

You're based in Miami — how do you serve Orlando clients?

Our Florida 2-15 Life, Health & Annuity license allows us to write life insurance for clients anywhere in the state, and health and Medicare appointments are handled virtually. Orlando clients get the same licensed agents, the same 24-hour response guarantee and the same carrier lineup — by phone, secure video, or in person when we're in Central Florida.

Can I bundle life insurance with health or Medicare coverage?

Yes, and it's often worth doing. We write ACA Marketplace plans, Florida KidCare, Medicare Advantage, Medigap and Part D, plus term, whole life and IUL policies in the same agency. Coordinating your life and health coverage through one licensed agent means one point of contact for every annual review and enrollment period.
