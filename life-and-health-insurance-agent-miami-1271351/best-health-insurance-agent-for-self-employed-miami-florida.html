Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Miami, Florida

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from self-employed workers, families and retirees across Miami-Dade. Still unsure? Call +1-540-352-6249 — available 24/7.

How much does it cost to work with an insurance agent in Miami, Florida?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by clients, so the premium you pay is identical whether you enroll alone or with our help. This is standard practice for independent agents — anyone charging you a fee for an ACA or Medicare quote should be questioned. Call +1-540-352-6249 to schedule a no-pressure appointment.

What's the best health insurance for self-employed people in Miami, Florida?

For most 1099 workers, gig drivers and freelancers under 65, an ACA Marketplace plan is the strongest value because self-employment income frequently qualifies for premium tax credits — many Miami-Dade households pay under $50 per month after subsidies. If you are 65 or older, Medicare Advantage or a Medigap Plan G usually wins. We compare Florida Blue, Ambetter, Oscar Health, Molina, UnitedHealthcare and Cigna side by side before recommending anything.

How quickly can I get covered by a health insurance plan in Miami?

ACA Marketplace applications submitted by the 15th of the month typically start on the 1st of the following month. During Open Enrollment (November 1 – January 15) start dates follow the same cutoff. Medicare Advantage and Part D elections made during Annual Enrollment (October 15 – December 7) activate January 1. Special Enrollment Periods for marriage, a move, job loss or a new baby can start coverage within 15 days of approval.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance?

Not always. Term life policies under roughly $250,000 often use accelerated underwriting — just health questions plus prescription and database checks, with no blood draw. Our final expense and guaranteed issue products for ages 50 to 85 require no health questions at all. If you have diabetes, high blood pressure or a prior cancer diagnosis, we shop impaired-risk carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Americo and Gerber Life to find realistic approval odds.

Can you help me qualify for ACA subsidies or Florida KidCare?

Yes — and it is one of the most valuable things we do. We calculate projected 2026 household income, household size and county so your premium tax credit is accurate. Overestimating income is the single most common reason Miami families overpay by hundreds of dollars a month. We also complete Florida KidCare enrollment at no cost to you, including the application and income documentation review.

I'm turning 65 — do you help with Medicare enrollment in Miami?

Absolutely. Our founder is AHIP Medicare certified every year and CMS compliant for marketing and enrollment. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement Plan G and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and Doctor's Health Plan. Plan G rates in Miami-Dade typically run $115–$210 per month depending on age, while many Advantage plans carry a $0 monthly premium. We verify your doctors and prescriptions before you enroll.

What happens if I need to file a claim?

We handle it for you. Our claims advocacy service means we call the carrier, submit paperwork and follow the case through resolution — including death claims for beneficiaries, who are often grieving and unfamiliar with the process. There is no charge for this, ever, and we respond to every claim question within 24 hours. Learn more on our services page.

Do you charge fees, hidden costs or markups on premiums?

No. Carrier commissions are already built into published rates, so using an independent agent costs you nothing extra. We also offer a rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, we will match it or explain in writing exactly why the difference exists. We quote real numbers, not teaser rates that disappear at underwriting.

Life: Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life. Can you help a small Miami business with employee benefits?
