Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Port St. Lucie, Florida

How much does life insurance cost in Port St. Lucie, Florida?

Rates are based on age, health and coverage amount, not your ZIP code. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old can often get $250,000 of 20-year term for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense coverage typically runs $32–$95 per month.

Are consultations and quotes really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation. We never charge a consultation fee, a policy fee, or a service fee. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carriers, so our advice costs you nothing and you are never asked to commit during the first appointment.

How long does it take to get approved and covered?

Fully underwritten term and whole life policies usually issue in 7 to 14 days, and many accelerated programs finish in 24 to 72 hours. Guaranteed-issue final expense plans often issue within 3 to 5 business days. We track every application and keep you updated until your policy is delivered.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify?

Not always. Many carriers now offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for healthy applicants under 60, using prescription history and a short phone interview instead. Guaranteed-issue final expense policies for ages 50–85 require no medical exam and no health questions at all.

What if I have a health condition like diabetes or high blood pressure?

You still have strong options. We work with carriers that specialize in impaired-risk underwriting, including Mutual of Omaha, Americo, Foresters and Gerber Life. Many clients with controlled diabetes or hypertension are approved at standard or table rates — and we always shop the guaranteed-issue route as a backup.

Can you help me during Medicare Annual Enrollment?

Absolutely. Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7, and ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15. During those windows we offer extended evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM and same-week enrollment help for St. Lucie County residents.

Which insurance carriers do you work with?

We are appointed with 18+ carriers, including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health and Simply Healthcare for health and Medicare, plus Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life for life insurance.

Your office is in Miami — do you actually serve Port St. Lucie?

Yes. Our agency holds active Florida licenses and serves clients statewide. Life insurance is fully available across Florida, and we meet Treasure Coast clients in person by appointment or by secure video conference. Reach us at +1-540-352-6249 to schedule.

What is final expense insurance and do I qualify?

Final expense (burial) insurance is a smaller whole life policy from $5,000 to $25,000 designed to cover funeral costs, medical bills and debts so your family isn't burdened. It's available to ages 45–85, with simplified and guaranteed-issue options where no health questions are asked.

Can you help me qualify for ACA subsidies or Florida KidCare?

Yes, and there is no cost for that help. Most Port St. Lucie households qualify for premium tax credits through the federal Marketplace, with many paying under $50 per month after subsidies. We also complete Florida KidCare applications and renewals for free.

What happens if my family needs to file a claim?

You call us first. Our agency handles the carrier calls, forms and follow-up on your beneficiary's behalf, which typically shortens the process. Beneficiary claims for final expense policies are commonly paid within 5 to 10 business days of a complete submission. Claims support is included with every policy we place.

Are you licensed, credentialed and insured?

Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40. Our agents complete annual continuing education, AHIP Medicare certification, and Fraud, Waste & Abuse training. We are A+ BBB accredited (since 2015), NABIP members, and carry errors and omissions coverage.
