Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Orlando, Florida

Straight answers to the questions our Orlando clients ask most often. Still unsure? Call and ask us directly.

How much does term life insurance cost in Orlando, Florida in 2026?

A healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old looking for $500,000 of 20-year term usually pays between $58 and $75 per month. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use, driving record and the carrier — which is exactly why we quote multiple companies side by side.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance?

Not always. Many carriers now offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for healthy applicants under 60 who qualify, and decisions can come back in 24 to 72 hours. We also offer guaranteed issue final expense coverage for ages 50 to 85 with no health questions at all — approval is based on age and coverage amount only.

How do I choose the best life insurance agent near me in Orlando, Florida?

Look for three things: an independent agent who represents multiple carriers, an active Florida Department of Financial Services license (2-15 for life and health), and written quotes from more than one company. Biscayne Bay Life & Health works with 8+ carriers, has been licensed since 2011, and offers a free annual policy review with no obligation.

How long does life insurance approval take?

Accelerated, no-exam policies can be approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten policies requiring labs, an exam and medical records typically take 2 to 4 weeks. We give every Orlando client a status update at least twice a week until the policy is issued, so you are never left wondering.

Do you charge a fee for your insurance services?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are paid by the insurance carrier — never by the client. That means our advice costs you nothing, whether you buy a policy or not.

Can you help me enroll in Medicare Advantage in Orlando?

Yes. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant, and we represent Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and Simply Healthcare. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Orange County are available at a $0 monthly premium, and Medicare Supplement Plan G rates in Florida typically range from $115 to $210 per month.

I’m self-employed with no employer benefits. What are my options?

Self-employed workers, gig-economy drivers and 1099 contractors in Orlando commonly qualify for ACA Marketplace plans with premium tax credits — many households pay under $50 per month after subsidies. We also pair health coverage with individual term life and disability income insurance for full protection.

Can I get life insurance if I have health issues?

Often yes. We work with carriers that accept controlled diabetes, high blood pressure, elevated BMI and past cancer history, and we know which ones price those conditions most fairly. If you have been declined elsewhere, guaranteed issue and final expense policies from Gerber Life and Americo are available for ages 50 to 85.

What happens if my claim is denied?

We advocate for you at no cost. Our agency handles the carrier calls, paperwork and follow-up on behalf of beneficiaries. We also review beneficiary designations annually so claims are paid quickly and to the right person — that single step prevents more delays than any other.

How much life insurance do I actually need?

A common guideline is 10 to 15 times your annual income, plus your mortgage balance and future education costs, minus existing savings and any group coverage you already have. We run a written needs analysis with every client so you can see the math before you decide on a coverage amount.

Can you review a policy I bought from another agent?

Absolutely — and it is free. Our policy review covers policies you did not purchase through us. We check the death benefit, premium, cash value, riders and beneficiaries, then tell you in plain language whether you are overpaying or underinsured. There is no obligation to change anything.

Do you offer service in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Yes. Three of our support staff and several of our eight licensed agents serve clients in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. Video and phone appointments are available statewide, so language is never a barrier to understanding your coverage.
