How much does life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?

A healthy 30-year-old Miami resident typically pays $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage. A healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies for ages 45 to 85 generally range from $32 to $95 per month. We quote 8 life carriers side by side at no cost, so you can compare real numbers instead of estimates.

Do you charge for insurance consultations or quotes?

Never. Consultations, quotes, policy comparisons and annual reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by you, which means your premium is identical whether you enroll through our agency or directly with the carrier — except you get an advocate on your side.

How fast can I get covered in Miami?

It depends on the product. Final expense and guaranteed issue policies are frequently approved the same day. No-exam accelerated underwriting life policies usually issue in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term life takes 2 to 5 weeks. ACA Marketplace plans enrolled during Open Enrollment start the first of the following month.

Do I need a medical exam for life insurance?

Not always. We represent carriers offering no-exam accelerated underwriting up to $1,000,000 for qualified applicants, and guaranteed issue final expense coverage for ages 50 to 85 with no health questions whatsoever. We review your health history privately and match you with the carrier most likely to approve you at the best rate.

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement in Miami?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a network-based plan — many are $0 monthly premium with copays for visits and prescriptions. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) Plan G works alongside Original Medicare, keeps your doctors nationwide, and typically costs $115 to $210 per month in Miami-Dade with very few out-of-pocket costs. We compare both side by side at no charge.

Can you review a policy I already own from another agency?

Yes, and it is completely free — including policies you did not purchase through us.
