How much does life insurance cost in Cape Coral, Florida?

Rates depend on age, health and coverage amount. As a benchmark, a healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally run $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. Florida's tobacco use, height/weight and driving record all affect your final rate.

Does it cost anything to work with an independent insurance agent in Cape Coral?

No — it is completely free to you. Carriers pay our commission after a policy is issued, so consultations, quotes, subsidy calculations and your annual policy review cost $0. We never charge a fee, and there is no obligation to purchase anything after an appointment.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Fully underwritten term, whole life and IUL policies usually issue in 7 to 21 days, depending on how quickly medical records arrive. We track every application and update you at each underwriting stage.

Yes. Guaranteed issue and final expense products for ages 50–85 require no blood work and no health questions.
