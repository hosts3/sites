Frequently Asked Questions About Life Insurance Agents in St. Petersburg, Florida

Straight answers to the questions our St. Petersburg clients ask most — before they ever sign anything.

How much does life insurance cost in St. Petersburg, Florida in 2026?

Pricing is based on age, health, coverage amount and term length. As a benchmark: a healthy 30-year-old can get $250,000 of 20-year term for approximately $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 typically runs $32–$95 monthly for $5,000–$25,000 of benefit. Your free quote is exact, not an estimate.

Do you charge a fee for consultations or quotes?

Never. Consultations, quotes, comparisons and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Insurance carriers pay our commission directly, so your premium is the same whether you buy through us or apply on your own. You will never receive an invoice from our agency.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Fully underwritten term and permanent policies typically issue in 2 to 4 weeks from application. Many clients qualify for accelerated underwriting with a decision in 24 to 72 hours and no blood draw. Guaranteed issue and final expense policies can be approved the same day, with coverage starting once the first premium is paid.

How do I know which life insurance agent reviews in St. Petersburg, Florida are trustworthy?

Look for specifics, not slogans. Verified reviews mention concrete numbers — savings amounts, approval timelines, response times — and name the agent. Check the reviewer has a real Florida city and ZIP, confirm the agency's license through the Florida Department of Financial Services, and make sure the agency is independent rather than tied to one carrier. Our own 317 reviews average 4.9 stars and are publicly viewable.

Can you help me with Medicare when I turn 65?

Yes. We are AHIP-certified annually and CMS-compliant. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna and Cigna. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Pinellas County carry a $0 monthly premium, and Plan G Medigap rates typically range from $115 to $210 per month depending on age.

What if I have health issues — can I still qualify?

Usually, yes. Different carriers treat diabetes, high blood pressure, sleep apnea, and past cancer history very differently, which is exactly why an independent agent matters. We screen your conditions against 20+ carriers to find the best offer. For ages 50–85 with serious health conditions, our guaranteed issue life insurance requires no health questions at all.

Do you offer life insurance with no medical exam?

Yes. We place accelerated-underwriting term life, simplified issue whole life, and guaranteed issue final expense plans with no physical, no blood draw and no fluids. Approval is generally based on a short health questionnaire plus prescription and MIB database checks, and decisions often arrive within 24 to 72 hours.

How do ACA Marketplace subsidies work in Florida this year?

Premium tax credits are based on your household income and family size relative to the federal poverty level. Most Pinellas County households qualify, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies. We help you project income accurately so you don't face a subsidy repayment at tax time, and we handle Florida KidCare enrollment at no cost.

Are you licensed to serve St. Petersburg if your main office is in Miami?

Absolutely. Our agents hold active Florida Life, Health & Annuity licenses (2-15) and Health licenses (2-40), and our life insurance appointments are valid statewide — including Pinellas County. St. Petersburg clients work with us by phone, secure video, or by scheduled in-person appointment at a location near you.

What happens at claim time — do you actually help?

Yes, and it's free. We call the carrier on your beneficiary's behalf, confirm exactly which documents are required, follow up until the claim is reviewed, and track the payout timeline. We advocate for families on policies we sold — and, on request, on policies we didn't. In one recent Pinellas claim, a $100,000 benefit was paid in 11 days.

Do you offer coverage for small businesses?

We place small group employee benefits for companies with 5 to 50 employees, including group health, dental, vision and voluntary life. We model true cost per employee, handle carrier paperwork, and run open enrollment on-site or by video with bilingual materials in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

Can you review a policy I already own?

Yes — and this is one of our most requested services. Bring any policy, including one purchased from another agency or directly from a carrier. We will verify the death benefit, check the beneficiary designations, compare current rates on identical coverage, and tell you honestly whether keeping it or replacing it makes more sense. No charge, no obligation.

What if I find a lower rate somewhere else?

We offer a rate-shopping guarantee. If you find a lower rate for identical coverage from a carrier we're appointed with, we will match it — or explain in plain terms exactly why the policies differ. Getting the right coverage matters more to us than winning a price contest, so we'll tell you when to walk away from a quote, including ours.
