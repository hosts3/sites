Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent Services in Tampa, Florida

Updated for 2026. These are the questions our Tampa clients ask most often — answered directly, without sales language.

How much does a life insurance agent cost me in Tampa, Florida?

Nothing. Our consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are completely free, and agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier — not by you. You pay the same premium whether you buy directly from a carrier or through our agency, but you get an advocate who shops 18 carriers on your behalf.

How fast can I get approved for life insurance in Tampa?

Fully underwritten policies typically take 24 to 72 hours with accelerated underwriting, or 2–4 weeks if a medical exam and records are required. Simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue final expense products can be approved the same day you apply. We tell you upfront which path your health profile will take.

What do life insurance agent reviews Tampa Florida clients leave about your agency?

Our Google rating is 4.9 out of 5 across more than 300 reviews, and we've been A+ BBB accredited since 2015. Recurring themes in the reviews are same-day callbacks, plain-language explanations of underwriting, bilingual service, and claims advocacy — like the Westshore family whose $15,000 final expense benefit was paid in 11 days.

Do you only work with clients in Tampa, or all of Florida?

Both. Our offices are in Miami (Little Havana and Kendall), and we serve Tampa and the entire Tampa Bay region by phone, secure video appointment, and scheduled in-person meetings. Life insurance is licensed statewide across all of Florida, and health and Medicare appointments are available by video throughout Hillsborough County.

Can you really find me a lower rate than what I'm paying now?

Often, yes. Our clients save an average of 31% versus a single-carrier quote, and roughly one in three policy reviews results in a recommended replacement or restructure. We also offer a rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, we will match it or explain exactly why the difference exists.

Is a free policy review really free if I didn't buy my policy from you?

Yes. We review policies purchased anywhere — including employer group certificates and policies bought 10 or 20 years ago. We grade the death benefit, premium structure, riders, beneficiary designations and cash value performance, then give you a written summary. If your current policy is already competitive, we will tell you to keep it.

What if I have health problems — diabetes, cancer history, or I'm a smoker?

There are still strong options. We work with carriers that underwrite controlled diabetes and treated cancer at preferred-plus rates, and we offer guaranteed-issue final expense coverage for ages 50–85 with no health questions at all. Being declined elsewhere does not mean you are uninsurable.

Do you handle claims for my family after I pass away?

Yes — claims advocacy is included at no charge. Our staff contacts the carrier, confirms the required documents, follows up on your behalf and keeps your beneficiary informed until funds are released. Families should never have to argue with an insurance company while grieving.

Can you help me with Medicare if I'm turning 65 in Tampa?

Absolutely. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant and represent 10 health carriers including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter and Molina. We compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap and Part D plans side by side. Many Advantage plans are $0 monthly premium, and Plan G supplements in this market typically run $115–$210 per month.

How do I qualify for an ACA subsidy in Hillsborough County?

Eligibility is based on household size and projected 2026 income relative to the federal poverty level. Most of our Tampa clients qualify for premium tax credits, and many households land under $50 per month after subsidies. We calculate it for free and help you document self-employment or 1099 income correctly so the credit isn't clawed back later.
