Can I get life insurance without a medical exam in Hialeah?

Yes. Guaranteed Issue Life accepts applicants ages 50–85 with no health questions and no exam, and most final expense products use a short health questionnaire instead of bloodwork. If you have diabetes, high blood pressure, COPD or a prior heart event, we have multiple simplified-issue carriers that underwrite those conditions favorably.

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Miami-Dade?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) often has a $0 monthly premium but uses networks and prior authorizations. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) Plan G lets you see any Medicare-accepting provider nationwide, with rates typically $115–$210 per month in Miami-Dade depending on age. We compare both against your actual prescription list before recommending.

Do you help with ACA Marketplace subsidies and Florida KidCare?

Yes — and this help is free. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our self-employed and 1099 clients end up paying under $50 per month for a Marketplace plan. We also assist with Florida KidCare applications for children at no cost. Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15.

How often should I review my life or health insurance policy?

Once a year at minimum, and immediately after a marriage, divorce, birth, new mortgage or job change. Claims advocacy is included with every policy we write. We call the carrier on your beneficiary's behalf, request the forms, submit the paperwork and follow the file until it is paid.
