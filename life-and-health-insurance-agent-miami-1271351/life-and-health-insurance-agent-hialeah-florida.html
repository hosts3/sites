How much does life insurance cost in Hialeah, Florida?

A healthy 30-year-old can typically secure $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for roughly $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies range from $32 to $95 per month based on age and the amount of coverage you choose ($5,000–$25,000).

Is the consultation with your Hialeah insurance agent really free?

Yes — consultations, quotes and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Insurance carriers pay our commission, not you, so your premium is the same whether you work with us or apply directly online. You'll never receive an invoice from our agency.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Fully underwritten term and permanent policies usually issue in 2 to 5 weeks, depending on medical records. Accelerated and no-exam final expense policies are frequently approved within 24 to 72 hours. Guaranteed issue plans for ages 50–85 with no health questions generally issue the same week.

Do you offer Medicare help in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Absolutely. Our bilingual licensed agents explain Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medigap and Part D drug plans in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. We also host a free Medicare workshop at our Little Havana office on the first Wednesday of every month — no cost, no sales pitch.

What happens if I have a health condition and get declined?

Declines are not the end of the road. We work with guaranteed issue carriers such as Gerber Life and Americo that accept clients with diabetes, COPD or a prior cancer diagnosis, and we know which underwriters are most lenient for specific conditions. If one carrier says no, we have seven more to try.

Can you help me qualify for ACA subsidies in Miami-Dade County?

Yes. Most Hialeah households qualify for premium tax credits based on income and family size, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies. We calculate your estimated credit, compare Florida Blue, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar and Simply Healthcare plans, and help you enroll before the January 15 deadline. Florida KidCare enrollment help is always free.

What areas does your insurance agency serve?

We serve all of Miami-Dade County, including Hialeah, Hialeah Gardens, Miami Lakes, Miami Springs, Doral, Little Havana, Kendall, Coral Gables, Aventura and Miami Beach, plus Broward County (Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Fort Lauderdale) and Monroe County. Do you charge more than buying directly from the carrier?

No. Life and health insurance premiums are filed with the state, so the rate is identical whether you buy direct or through an independent agent. The difference is that we compare multiple carriers at once, explain the fine print, and remain your advocate after the policy is issued — at no extra cost to you.

What happens if my family needs to file a claim?

You call us first. Our claims advocacy service means we contact the carrier, request the required forms, help complete the death certificate and beneficiary paperwork, and follow up until funds are released. Families should not have to navigate a claims department during a loss — that is our job.

Can you review a life insurance policy I already own?

Yes, and it's free — even if you bought the policy from a different agency or directly online.
