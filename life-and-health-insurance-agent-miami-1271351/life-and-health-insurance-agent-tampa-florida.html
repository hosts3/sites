Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Tampa, Florida

How much does it cost to work with a life and health insurance agent in Tampa, Florida?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, plan comparisons and annual policy reviews are always free — there are no fees, no service charges and no obligation. Insurance carriers pay our commission after a policy is issued, so our compensation does not add a single dollar to your premium.

How quickly can I get life insurance coverage in Tampa?

Most healthy applicants are approved in 24 to 72 hours with accelerated underwriting, and some no-exam policies are issued the same day. Applications requiring a medical exam or records typically take 2 to 4 weeks. We track every application daily and notify you the moment underwriting makes a decision.

What life insurance rates can a 45-year-old in Tampa expect to pay?

A healthy 45-year-old non-smoker typically qualifies for $500,000 of 20-year term coverage in the $58 to $75 per month range, depending on carrier and underwriting class. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 generally runs $32 to $95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of benefit. Exact pricing depends on health history and tobacco use.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap plans in Hillsborough County?

Yes. We are AHIP Medicare certified annually and CMS-compliant, and we compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Plan G, Plan N) and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and more. Many Tampa-area Advantage plans have a $0 monthly premium, while Medigap Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month.

Can I still get coverage if I have a pre-existing condition like diabetes or high blood pressure?

Frequently yes. Different carriers view controlled diabetes, high blood pressure and sleep apnea very differently. We place cases with the carrier most likely to approve you at the best underwriting class, and for ages 50–85 we offer guaranteed-issue final expense policies with no health questions at all.

How do ACA Marketplace subsidies work for self-employed Tampa workers?

Subsidies are based on your estimated household income and family size, not your employment status. Many self-employed, gig-economy and 1099 clients in Hillsborough County qualify for premium tax credits that reduce monthly costs to under $50. We calculate the estimate, submit the application and help reconcile income changes during the year — at no cost to you.

What happens if I need to file a claim or my family needs to file one?

Our claims advocacy service is included with every policy. We place the carrier calls, gather the required documents and follow the claim through to payment — our office handles the paperwork so your beneficiary does not have to navigate a claims department alone during a difficult time.

Are you a captive agent tied to one insurance company?

No. We are a fully independent insurance agency appointed with 18+ carriers across life, health and Medicare. That means we can compare options from Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, Nationwide, Gerber Life and others, then recommend the plan that best fits your situation rather than the one we are required to sell.
