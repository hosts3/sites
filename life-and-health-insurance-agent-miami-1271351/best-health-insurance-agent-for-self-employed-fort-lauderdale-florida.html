Can you help self-employed workers who have a pre-existing condition?

Yes. ACA Marketplace plans are guaranteed-issue, meaning carriers cannot deny you coverage or charge more because of a pre-existing condition such as diabetes, high blood pressure or a past surgery. For life insurance, we work with carriers like Mutual of Omaha and Americo that underwrite these conditions favorably rather than auto-declining.

What happens if I miss open enrollment in Florida?

Florida ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15. Outside that window, you may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period after marriage, a birth or adoption, job loss, a move, or a significant income change. Medicaid and Florida KidCare accept applications year-round, and we help with both at no cost.
