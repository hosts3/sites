Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in St. Petersburg, Florida

Updated for 2026 — the questions we hear most from Pinellas County families comparing online quotes with a local licensed agent.

Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in St. Petersburg, Florida?
Neither channel is cheaper by default — the premium comes from the carrier's filed rate table, not from where you click. What changes is underwriting. An agent can match you to the carrier that will classify you as Preferred rather than Standard, which can cut your premium 20–40%. Our service costs you $0 because carriers pay the commission.
How much does life insurance cost in St. Petersburg in 2026?
As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old pays roughly $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term. A healthy 45-year-old pays about $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies run $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. Real pricing depends on age, health and tobacco use.
Can I get approved online without a medical exam?
Yes. Accelerated underwriting now uses prescription databases, MIB records and motor vehicle reports instead of a blood draw for many applicants under 60. Decisions often arrive in 24–72 hours. For ages 50–85 we also place guaranteed issue policies with no health questions and no exam at all.
What happens to my claim if I bought the policy online myself?
Your beneficiary files directly with the carrier's claims department and waits. With us, claims advocacy is included: we call the carrier, submit the certified death certificate, track the file weekly and follow up until the check is issued to your beneficiary. Families get a named agent, not a ticket number.
How long does it take to get a policy issued?
Fully electronic applications with accelerated underwriting are typically issued in 24 to 72 hours. Applications requiring a paramedical exam or attending physician statement average 2 to 4 weeks. We send you a status update every 48 hours so you are never guessing where things stand.
Do you charge a fee or add commission to my premium?
No. Consultations, quotes, applications and annual policy reviews are 100% free. Insurers pay our commission out of their own marketing budget, and the rate table is identical whether you apply through us or directly. You can read more on our services page.
I live in St. Petersburg — can I meet an agent in person?
Yes. We meet clients at their home, office or a neutral location across Pinellas County, and virtual appointments are available statewide. Saturday appointments run 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM by request, and evening video calls are available until 8:00 PM during Medicare and ACA enrollment.
What credentials and licenses should I check before hiring an insurance agent?
Verify a Florida Department of Financial Services license — ours is License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and 2-40 (Health). Ask whether the agent is independent or captive, whether they are AHIP certified for Medicare, and whether they carry E&O insurance. We are A+ BBB accredited since 2015 and NABIP members.
"Can you help if I already have a policy somewhere else?
Absolutely. Our free policy review covers existing policies even if you never bought from us. We check the death benefit against your current mortgage and income needs, verify your beneficiary designations are current, and show you whether a replacement policy would genuinely save money — sometimes the answer is to keep what you have.
Do you offer emergency or same-day appointments?
For urgent situations — a terminal diagnosis, an upcoming surgery, or a mortgage closing deadline — we can often complete an application the same day and use accelerated underwriting for a faster decision. Call +1-540-352-6249 any time; our phone line is staffed 24/7.
Is an online life insurance quote accurate?
Online quote engines typically use the best-case health class to make the number look attractive. If you take a prescription for blood pressure or cholesterol, your real offer is often one or two classes lower — sometimes double the displayed price. We quote the class you will actually qualify for.
What areas of Florida do you serve?
Life insurance is licensed statewide, so we serve all of Florida. Health, Medicare and ACA appointments are handled in Miami-Dade, Broward and Monroe counties for in-person visits, with video appointments available for St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Largo and the entire Tampa Bay region.
