How much does it cost to work with an affordable health insurance agent near me in Tampa Florida?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, subsidy calculations and annual policy reviews are 100% free, with no obligation. Our agents are paid by the insurance carrier once a policy is issued — never by you. You pay the same premium whether you enroll through us or directly with the carrier.

How fast can I get a quote and get covered in Tampa?

Most quote requests get a callback in under 2 hours during business hours, and every request receives a response within our 24-hour guarantee. Term life policies typically issue in 24–72 hours when no exam is required; ACA health and Medicare enrollments are confirmed in about 2–5 business days.

Do I qualify for a subsidy on ACA Marketplace health insurance in Hillsborough County?

Most households in Hillsborough County do qualify for premium tax credits based on income and household size. Many of our Tampa clients end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies, and some pay $0. Enrollment help with the Marketplace and Florida KidCare is always free of charge.

What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent can only sell one company's products. As an independent insurance agency, we are appointed with 10+ carriers — Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo and more — so we can compare plans and match you to the best value rather than the only option.

Can I get life insurance in Tampa without a medical exam?

Yes. Several carriers we represent offer accelerated underwriting that can approve applicants in 24–72 hours without an exam for coverage up to $1,000,000, depending on age and health history. For ages 50–85, our Guaranteed Issue policies require no health questions and no exam at all, with full benefits beginning in year two.

When can I enroll in Medicare or change my plan in Tampa, Florida?

Your Initial Enrollment Period runs seven months around your 65th birthday. Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 – December 7 each year, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs January 1 – March 31. During Annual Enrollment we offer evening video appointments until 8:00 PM Monday–Thursday so you can review plans after work.

What does your free policy review include?

We review any life or health policy — even one you bought elsewhere or years ago. You get a plain-English summary of coverage, exclusions, cash value and beneficiary designations, plus a written comparison against current 2026 market rates. If your existing policy is better, we will tell you so and leave it alone.

What credentials and licenses do your agents hold?

Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license: License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) or License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). Yes — claims advocacy is included at no charge. When a claim is filed, our agency contacts the carrier directly, submits the required documentation on behalf of your beneficiaries, and follows up until the benefit is paid. Families should not have to navigate a national call center during a loss.
