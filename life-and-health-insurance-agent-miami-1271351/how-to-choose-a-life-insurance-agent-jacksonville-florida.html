Frequently Asked Questions About Choosing a Life Insurance Agent in Jacksonville, Florida

Updated for 2026 with the questions Jacksonville residents ask us most often. Still unsure? Call +1-540-352-6249 for a free, no-pressure conversation.

How do I know if a life insurance agent in Jacksonville is actually licensed?

Ask for their Florida license number and verify it yourself in about 60 seconds at the Florida Department of Financial Services licensee search. Ours is a Florida 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) appointment, and our agency also holds 2-40 Health licenses. Every one of our 8 agents carries an active license and completes annual continuing education.

Does it cost anything to work with your insurance agency?

No. Consultations, quotes, needs analyses, and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Our commissions are paid by the insurance carrier after a policy is issued — you never write us a check. If we cannot beat or clearly explain your current coverage, we will tell you to keep it.

How much does term life insurance cost in Jacksonville, Florida in 2026?

As a benchmark, a healthy 30-year-old can often get $250,000 of 20-year term for roughly $18–$24 per month, and a healthy 45-year-old can expect about $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Your exact rate depends on age, tobacco use, build, health history, and the carrier's underwriting class — which is exactly why comparing multiple carriers matters.

How long does it take to get a life insurance policy issued?

No-medical-exam products frequently receive a decision in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten policies that require labs and medical records average 2 to 4 weeks, occasionally longer if a physician's office is slow to return records. We give you a written status update at least every 3 business days until your policy is delivered.

Can I get coverage if I have diabetes, high blood pressure, or a past cancer diagnosis?

Usually yes. Many carriers we represent underwrite controlled diabetes and hypertension at standard or modestly rated classes. For more serious histories — a recent stroke, COPD, or metastatic cancer — we place clients into guaranteed issue or simplified issue final expense coverage with no health questions and no exam. We will tell you honestly which path is realistic.

What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents one insurance company only. An independent agent like ours is appointed with multiple carriers — including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, Transamerica, John Hancock, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life — so you can compare identical coverage across several companies and choose the strongest price and underwriting fit rather than the only option available.

Do you offer bilingual service for Jacksonville's Spanish and Haitian Creole communities?

Yes. We provide full service in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole by phone, video, and in person. Documents can be explained line by line in your preferred language, and benefit claim support for your family is available in all three languages — a service many Jacksonville agencies simply cannot match.

What happens if I need to file a claim or my beneficiary needs help?

We act as your claims advocate. Our team contacts the carrier, orders certified death certificates, completes the claimant statement, and follows the claim until funds are disbursed. Families typically receive payment within 10 to 30 days for clean claims. That support costs you nothing and continues for the life of the policy.

Can you review a life insurance policy I bought from a different agent?

Absolutely — and it is one of our most requested services. Our free no-obligation policy review checks your death benefit, premium, cash value performance, riders, and beneficiary designations, including policies purchased elsewhere. We regularly find outdated beneficiaries, duplicate coverage, and term policies that quietly expired years ago.

Is there a guarantee or cancellation window if I change my mind?

Yes. Florida law gives you a free-look period — generally 10 days on most life insurance policies and 14 days on annuities — during which you can cancel for a full premium refund, no questions asked. We also provide a written 24-hour response guarantee and a rate-shopping guarantee: find a lower rate for identical coverage and we will match it or explain the difference.

Do you handle Medicare and ACA health coverage in Jacksonville too?

Yes. We are AHIP Medicare certified and CMS-compliant, and we compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap, and Part D plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar and Simply Healthcare. We also enroll ACA Marketplace plans with subsidy calculations and Florida KidCare applications at no cost to you. Evening video appointments run Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15).
