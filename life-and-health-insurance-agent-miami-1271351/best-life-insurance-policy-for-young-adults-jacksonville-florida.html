How much does the best life insurance policy for young adults in Jacksonville, Florida cost each month?

For a healthy 30-year-old non-smoker in Jacksonville, a $250,000 20-year term policy typically runs between $18 and $24 per month. A $500,000 policy for the same applicant generally lands in the low $30s. Final rates depend on your age, health history, tobacco use and the carrier's underwriting guidelines — and your quote is always free.

Are you licensed to sell insurance in Jacksonville, Florida?

Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance). We are licensed to write life insurance statewide across Florida, including Jacksonville and all of Duval, Clay, St. Johns and Nassau counties.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance in Jacksonville?

Not necessarily. Many carriers now offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for applicants under 50 in good health — often just a few health questions and a prescription check. For final expense and guaranteed issue policies between ages 50 and 85, no health questions and no exam are required at all.

How quickly can my Jacksonville life insurance policy be approved?

No-exam policies are frequently approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten policies that require labs and medical records generally take two to four weeks. Either way, our 24-hour response guarantee means you hear back from a licensed agent the same day you request a quote.

How much life insurance do young adults in Jacksonville actually need?

Most financial professionals recommend 10 to 15 times your annual income, plus enough to retire your mortgage and other debts. For a young Jacksonville family earning $60,000 with a mortgage, a $500,000 to $750,000 term policy is a common target — and often costs less than a streaming subscription bundle.

Do you charge a fee for consultations or policy reviews?

Never. Every consultation, quote and annual review is 100% free with no obligation to purchase. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carrier, not by the client. That structure keeps our recommendations unbiased whether you buy from us or not.

Can you review a life insurance policy I already own?

Absolutely — and we encourage it. Our free policy review covers policies purchased anywhere, even years ago from a different company. We compare your death benefit, premium, riders and beneficiary designations, then explain in plain English whether you are overpaying or under-covered.

What if I have diabetes, high blood pressure or a prior health issue?

We are appointed with more than 15 carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, Transamerica, John Hancock, Nationwide and Gerber Life — each with different underwriting tolerances. Yes. Our agents are AHIP Medicare certified and CMS compliant, and we compare Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina and Oscar plans. What happens if my family needs to file a claim?

We advocate for your beneficiaries at no cost. Our team handles the carrier calls, completes and submits the paperwork, and follows up until the benefit is paid. In 2025 we supported multiple Duval County families through claims that were settled in as little as 11 days.

Why work with an independent insurance agency instead of a single carrier?

A captive agent can only sell one company's products. As an independent agency, we place your case with whichever of our 15+ carriers offers the best combination of price, underwriting flexibility and living benefits. On average, Jacksonville clients who let us shop their case find better coverage for less than a single-carrier quote.
