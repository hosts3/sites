How much does life insurance cost in Jacksonville, Florida?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier, not by you — so the price you pay is the same whether you work with us or apply directly. There is no cost to the client at any stage.

Yes. What is a free policy review and who qualifies for one?

Any Florida resident qualifies — even if you bought your policy from a different agent or company years ago.
