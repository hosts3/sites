How We Ranked These 10 Picks

Every entry below was scored against five criteria that matter when you are shopping for life and health coverage in Miami:

  1. Carrier access — how many A-rated insurers the agent can quote side by side (more carriers = better pricing).
  2. Licensing scope — Florida 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) versus 2-40 (Health only) versus a single-line appointment.
  3. Language support — English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole service for Miami-Dade households.
  4. Total cost to the client — commissions are carrier-paid; no one should ever bill you for a quote.
  5. Post-sale service — free annual policy reviews, beneficiary updates, and claims advocacy for your family.

2. Captive Career Agent (Single Carrier)

Rating: ★★★☆☆ 3.4 / 5

These are the agents employed directly by one insurer — you will meet them through a company website or a mailer. They know their own product line deeply and can often push underwriting exceptions through faster, but they can only quote one company. If their rates are uncompetitive for your age or health profile, you will never know, because no comparison is shown.

Pros:

  • Deep product knowledge and direct underwriting contacts
  • Usually fast, streamlined application portals

Cons:

  • Zero carrier comparison — pricing is take-it-or-leave-it
  • Limited help if your health history falls outside their underwriting guidelines

3. Online-Only Digital Broker or Quoting Platform

Rating: ★★★★☆ 3.9 / 5

Web-first brokers let you run instant quotes and complete an application without a phone call. They are convenient for a healthy 32-year-old who already knows exactly what term length and face amount they want. The tradeoff is service: when a claim is filed, or when underwriting asks for medical records, you are routed to a 1-800 queue rather than a named local agent in Miami.

Pros:

  • Instant online quotes, often within minutes
  • No appointment needed for straightforward term coverage

Cons:

  • Little to no guidance on living benefits, riders, or trust/beneficiary wording
  • Claim-time support is impersonal and slow to escalate

5. Final Expense & Burial Insurance Specialist

Rating: ★★★★☆ 4.1 / 5

These agents focus on ages 45–85 with simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue products — no medical exam, and in many cases no health questions for ages 50–85. FAQ: Choosing the Best Life Insurance Agent Near Me in Miami

Does it cost anything to work with a life and health insurance agent?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, and enrollment help are free, and agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier — never by you. If anyone asks you to pay a fee for a life insurance quote or a Medicare enrollment, walk away. Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group charges $0 for quotes and reviews, including reviews of policies you bought elsewhere.

What license should I look for on a Florida agent?

For life insurance, look for a Florida Department of Financial Services 2-15 license (Life, Health & Annuity). A 2-40 license covers health insurance only and legally cannot write a life policy. You can verify any agent's license status for free on the Florida DFS website using their name or license number — a legitimate agent will gladly give you that number.

How much does term life insurance cost in Miami?

Rates depend on age, health, tobacco use, and coverage amount. As a benchmark, a healthy 30-year-old might pay roughly $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage, and a healthy 45-year-old around $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Florida also has no state income tax, which has no effect on premiums but does change how much coverage makes sense relative to income replacement.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. Simplified-issue policies typically use a health questionnaire instead of bloodwork and a paramedical exam, and guaranteed-issue policies for ages 50–85 ask no health questions at all. The tradeoff is a lower face amount and a graded benefit period — often two years — during which only premiums paid plus interest are returned if death is from natural causes.

I am self-employed with 1099 income. What should I look for?

You have no employer group life, no disability coverage, and often no paid time off, so individual term life plus disability income insurance is usually the priority. Bring your last two years of tax returns or 1099s — some carriers use financial underwriting to set a maximum face amount, and a local agent who understands gig-economy and cash-business income documentation will get you approved faster than an online portal.

Do I need a separate agent for Medicare and for life insurance?

Not necessarily, and there is a real benefit to keeping them together. A single licensed 2-15 agency can coordinate your Medicare Advantage or Medigap Plan G with your final expense policy, so your beneficiaries are consistent and your budget is reviewed as one plan. Medicare Supplement Plan G rates in Miami-Dade typically run about $115–$210 per month, and many Medicare Advantage plans are available at $0 monthly premium.

Can I get service in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Yes — and you should insist on it. Understanding exclusions, beneficiary designations, and Medicare Part D formularies in your first language prevents expensive mistakes. Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group provides bilingual English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole phone support and in-person appointments at its Little Havana and Kendall offices.

What happens if my claim is denied or my beneficiary needs help?

A good agent stays involved after the sale. Ask upfront whether the agency handles carrier calls and paperwork on behalf of beneficiaries. Claims advocacy — gathering death certificates, filing the claim, and following up on contestability periods — is one of the clearest markers between an order-taker and a true advisor.
