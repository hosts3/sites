Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Tampa, Florida

Updated for 2026. These are the questions our Tampa clients ask most before their first appointment.

How much does a term life insurance policy cost in Tampa, Florida?
As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Tampa resident can get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 per month. Your final rate depends on age, health history, nicotine use and the carrier's underwriting class — which is exactly why we quote at least three carriers side by side.
How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?
Most of our Tampa clients are approved in 24–72 hours through accelerated underwriting, which skips the medical exam. Full underwriting cases that require labs or medical records typically take 2–4 weeks. Once the carrier issues the policy, we deliver it within 24–72 hours and walk you through the beneficiary designations line by line.
Do you charge any fees for your services?
No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and claims advocacy are 100% free — no fees, no obligation, no cost to the client. We are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you, so your premium is the same whether you work with us or apply directly. If you ever find a lower rate for identical coverage, we'll match it or explain exactly why it differs.
Why use an independent insurance agent instead of buying online?
Online quote engines typically show you one carrier or sell your contact information to multiple call centers. As an independent agency appointed with more than 20 carriers — including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare and Humana — we shop your case across the market, then advocate for you during underwriting if the rate class isn't what we expected.
Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?
Yes. Several carriers we represent offer accelerated and simplified-issue underwriting with no exam and no blood draw, based on prescription history and database checks. We also offer guaranteed-issue final expense coverage for ages 50–85 with no health questions at all. Coverage amounts and waiting periods vary, so we'll confirm what you qualify for during the assessment.
What Medicare help do you provide during Annual Enrollment?
We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant, and we enroll Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna and Cigna. During Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) we run video appointments until 8:00 PM Monday through Thursday and host a free bilingual Medicare workshop on the first Wednesday of each month.
Do you help with ACA Marketplace subsidies in Hillsborough County?
Yes, at no cost to you. Most Tampa-area households qualify for premium tax credits. We screen your income, household size and employer coverage, then compare Ambetter, Molina, Oscar, Cigna and Simply Healthcare plans. Many of the families we've enrolled pay under $50 per month after subsidies — and we handle the paperwork for Florida KidCare at no charge.
What happens if my beneficiary needs to file a claim?
You call our office first, not the carrier's automated line. Our agency acts as claims advocate: we place the initial claim notification, confirm which documents the carrier requires, submit the certified death certificate and any supporting paperwork, and then follow up weekly until the payout is issued. We do not charge the beneficiary a fee for this service.
Will you review a policy I already own — even if I didn't buy it from you?
Absolutely. Our free no-obligation Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies purchased anywhere — other agencies, direct carriers, or employer group plans. We check your guaranteed premium, cash value performance, riders you may be paying for but don't need, and whether your beneficiary designations are still accurate after a marriage, divorce or birth. There's no requirement to switch anything.
