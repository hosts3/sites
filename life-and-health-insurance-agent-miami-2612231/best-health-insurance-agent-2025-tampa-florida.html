What's the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement in Tampa?
Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a private network plan — many are $0 monthly premium and include dental, vision, hearing, and OTC benefits, but you stay in-network and prior authorizations apply. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) pays alongside Original Medicare, lets you see any provider nationwide who accepts Medicare, and requires no referrals — Plan G in the Tampa area typically runs $115–$210 per month. We show both side-by-side with your actual prescriptions before you decide.
