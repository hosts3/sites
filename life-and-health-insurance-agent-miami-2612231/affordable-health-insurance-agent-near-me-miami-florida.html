When is Open Enrollment for 2026 health plans in Florida?
ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15, and Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7. Outside those windows you may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period after a move, job loss, marriage, divorce, birth or loss of Medicaid. We check eligibility for free year-round.
Can you help me choose Medicare when I turn 65 this year?
Absolutely. Yes. Guaranteed issue life insurance is available for ages 50–85 with no health questions and no exam, and final expense coverage for ages 45–85 uses simplified underwriting.
