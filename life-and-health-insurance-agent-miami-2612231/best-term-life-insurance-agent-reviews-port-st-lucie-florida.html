How much does term life insurance cost in Port St. Lucie, Florida?
For healthy, non-tobacco applicants, a $250,000 20-year term policy at age 30 typically runs $18–$24 per month, and $500,000 at age 45 usually falls between $58 and $75 per month. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, family medical history, tobacco use and the length of the term you choose. Women often qualify for lower rates than men at the same age.
How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?
Fully underwritten policies normally issue in 7 to 21 business days, while accelerated and no-exam products can issue in as little as 24 to 72 hours. We submit your application the same day you decide to move forward and follow up with the carrier daily, so there are no long silent gaps.
Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance?
Not necessarily. Many of our carriers offer no-exam term life, final expense and guaranteed issue policies for ages 45 to 85. A short phone health interview often replaces the blood draw and paramedical exam completely, which speeds up approval considerably — especially for self-employed and gig-economy workers who cannot take time off.
Is the consultation really free, and how do you get paid?
Yes — quotes, policy reviews, Medicare comparisons and ACA subsidy calculations are 100% free with no obligation. Our commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier, never by you. Your premium is identical whether you enroll through our agency or contact the carrier yourself, so you get expert guidance at no extra cost.
Do you work with more than one insurance company?
We are a fully independent agency appointed with 8 or more A-rated carriers, including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, Transamerica, John Hancock, Nationwide, Gerber Life, Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health, Doctor's Health Plan and Simply Healthcare.
