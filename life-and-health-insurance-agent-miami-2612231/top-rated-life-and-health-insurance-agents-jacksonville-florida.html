Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Jacksonville, Florida

Updated for 2026 enrollment periods. If your question isn't answered here, call +1-540-352-6249 — a licensed agent answers, not a script reader.

How much does a life insurance agent cost in Jacksonville, Florida?
Nothing. Our consultations, quotes and policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Agent commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier, so you pay the exact same premium whether you enroll through us or go straight to the company. There are no broker fees, no service charges and no hidden costs on any policy we write.
How quickly can I get covered?
Many clients are approved in 24 to 72 hours. Simplified-issue and final expense policies with no medical exam can be issued same day or next day. Fully underwritten term and permanent policies typically take 3 to 10 business days, depending on how fast the paramedical exam and medical records come back. Medicare enrollment can be completed in a single 30-minute appointment.
How much does term life insurance cost for a healthy 40-year-old in Jacksonville?
As of 2026, a healthy 40-year-old non-smoker typically pays roughly $35–$48 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term coverage, and about $22–$30 per month for $250,000. A healthy 45-year-old can usually get $500,000 of 20-year term for approximately $58–$75 per month. Your exact rate depends on height, weight, medications, family history and tobacco use.
What if I have health problems — can I still qualify?
Very often, yes. We work with carriers that specialize in diabetes, controlled high blood pressure, past cancer, sleep apnea and elevated cholesterol. For ages 50–85 we offer guaranteed-issue whole life with no health questions and no medical exam, so even clients with serious conditions can secure final expense coverage between $5,000 and $25,000.
Do you help with Medicare if I'm turning 65?
Yes — we are AHIP Medicare-certified annually and CMS-compliant. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and more. Many Advantage plans have a $0 monthly premium; Medigap Plan G in Duval County typically runs $115–$210 per month. We also host a free bilingual Medicare workshop on the first Wednesday of every month.
Can you help me get ACA Marketplace subsidies?
Absolutely, at no cost to you. We calculate your premium tax credit, compare Ambetter, Oscar Health, Molina, Florida Blue and Doctor's Health Plan options, and submit the application with you. Most Miami-Dade and Jacksonville households we assist end up paying under $50 per month after subsidies, and many qualify for $0 Silver plans. We also provide free Florida KidCare enrollment help for families with children.
Are you an independent agent or do you work for one insurance company?
We are fully independent. Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group is appointed with more than 20 carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, Nationwide, Gerber Life, Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina and Oscar Health. That means we can honestly recommend the plan that fits your budget instead of the only plan we're allowed to sell.
Where are you licensed, and what credentials do you hold?
We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only), and all eight of our agents carry active Florida licenses with annual continuing education. Life insurance is licensed statewide in Florida. We are A+ BBB accredited since 2015, NABIP members, and carry a 4.9-star rating across 300+ client reviews.
What happens if I need to file a claim?
Our agency handles it for you. We call the carrier, confirm exactly which documents are required, submit the death certificate or claim forms on your behalf, and follow the claim until funds are released to the beneficiary. Most life claims are paid within 7 to 14 days once documentation is received. This claims advocacy service is included at no charge for every policy we place.
Can you review a policy I already own?
Yes — and it's completely free, even if you bought the policy somewhere else. Our free annual policy review checks your death benefit against your current mortgage, income replacement needs and inflation, verifies your beneficiary designations are up to date, and compares your premium against current 2026 rates. Clients regularly find savings of $30 to $50 per month on identical coverage.
Do you offer appointments outside business hours?
Yes. Standard hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM EST and Saturday 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM by appointment. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15) we offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM. We also provide bilingual phone support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.
Is there a guarantee if I'm not satisfied?
Every policy we issue carries the standard free-look period required by Florida law, which allows you to cancel within the stated review window for a full premium refund. On top of that, we offer a rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower rate for identical coverage within the first year, we will match it or explain in writing why the difference exists.
