How much does term life insurance cost in Miami, Florida in 2026?

A healthy 30-year-old in Miami can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old buying $500,000 of 20-year term generally pays $58 to $75 per month. Final expense coverage runs roughly $32 to $95 per month depending on age and coverage amount. Your actual rate depends on age, health history, nicotine use, driving record and which carrier we place you with.

Are consultations with your Miami insurance agency really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation and no strings attached. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by clients, so you never pay a fee, a service charge or a renewal fee for our advice or enrollment help. Many Medicare Advantage plans also have a $0 monthly premium.

How quickly can I get covered after I apply?

Fully underwritten term life policies typically issue in 3 to 10 business days. Accelerated underwriting with no medical exam can be approved in 24 to 72 hours. Final expense and guaranteed issue plans are often active the same week, sometimes within 24 hours of application.

Do you offer no-medical-exam life insurance?

Yes. We are appointed with multiple carriers that offer no-exam term and final expense coverage based on health questionnaire answers alone. We also place guaranteed issue plans for ages 50 to 85 with no health questions at all — acceptance is guaranteed regardless of medical history.

What is the difference between term life and whole life insurance?

Term life covers you for a set period — 10, 15, 20 or 30 years — and is the most affordable way to protect a mortgage or young family. Whole life is permanent, never expires and builds guaranteed tax-deferred cash value, but costs three to eight times more for the same death benefit. Most Miami families start with term and add permanent coverage later.

Can you help me if my health history is complicated?

Absolutely — this is where an independent agent matters most. Because we work with 20+ carriers, we can match diabetes, controlled hypertension, past cancer treatment or high BMI to the carrier that underwrites it most favorably. In many cases we secure a standard rate where a direct carrier would have declined or heavily rated the policy.

What does your free policy review include?

We will review any existing life, Medicare or ACA policy — even ones you did not buy through us. We check your death benefit against inflation and current debt, confirm your beneficiary designations are correct, verify your premium is still competitive in 2026, and flag gaps such as missing living benefits riders. The review takes about 25 minutes and costs nothing.

Are your agents licensed and certified in Florida?

Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — License 2-15 for Life, Health & Annuity or License 2-40 for Health Insurance. Our team completes annual continuing education and is AHIP Medicare certified with Fraud, Waste & Abuse training every year. We are CMS-compliant for Medicare marketing and enrollment. Our agency has been A+ BBB accredited since 2015.

Do you help with Medicare if I am turning 65 this year?

Yes. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna and others. Medigap Plan G in Miami-Dade typically runs $115 to $210 per month depending on age, while many Medicare Advantage plans are $0 monthly premium. We also host a free bilingual Medicare workshop at our Little Havana office on the first Wednesday of every month.

Can you help self-employed and 1099 workers with no employer benefits?

That is one of our specialties. Gig drivers, restaurant staff, salon professionals and construction contractors across Miami-Dade come to us for individual ACA Marketplace plans plus term life. Most households qualify for premium tax credits — many of our clients pay under $50 per month for health coverage after subsidies, and we handle the entire subsidy application at no cost to you.

What if my claim is denied or a beneficiary needs help?

We advocate for you directly. When a client passes, our agency contacts the carrier, orders death certificates, submits the claim and follows up until the beneficiary is paid — you never have to navigate the insurer alone. If a claim is contested, we escalate it with the carrier and guide the family through the appeal process step by step.
