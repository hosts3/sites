How much does life insurance cost in Tallahassee, Florida?

A healthy 30-year-old can typically qualify for $250,000 of 20-year term life coverage for approximately $18 to $24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old usually pays about $58 to $75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies for ages 45 to 85 generally range from $32 to $95 per month. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use and coverage amount — we quote multiple carriers so you can see the spread.

Do you charge a fee for quotes, consultations or policy reviews?

Never. Every consultation, quote and annual policy review is 100% free with no obligation. Insurance carriers pay our commission, not you, which means your premium is the same whether you work with us or buy directly from the company. In many cases we can find the same coverage for less.

How quickly can I get covered?

Many ACA Marketplace health plans and Medicare Advantage enrollments can be effective the first of the following month. Life insurance with accelerated underwriting can be issued in 24 to 72 hours, while fully underwritten policies with a medical exam typically take 2 to 4 weeks. We track every application daily and update you at each stage.

Are you licensed to sell insurance in Florida?

Yes. We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only), and we are licensed to write life insurance statewide across all of Florida — including Leon, Wakulla, Gadsden and Jefferson counties. Our agents also complete annual continuing education and AHIP Medicare certification.

Medicare Advantage or Medigap — which one should I choose?

It depends on how you use healthcare. Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans often carry a $0 monthly premium but use networks, referrals and copays. Medigap Plan G has a higher monthly premium — typically $115 to $210 in Florida depending on age — but lets you see any Medicare-accepting provider nationwide with very little out-of-pocket. We compare both side by side using your actual doctors and prescriptions.

What if I have a health condition and worry about being declined?

We pre-screen your health history against each carrier's underwriting guidelines before submitting anything, which dramatically reduces declines and protects your record. For ages 50 to 85 we also offer guaranteed issue and final expense policies with no health questions and no medical exam — coverage is immediate for accidental death with a graded benefit period for natural causes.

Can you help me get ACA subsidies or Florida KidCare?

Yes, and it costs you nothing. We screen your household income and size for premium tax credits, then enroll you during Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15) or a qualifying Special Enrollment Period. Most Florida households qualify for subsidies, and many of our clients pay under $50 a month for a Marketplace plan. We also complete Florida KidCare applications at no charge.

Do I need to meet in person, or can this be done by phone?

Both work. We serve Tallahassee, Florida clients primarily by phone and secure video appointment, and life insurance is licensed statewide so there's no geographic limitation. In-person appointments are available at our Little Havana and Kendall offices for clients in South Florida, and during Medicare Annual Enrollment we offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM.

What happens if I need to file a claim?

Our claims advocacy team handles it with you. We call the carrier, request the correct forms, help you complete them, and follow up until the benefit is paid. For beneficiaries, we do the heavy lifting so a grieving family isn't left navigating call centers alone. Claim support is included with every policy we write, at no additional cost.

Do you offer insurance for small businesses in Tallahassee?

Yes. We build group benefit packages for businesses with 5 to 50 employees — health, dental, vision, group life, short-term disability and ancillary products. We benchmark at least three carriers, present the comparison to ownership, and run on-site or virtual employee education meetings. We also handle carrier paperwork for onboarding and annual open enrollment.

Can I review a policy I already own?

Absolutely, and we encourage it. Our free Life Insurance Policy Review covers policies you bought from any agent or company — including ones you purchased years ago. We check your death benefit against inflation, confirm your beneficiaries are correct, look for better rates on identical coverage, and flag any riders or cash value you may not know you have.

Is there a guarantee if I'm not satisfied?

Yes. Consultations are free and non-committal, and we back our service with a rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, we will match it or explain precisely why the difference exists. We also provide a free annual policy review for every client and maintain a 24-hour response guarantee on all questions.
