Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in St. Petersburg, Florida

The questions our St. Petersburg clients ask most often — answered in plain English, with real numbers.

How much does term life insurance cost in St. Petersburg, Florida right now?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in St. Petersburg can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old usually pays about $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final rates are set by the carrier and depend on your age, health history, tobacco use, driving record and the term length you choose.

Are consultations and quotes really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation. Our agents are paid by the insurance carriers, not by you, so your premium is identical whether you work with us or apply directly. There are no consultation fees, no service charges and no cost to compare plans or ask questions.

How fast can I get covered?

It depends on the product. Final expense and guaranteed issue policies can be approved the same day with no medical exam. Many term life applications get an accelerated underwriting decision in 24–72 hours, while fully underwritten policies typically issue in 2–6 weeks.

What if I have health issues or have been declined before?

That is exactly why working with an independent agent matters. We are appointed with Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, Gerber Life, Transamerica, John Hancock, North American and Nationwide — so we can match you to the carrier with the most favorable underwriting for your specific condition, including no-exam guaranteed issue options for ages 50–85.

Do you help my family file a claim if something happens to me?

Yes, and it costs your family nothing extra. Our agency serves as claims advocate: we call the carrier, complete the paperwork and follow up until the benefit is paid. Most claims we assist with are paid within 2–4 weeks once the required documents are received.

Do you serve St. Petersburg in person, or only by phone?

Both. Life insurance is licensed statewide, so we complete most policies by phone or secure video from anywhere in St. Petersburg. For health and Medicare appointments, we schedule in-person visits throughout Pinellas County — including downtown, Gulfport, Clearwater, Largo and Pinellas Park.

Can you review a policy I already own from another agent?

Absolutely. Our free policy review covers coverage you bought anywhere — even years ago. We check your death benefit against today's needs, verify beneficiary designations, and compare your current premium against the 2026 market. Many clients find they are overpaying or underinsured. There is no obligation to change anything.

Do you charge more than buying directly from an insurance company?

No. Life insurance premiums are filed with the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation, so the rate is the same regardless of how you buy. The difference is that an independent agent can shop 18+ carriers for you and explain the fine print, while a captive agent can only present one company's products.

How much life insurance do I actually need?

A common starting point is 10–15 times your annual income, plus your mortgage balance, minus existing savings and employer coverage. A St. Petersburg parent earning $65,000 with a $280,000 mortgage often lands in the $750,000–$1,000,000 range for a 20-year term. We run your specific numbers during the free assessment.

What credentials should I look for in a St. Petersburg insurance agent?

Look for an active Florida Department of Financial Services license (2-15 for life, health and annuities, or 2-40 for health only), errors-and-omissions coverage, and carrier appointments with multiple A-rated insurers. Our founder holds a Florida 2-15 license, completes annual continuing education, maintains AHIP Medicare certification, and our agency has held BBB A+ accreditation since 2015.

Do you offer emergency or after-hours help?

Yes. Our goal is to respond to every quote request and client question within 24 hours, and our average response time is under 4 business hours. Urgent calls — a lapsed policy, a claim deadline, or an enrollment window closing — are handled the same day, including evenings. Call +1-540-352-6249 at any hour.

Who is the best term life insurance agent in St. Petersburg, Florida?

The "best" agent is simply the one who shops multiple carriers, explains underwriting honestly and stays available after the policy is issued. Independent reviews of our agency highlight four things repeatedly: 14+ years licensed, a 4.9-star average across 300+ reviews, a 24-hour response guarantee, and in-house claims advocacy.
