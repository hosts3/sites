How much does term life insurance cost in Tallahassee, Florida?

A healthy 30-year-old in Tallahassee typically pays $18 to $24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage. A healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58 to $75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies for ages 45 to 85 run $32 to $95 per month. Exact pricing depends on your age at application, health history, tobacco use, family medical history and the coverage amount you choose.

Do you charge a fee for quotes, consultations or policy reviews?

No — everything is free with no obligation. Our agency is compensated by the insurance carrier, not by you, so your premium is the same whether you work with us or buy direct from the company. Annual policy reviews, beneficiary updates and claims support are included at no charge for the life of your policy.

How fast can I get approved and covered?

Many applicants receive a decision within 24 to 72 hours through accelerated underwriting, with no medical exam required. Applications that require an exam or physician records typically take 2 to 4 weeks. Guaranteed issue final expense coverage for ages 50 to 85 can be issued the same day with no health questions. We tell you upfront which track your profile will follow.

Are your agents licensed to sell insurance in Florida?

Yes. Our agency holds active Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). Every agent on our team carries an active Florida license, completes annual continuing education and is AHIP-certified each year for Medicare, plus CMS-compliant on Medicare marketing and enrollment.

What makes an independent agent different from buying direct or through a captive agent?

An independent agency like ours contracts with 20+ carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, Nationwide, Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare and Ambetter. A captive agent can only sell you one company's products. We can place your health profile with the carrier most likely to approve you at the best underwriting class — which is often the difference between a standard and a preferred rate.

Can I get life insurance if I have health conditions or have been declined before?

Often, yes. We work with carriers that specialize in diabetes, controlled hypertension, sleep apnea, prior cancer history and cardiac conditions. For ages 50 to 85 with significant health issues, guaranteed issue whole life policies require no health questions and no medical exam. The key is knowing which carrier wants your specific profile — that is exactly what a free needs analysis with our agency determines.

Do I need a medical exam for life insurance?

Not always. Many of our carriers offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for applicants under 55 who meet basic health criteria — the decision is based on prescription history and a short questionnaire. Exam-free options are also standard for final expense and guaranteed issue products. We always show you the exam-free options alongside fully underwritten policies so you can compare price versus convenience.

What Medicare help do you provide in Tallahassee?

We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and Molina. Many Advantage plans in Leon County carry a $0 monthly premium. We review your doctor list and medication list before recommending anything, and we re-check your Part D coverage every Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7.

How do ACA Marketplace subsidies work for Tallahassee households?

Premium tax credits are based on your household income and size relative to the federal poverty level. Most Tallahassee-area households qualify for significant credits — many pay under $50 per month for a silver plan after subsidies are applied. We calculate your subsidy, compare Ambetter, Molina, Oscar Health and Doctor's Health Plan networks, and handle enrollment at zero cost to you.

What happens if I need to file a claim?

You call our agency first, not the insurance company's 1-800 number. We verify the policy is in force, order the required forms, walk your beneficiary through each document and follow up with the carrier until the claim is paid. There is never a charge for this service. Recent final expense claims our team handled in Tallahassee paid within 11 to 21 days of complete documentation.

Can you review a life insurance policy I already own?

Absolutely — even if you bought it from another agent or directly from a carrier. Our free policy review checks your rate against current 2026 pricing, confirms whether your underwriting class is accurate, verifies beneficiary designations and identifies gaps like missing living benefits or an outdated mortgage match. Many clients discover they are overpaying by $300 to $1,100 per year.

Do you offer bilingual service and evening appointments?

Yes. We provide phone support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. Evening video appointments are available Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare Annual Enrollment (October 15 – December 7) and ACA Open Enrollment (November 1 – January 15).
