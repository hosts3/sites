How much does life insurance cost in Cape Coral, Florida?

A healthy 30-year-old can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old quoting $500,000 of 20-year term usually sees $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally run $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage.

Is your help really free, and how do you get paid?

Yes — consultations, quotes and annual policy reviews cost you nothing. We are paid by the insurance carrier after a policy is issued, never by the client. That means our advice is independent and you never pay a broker fee, enrollment fee or service charge.

How fast can I get covered?

Most life insurance applications with accelerated underwriting are approved and issued within 24 to 72 hours; fully underwritten policies can take two to four weeks. ACA Marketplace plans can be effective as early as the first of the next month, and many Medicare Advantage enrollments are confirmed within 48 hours.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify for life insurance?

Not always. Many carriers now offer accelerated or no-exam underwriting using prescription, motor vehicle and claims database checks. We also offer guaranteed-issue final expense coverage for ages 50–85 with no health questions and no exam at all, though premiums are higher than fully underwritten policies.

Can you help me get ACA Marketplace subsidies in Cape Coral?

Absolutely, and the help is free. Most Lee County households qualify for premium tax credits based on income and household size, and many of our clients pay under $50 per month after subsidies. We handle the application, income projections and plan comparison, and we also assist with Florida KidCare enrollment.

When can I enroll in Medicare or change my plan?

Your Initial Enrollment Period runs seven months around your 65th birthday. The Medicare Annual Enrollment Period is October 15 – December 7 each year, and ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 – January 15. During those windows we offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM.

Do you meet clients in Cape Coral in person, or only by phone and video?

Life insurance is handled statewide by phone, email and secure video. We offer a free no-obligation policy review — even for policies we did not sell. We check your coverage amount, beneficiaries, riders and premium against current 2026 carrier rates. If your existing policy is already competitive, we will tell you so and leave it alone.

Do you offer coverage for self-employed workers and 1099 contractors?

Yes — this is one of our specialties. Rideshare drivers, contractors, salon and restaurant workers, and small business owners across Cape Coral and Fort Myers make up a large part of our client base. What happens if my claim is denied or my family needs help filing?

We provide full claims advocacy. Our licensed staff contacts the carrier, gathers the required documentation, submits the claim on your beneficiary's behalf and follows up until it is paid. Your family never has to navigate carrier call centers or paperwork alone.

What if I have health conditions like diabetes or high blood pressure?
