Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Miami, Florida

The questions our agents hear most often from young adults, families and retirees across Miami-Dade County.

How much does life insurance cost for a young adult in Miami, Florida?

In 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Miami resident typically pays about $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year level term coverage, and roughly $28–$36 per month for $500,000. A healthy 45-year-old pays about $58–$75 per month for $500,000 over 20 years. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally run $32–$95 monthly. Your exact rate depends on age, height and weight, tobacco use, health history and the carrier's underwriting class — which is why we quote multiple carriers instead of one.

Do I need a medical exam to get approved?

Not always. Many of our carriers offer accelerated underwriting, which can approve healthy applicants up to age 60 with no exam — just a health questionnaire and prescription/database check. Policies are often issued in 48 hours to a few business days. If you have diabetes, COPD, prior heart issues or take multiple medications, we route you to guaranteed issue or simplified issue carriers that ask no health questions at all, with approval in as little as 24 hours.

What is the best life insurance policy for young adults in Miami Florida if I'm self-employed or a 1099 contractor?

For most self-employed Miami professionals under 40, level term insurance wins on cost-per-dollar of protection: 20 or 30 years of guaranteed level premiums that no one can cancel as long as you pay. If you want coverage that never expires and builds cash value you can borrow from later, an Indexed Universal Life or whole life policy makes sense. Many of our gig-economy clients do both — a large term base plus a smaller permanent policy. We model three scenarios with real numbers so you can see the trade-off yourself.

How long does the whole process take from first call to issued policy?

Quote delivery is same-day in most cases — often within 45 minutes during business hours. Accelerated-underwriting term applications are frequently approved in 48 hours to 5 business days. Fully underwritten policies requiring labs and medical records typically take 2–4 weeks, and we track every step and chase records on your behalf. Final expense and guaranteed issue products are usually approved within 24–72 hours.

Are your consultations and quotes really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation and no hidden fees. Our agency is compensated by the insurance carrier when a policy is issued, never by the client. That means our quotes, needs analysis, subsidy calculations, annual policy reviews and enrollment assistance cost you nothing. You are also never asked to commit during a first appointment; we send information and let you decide on your own timeline.

Can you review a policy I already bought from another agency?

Absolutely, and it is one of our most popular free services. Bring your declarations page or policy documents and we will audit coverage amount, term length, premium, riders, beneficiary designations and cash value performance. Clients regularly discover they are overpaying for identical coverage or that a beneficiary form was never updated after a marriage or divorce. If we find a lower rate for identical coverage, our rate-shopping guarantee means we match it or explain precisely why the difference exists.

What are my health insurance options if I have no employer coverage in Miami?

You have three main paths. First, ACA Marketplace plans, where most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits and frequently pay under $50 per month after subsidies. Second, private short-term or association plans if you miss Open Enrollment and do not qualify for a Special Enrollment Period. Third, group benefits if you own a business with 5–50 employees. We are also licensed for Florida KidCare enrollment help at no cost. Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15 each year.

When can I enroll in Medicare or change my plan?

Your Initial Enrollment Period begins three months before your 65th birthday month and runs three months after. Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7 each year for Advantage and Part D changes. Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs January 1 through March 31. During Annual Enrollment we offer evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM so you never have to take time off work to review your options.

Do you offer service in Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Yes. Our agency has three dedicated bilingual support staff and Spanish- and Haitian Creole-speaking licensed agents. Documents, policy explanations, enrollment forms and claims assistance are all available in your preferred language. Our Little Havana office at 2740 SW 8th Street was chosen specifically because it is easy to reach for families across Miami-Dade, and we also serve clients by phone and video statewide.
