Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Orlando, Florida

Updated for 2026 enrollment rules, ACA subsidy changes, and Medicare costs in Orange County.

How much does it cost to work with a health insurance agent in Orlando?
Nothing. Consultations, quotes, enrollment help and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Our commissions are paid directly by the insurance carriers — Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Ambetter and others — never by you. In practice, working with an independent agent usually costs less than buying direct, because we can check subsidy eligibility and compare 10+ carriers at once.
I'm self-employed with 1099 income. Do I qualify for an ACA subsidy in Orlando?
In most cases, yes. Eligibility is based on your projected household income relative to the Federal Poverty Level, not on your employment status. Most self-employed clients we help in Orlando, Florida qualify for premium tax credits, and many households end up paying under $50 a month after subsidies. We calculate your number using your projected 2026 net income — not your gross — which often makes a significant difference.
How quickly can I get covered?
ACA Marketplace plans submitted before the 15th of the month typically start the 1st of the following month. Medicare Advantage and Part D enrollments during Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) take effect January 1. Term life usually issues in 3–10 days with a medical exam, or as fast as 24–48 hours with accelerated no-exam underwriting. Our average quote turnaround is under two business hours.
What if I already have a policy — will you still review it?
Absolutely. Our free no-obligation policy review covers policies you bought anywhere, from any carrier or agent. We'll check your beneficiary designations, look for coverage gaps after a marriage, divorce, new baby or home purchase, and tell you honestly if what you have is already competitive. If it is, we'll say so.
Are you licensed to sell insurance in Orlando and across Florida?
Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). We are an A+ Better Business Bureau accredited agency since 2015, a NABIP member, and all agents complete annual continuing education plus AHIP Medicare certification. Life insurance is available statewide; health and Medicare service focuses on Central and South Florida.
Do you offer bilingual service?
Yes — full phone and appointment support in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. Three of our eleven staff members are dedicated bilingual support specialists, and we host a free bilingual Medicare workshop at our Little Havana office on the first Wednesday of every month, open to any Florida resident at no cost.
Can I get life insurance if I have health problems?
Often, yes. Depending on the condition, we can place you with a carrier that rates it favorably, or use simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue products. Guaranteed Issue Life is available for ages 50–85 with no health questions at all — approval is based only on age and coverage amount. Final expense no-exam options for ages 45–85 typically run $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage.
What does "independent agent" actually mean for me?
It means we are not captive to one insurance company. We hold appointments with Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar, Simply Healthcare, Doctor's Health Plan, Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica and more. If a plan isn't right for you, we simply recommend a different one — our income doesn't depend on steering you into a single carrier's product.
How do I know my doctors and hospitals are in the network?
Before you enroll, we verify that your preferred providers — AdventHealth, Orlando Health, Nemours, or a specific specialist in Winter Park or Lake Nona — are in-network under the exact plan you're considering. Network verification is part of every enrollment we handle, and we document it in writing so there are no surprises at your first appointment.
Do you handle claims if something happens to me?
Yes. Claims advocacy is included at no charge. Our team calls the carrier, files and follows up on paperwork, and coordinates directly with your beneficiaries during an already difficult time. One family we assisted had a $15,000 final expense claim denied twice before we took it over — it paid out in 11 days.

Our team is available 24/7 by phone at +1-540-352-6249. Standard office hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM EST and Saturday 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM by appointment. During ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15) and Medicare AEP (Oct 15 – Dec 7), we add evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM EST.
Is there any pressure or obligation if I just want to compare numbers?
None. No client is ever asked to commit during the first appointment — that's a written agency policy, not marketing language. We follow an education-first approach: you'll learn how underwriting, ACA premium tax credits and Medicare enrollment periods actually work, take the comparison home, and decide on your own timeline.
