How much does life insurance cost in St. Petersburg, Florida?
A healthy 30-year-old typically pays $18–$24 per month for a $250,000, 20-year term policy. A healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense coverage runs $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000. Florida has no state income tax, which often leaves more room in a household budget for coverage — and our agents shop 8 life carriers to find your lowest issued rate.
Do you charge a fee for quotes or consultations?
Never. Consultations, quotes, policy comparisons and annual reviews are 100% free with zero obligation. Our compensation comes from the insurance carrier, not from you — so the premium you pay is the same as buying direct, and you get a licensed advocate at no additional cost.
How fast can my policy be approved?
No-exam final expense and guaranteed-issue policies are frequently issued within 24–72 hours. Fully underwritten term life generally takes 2–4 weeks depending on medical records and exam scheduling. We respond to every quote request within 24 hours, and most come back the same business day.
What parts of St. Petersburg and Pinellas County do you serve?
All of Pinellas County — Downtown St. Pete, Old Northeast, Snell Isle, Kenwood, Crescent Lake, Coquina Key, Tyrone, Shore Acres, Pinellas Point, Gulfport, St. Pete Beach, Treasure Island, Tierra Verde, Pinellas Park, Largo, Seminole and Clearwater — plus Tampa, Bradenton and statewide Florida for life insurance.
I turn 65 this year. When should I start Medicare enrollment?
Start three months before your 65th birthday month. That's when your Initial Enrollment Period opens, and it's the best window to compare Medicare Advantage versus Medigap without underwriting delays. We're AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant, and we compare Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna and Cigna plans using the doctors and hospitals you already use in Pinellas County.
Can I get ACA Marketplace subsidies if I live in St. Petersburg?
Most households in Pinellas County qualify for premium tax credits. After subsidies, many of our clients pay under $50 per month — and some qualify for $0-premium bronze or silver plans. We complete the healthcare.gov application with you, verify your income projection, and re-check your plan every November during Open Enrollment at no cost.
What happens if a claim is denied or my family needs help filing?
We act as your claims advocate. Our office contacts the carrier, files the required paperwork and follows the claim through to payment on your beneficiary's behalf — at no charge. This service is included with every policy we place and is one of the clearest reasons to work with a local insurance agency instead of an online quote engine.
Is your agency licensed and accredited?
Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Only). We are A+ accredited by the Better Business Bureau (since 2015), members of NABIP, AHIP Medicare-certified annually, and carry a 4.9-star Google rating across 300+ reviews. Absolutely, and it's free. We review your death benefit, cash value, riders, premium and beneficiary designations, then show you in writing whether a newer carrier or product would save money or increase coverage. If your current policy is already the best fit, we'll tell you that too.
