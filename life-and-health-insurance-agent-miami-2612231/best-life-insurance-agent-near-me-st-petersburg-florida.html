How much does life insurance cost in St. Petersburg, Florida?

Pricing depends on age, health and term length. In 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in St. Petersburg can typically qualify for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old usually pays $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies range from roughly $32 to $95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. We quote 8+ carriers so you can see the real spread, not one company's rate card.

Yes — 100% free, with no fees, no obligation and no cost to you at any point. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier when a policy is issued, not by clients. That means the quote you receive is the same rate you would get by going directly to the carrier.

Most quote requests receive a same-day or next-business-day response under our 24-hour response guarantee.
