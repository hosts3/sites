Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in Hialeah, Florida?

For identical coverage from the same carrier, the price is the same either way — Florida agents are not permitted to mark up life insurance premiums, and commissions are paid by the carrier. What changes is what happens around the price. An independent Hialeah agent shops multiple carriers, catches health-history details that would trigger a rate increase or a decline, and can re-shop you if underwriting comes back worse than expected. Many clients who thought they were saving money online ended up paying more because the instant-decision engine placed them in a higher rate class.

How much does term life insurance cost in Hialeah in 2026?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old in Hialeah can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for roughly $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old looking at $500,000 of 20-year term usually falls between $58 and $75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 range from about $32 to $95 per month depending on age and coverage amount. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use and the carrier's underwriting rules — which is why we quote more than one.

Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance in Florida?

Not always. Many of the carriers we represent offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for healthy applicants under 55, depending on the coverage amount. If an exam is required, we schedule it at your home in Hialeah at no cost to you and the examiner comes to you.
