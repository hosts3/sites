How do I choose a life insurance agent in Fort Lauderdale, Florida?

Start by verifying the agent's Florida Department of Financial Services license — a 2-15 Life, Health & Annuity license is required to sell life insurance in Florida. Confirm they are independent rather than captive to a single carrier, ask how many carriers they represent, and request a written comparison of at least three quotes. Our agency holds Florida license 2-15, represents eight life carriers, and provides a written comparison before you commit to anything.

How much does life insurance cost in Fort Lauderdale in 2026?

A healthy 30-year-old Fort Lauderdale resident typically pays about $18 to $24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage. A healthy 45-year-old can expect roughly $58 to $75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense policies for ages 45 to 85 generally run $32 to $95 per month for $5,000 to $25,000 of coverage. Your exact rate depends on health history and tobacco use.

Are consultations with your insurance agency really free?

Yes — every consultation, quote and annual policy review is 100% free with no obligation. Our agency is compensated by the insurance carrier that issues your policy, never by you. You will never be charged a fee, and no client is ever pressured to commit during a first appointment. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

How fast can I get life insurance coverage in Fort Lauderdale?

Guaranteed issue and final expense policies can be approved in as little as 24 to 48 hours with no medical exam. Fully underwritten term and whole life policies with a medical exam usually take 2 to 4 weeks. We respond to every quote request within 24 hours, and in most cases the same business day.

Do you offer bilingual insurance services in Fort Lauderdale?

What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents one insurance company only, so they can offer just that carrier's rates and underwriting rules. An independent agent like ours represents many carriers — including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, John Hancock, Transamerica, North American, Nationwide and Gerber Life — and can shop your health profile across all of them to find the lowest legitimate rate.

Can you help me with Medicare in Fort Lauderdale?

We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans from carriers including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare and Doctor's Health Plan. Many Broward County Medicare Advantage plans are available at a $0 monthly premium.
