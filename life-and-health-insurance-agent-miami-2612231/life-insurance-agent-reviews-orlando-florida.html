Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Orlando, Florida

Updated for 2026. These are the questions Orlando clients ask us most often — answered directly, with real numbers.

How much does it cost to work with an insurance agent in Orlando?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews, and enrollment help are completely free, and agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier — not by you. That means using our agency costs you the same as buying directly from a carrier, except you get an advocate who compares 18+ carriers for you.

How quickly can I get life insurance coverage in Orlando, Florida?

With accelerated underwriting, many applicants receive a decision in 24 to 72 hours and coverage can begin the same week. Fully underwritten policies requiring bloodwork and medical records typically take two to four weeks. Final expense and guaranteed issue plans are usually faster, often three to seven business days.

Are the life insurance agent reviews in Orlando, Florida on this page verified?

Yes. Our agency carries a 4.9-star average across 300+ Google reviews and has been A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015. Reviews on this page are from real Orlando-area clients with their permission, and you can verify our license status at any time through the Florida Department of Financial Services.

What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only offer that company's products. An independent agent like ours is contracted with 18+ carriers — including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Transamerica, Americo, Nationwide, and Gerber Life for life insurance — so we can compare pricing and underwriting side by side instead of pushing a single option.

Do you offer no-medical-exam life insurance for Orlando residents?

Yes. Many applicants under 60 with manageable health histories qualify for fully accelerated underwriting with no exam at all. For ages 50–85, we also offer guaranteed issue final expense policies that require no health questions — approval is based on age and coverage amount only. Premiums for final expense typically run $32 to $95 per month.

Can you help me with Medicare if I am turning 65 in Orlando this year?

Absolutely. Our agents are AHIP Medicare certified and CMS-compliant every year. We compare Medicare Advantage and Medigap plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, and Simply Healthcare, and we check that your Orlando-area physicians and hospitals stay in network. Many Advantage plans in Orange County are available at a $0 monthly premium.

What if I already have a life insurance policy I bought somewhere else?

Bring it in. Our free no-obligation policy review covers policies purchased from any agent or carrier, including term, whole life, and final expense contracts. We look at premium, death benefit, cash value, riders, and beneficiary designations — then tell you honestly if you are overpaying or if your current policy is a better fit than anything we can offer.

Do you charge a fee if I find a lower rate somewhere else?

No, and we go a step further: we offer a rate-shopping guarantee. If you find identical coverage for a lower premium from another carrier, we will either match that rate or give you a written explanation of exactly why the difference exists — for example, different underwriting class or a missing rider. No pressure and no penalties either way.

How do you help my family if I pass away and a claim needs to be filed?

Claims advocacy is included with every policy we place. Our staff contacts the carrier, requests the correct forms, submits the certified death certificate, tracks the claim status, and follows up weekly until the benefit is paid. Beneficiaries never have to chase an insurance company alone — they can call us at +1-540-352-6249 and we take it from there.
