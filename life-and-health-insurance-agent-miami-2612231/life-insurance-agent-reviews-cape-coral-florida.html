Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Cape Coral, Florida

Updated for 2026 — the questions Cape Coral clients ask us most often about pricing, timelines, credentials and coverage.

How much does a life insurance agent cost in Cape Coral, Florida?

Nothing. Our consultations, quotes and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no obligation. Agent commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier, never by the client. You pay exactly the same premium whether you buy through us or directly from the carrier.

What are real life insurance rates in Cape Coral right now in 2026?

A healthy 30-year-old can get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage from about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old may pay roughly $58–$75 monthly for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense plans for ages 45–85 typically run $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage.

How fast can I get covered — what is your typical timeline?

Most Cape Coral clients complete the free assessment in one 15-minute call. Fully underwritten term policies are usually issued in 7–14 days, while Guaranteed Issue and final expense policies with no medical exam can be approved in 24–72 hours.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify for coverage?

Not always. Term and permanent life policies above $250,000 usually require a paramedical exam, but final expense and Guaranteed Issue Life Insurance (ages 50–85, no health questions) do not. We tell you upfront which carriers will approve you without an exam.

Are you a licensed insurance agency in Florida?

Yes. Biscayne Bay Insurance Group, LLC holds Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health). We are AHIP Medicare and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified annually, CMS-compliant, NABIP members, and A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015.

What is your 24-hour response guarantee?

Every quote request and client question receives a response within 24 hours — our internal average is under 4 business hours. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) we also run evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM EST.

Can you help if I already have a policy with another company?

Absolutely. Our free policy review covers policies you did not purchase through us. We check the death benefit, cash value performance, beneficiary designations, riders and premium against current 2026 market rates — then tell you honestly whether switching is worth it.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Cape Coral?

Yes. We compare Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna and Cigna. Many Advantage plans are $0 monthly, and Medigap Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month in this region depending on age.

What if I'm self-employed or a 1099 gig worker with no benefits?

You are our most common client. We combine term life for income replacement with an ACA Marketplace plan — most Florida households qualify for premium tax credits, and many pay under $50 per month after subsidies. Florida KidCare enrollment help is also free.

Do you offer emergency or same-day help?

Yes. Call +1-540-352-6249 and we triage same-day for urgent situations such as a hospitalization, a lapsed policy or an enrollment deadline. Our phone support line is monitored 24/7 with bilingual English, Spanish and Haitian Creole coverage.

What guarantees do you offer on price and service?

We offer a rate-shopping guarantee: if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, we match it or explain the difference in writing. Every client also receives a free annual policy review, claims advocacy, and a no-pressure consultation where you are never asked to commit on the first appointment.

How does your agency compare to buying online or through a captive agent?

Captive agents can only quote one carrier. Online quote engines rarely verify beneficiary details or handle claims. As an independent agency we quote 18+ carriers, verify your paperwork, and stay involved at claim time — which is when reviews like ours actually get earned.
