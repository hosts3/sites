Life insurance, by contrast, is licensed statewide, so an Orlando client can apply from a hotel in Miami or a winter home in Naples without changing agents.

How much does a life insurance agent cost in Orlando, Florida?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes and policy reviews are completely free, and our commissions are paid by the insurance carrier after a policy issues — never by you. You pay the same published premium whether you work with us or apply directly, so there is no financial downside to using an independent agent.

How fast can I get life insurance coverage in Orlando?

Fully underwritten term policies usually take 2–4 weeks, but many of our clients qualify for accelerated or no-exam underwriting with Mutual of Omaha, Americo or Foresters and are approved in as little as 24–72 hours. Guaranteed-issue final expense plans frequently issue within 48 hours of application.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify for term life insurance?

Not always. Applicants under 60 in reasonable health often qualify for no-exam coverage up to $500,000, and simplified-issue products ask only a short health questionnaire. We check which carrier treats your specific condition — diabetes, sleep apnea, high blood pressure — most favorably before submitting anything.

What does a health insurance agent in Orlando actually do during open enrollment?

We review your household income to calculate your premium tax credit, compare ACA Marketplace plans by deductible, network and drug formulary, then submit the application and confirm your subsidy is applied correctly. ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15, and we help with Florida KidCare year-round at no cost.

When can I enroll in Medicare Advantage or a Medigap plan in Orlando?

Your Initial Enrollment Period is the seven months around your 65th birthday. Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7 each year, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs January 1 through March 31. We offer evening video appointments Monday–Thursday until 8:00 PM during enrollment season.

What is your guarantee if I am not satisfied with a recommendation?

You are never asked to commit during a first appointment, and every life insurance policy includes the carrier's free-look period — typically 10 days in Florida — during which you can cancel for a full refund. We also provide a rate-shopping guarantee: find a lower rate for identical coverage and we will match it or explain the difference in writing.

Can you review a policy I already bought from another agent?

Yes, and there is no charge for it. In 2025 we reviewed more than 600 existing Florida policies and found that roughly one in three clients was paying for riders they did not need, had an outdated beneficiary listed, or qualified for a better rate class than the one they were issued.

Do you help with claims if something happens to me?

Yes. Claims advocacy is included at no cost for the life of the policy. We contact the carrier, confirm the required documents, follow up on timelines and stay on the phone with your beneficiaries so a grieving family in Orlando never has to navigate a claims department alone.
