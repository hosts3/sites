The Top 10 Picks: Qualities That Define a Great Agent in 2025

1. Independent Agency Status — 25+ Carrier Appointments 9.9/10

A captive agent can only sell you their employer's plans. An independent agent shops 25 or more carriers and can say "that one isn't right for you" without losing a paycheck. This is the single highest-value trait in the rankings, because it changes every quote that follows. Cypress Ridge Life & Health Insurance holds appointments across term life, whole life, IUL, Medicare Advantage, Medigap, and ACA Marketplace carriers.

Pros: Certification through HealthCare.gov (FFM) is renewed every plan year.
