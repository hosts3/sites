Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Orlando

Straight answers to the questions our Orlando clients ask most. Still unsure? Call +1-540-352-6249 — there is no charge and no pressure.

How much does it cost to work with Cypress Ridge Life & Health?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, needs analyses, applications and annual reviews are completely free. We charge no broker fees, no monthly service charges and no markup on premiums — insurance carriers pay our commission directly. You pay the same rate you would pay buying straight from the carrier, but with a licensed Orlando agent handling the paperwork and claims.

What is the cheapest life insurance I can get in Orlando as of 2026?

For most healthy applicants, level term life is the lowest-cost option. A healthy 35-year-old in Orlando commonly qualifies for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage in the $18–$25 per month range. Final expense policies, which require no medical exam, typically run $35–$90 per month depending on age and health. Every number is an estimate until we run a real carrier quote against your health history.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Simplified-issue and no-medical-exam policies are often approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term and permanent policies typically take two to four weeks, and can run longer if medical records are requested. We track every application with the carrier and update you at each stage rather than leaving you waiting.

Do you charge extra for Medicare or ACA Marketplace help?

No. As ACA Marketplace certified and AHIP Medicare certified agents, we are compensated by the carriers and plans we enroll you in. Medicare Advantage plans frequently carry a $0 monthly premium, and many Central Florida households qualify for ACA Marketplace coverage between $0 and $50 per month after premium tax credits. You pay nothing to us for the comparison.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. We write no-medical-exam and simplified-issue products for ages 0 to 85, plus guaranteed issue final expense coverage for applicants with serious health conditions. Approval is usually based on a short health questionnaire and prescription history check instead of blood work and a physical, and many Orlando clients are approved the same week.

What happens if I already own a policy from another agency?

We offer a free 15-minute policy review. Bring your current declarations page and we will compare your coverage, premium, riders and carrier rating against current options. Sometimes we tell you to keep exactly what you have — that is a perfectly good outcome. If we recommend a change, you are protected by your new policy's free-look period.

Are you a captive agency tied to one insurance company?

No. Cypress Ridge Life & Health Insurance is an independent agency with more than 25 carrier appointments, including Mutual of Omaha, Americo, Corebridge, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare and Florida Blue. Independence matters because we can compare underwriting outcomes and pricing across companies instead of selling you the single product our employer requires.

What are your guarantees if I am not happy with a policy?

Three guarantees: a zero-pressure guarantee, meaning you never have to buy during a consultation; a 30-day policy review guarantee, where we re-shop a new policy at no cost if it is not the right fit; and full compliance with Florida's free-look period, which is typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product, with a full refund of premium.

Do you help with claims, or do I have to call a 1-800 number?

Your assigned agent personally assists with filing a claim — the same licensed person who wrote your policy. We help gather death certificates, complete carrier forms, and follow the claim through to payment. One Sanford family we served had a $15,000 final expense claim paid out in 12 days, with the agent returning their call within 30 minutes.
