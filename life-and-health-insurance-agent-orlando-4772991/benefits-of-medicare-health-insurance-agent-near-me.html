Why This Decision Matters More Than You Think

Medicare is not one plan. It is a stack of moving parts: Part A hospital coverage, Part B medical coverage, Part D prescriptions, and then a fork in the road between Original Medicare plus a Medigap supplement or a Medicare Advantage plan. Choose well and you keep your doctors, your pharmacy and your budget intact. Choose poorly and you can spend a year fighting prior authorizations — or waiting until the next enrollment window to fix it.

Florida has one of the most crowded Medicare markets in the country. In the Orlando area alone, you can choose from dozens of Medicare Advantage HMO and PPO plans, multiple Medigap carriers, and a separate stack of Part D drug plans. What a Local Medicare Health Insurance Agent Actually Does

An independent agent is a translator, a researcher and an advocate rolled into one. In practice, here is the work that happens behind the scenes when you sit down with us at our Orlando office, meet by phone, or join a Zoom call from your kitchen table:

  • Provider and pharmacy verification. We check that your specific physicians, specialists, hospitals and the pharmacy counter you actually use are in-network for each plan we recommend — not just the plans advertised on television.
  • Drug formulary cross-checking. Every prescription you take gets run through each Part D or Advantage formulary, with quantity limits, prior authorization flags and tier placement reviewed line by line.
  • Cost modeling. We estimate your realistic annual cost — premiums, deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and the annual Part D out-of-pocket cap (currently $2,000) — rather than quoting only the monthly premium.
  • Enrollment paperwork and error prevention. One mistyped enrollment period or an election made during the wrong window can delay coverage by months. We file it correctly the first time.
  • Annual review. Plans change every January. Formularies shift, networks shrink, premiums move. We re-run your comparison each fall at no charge.

Metro Orlando is one of the most competitive Medicare Advantage markets in the United States, and that competition creates genuinely good value — often a $0 monthly premium plan with copays, dental, vision and an over-the-counter allowance. But “$0 premium” is not the same as “$0 cost,” and it is not automatically the right answer for everyone.

Enrollment Windows You Cannot Miss

Most Medicare mistakes are calendar mistakes. Mark these dates:

  • Initial Enrollment Period (IEP): the 7-month window around your 65th birthday — three months before, your birthday month, and three months after. Enroll late and you can face lifelong Part B and Part D late-enrollment penalties.
  • Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (AEP): October 15 – December 7. This is when you can switch Advantage plans or move between Advantage and Original Medicare. During AEP our Orlando office runs extended hours: Monday–Saturday 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM, plus Sunday 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM.
  • Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment: January 1 – March 31, for a one-time change if you are already in an Advantage plan.
  • ACA Marketplace Open Enrollment: typically November 1 – January 15, with Special Enrollment Periods available year-round for qualifying life events like marriage, a move, job loss, or the birth of a child.
  • Medigap guaranteed-issue rights: the six months starting the first month you are 65 and enrolled in Part B. Outside that window, health questions may apply in Florida.
