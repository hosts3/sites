Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Orlando

These are the questions our Orlando clients ask most — answered plainly, with current 2026 figures.

How much does it cost to work with an insurance agent in Orlando?

Nothing. Cypress Ridge charges no consultation fee, no broker fee, no mark-up on premiums and no monthly service charge. Carriers pay our commission directly, so your premium is the same whether you buy through us or on your own — except you get a licensed advocate at no extra cost.

How fast can I get a life insurance policy in Orlando?

Simplified-issue and no-medical-exam policies are often approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term and whole life coverage typically takes two to four weeks depending on how quickly medical records and labs come back. We track every application and update you at each stage.

Do you charge broker fees or mark up my premium?

No. We are compensated only by the insurance carriers, and we will show you the carrier's published rate alongside our quote so you can verify it. There are no application fees, no policy-issue fees added by our agency, and no service charges of any kind.

Can you help if I already have coverage from another agency?

Yes — that's one of our most common requests. We offer a free 15-minute policy review, then compare your current life or health plan against alternatives from our 25+ carrier appointments. If your existing coverage is already strong, we will tell you so and leave it alone.

What's the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only offer that company's products. As an independent agency, Cypress Ridge represents more than 25 carriers, so we can compare rates, networks and underwriting rules without a quota pushing us toward one brand.

Do you have Spanish- or Portuguese-speaking agents in Orlando?

We do. Our bilingual staff serves clients in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and we handle the entire enrollment conversation in your preferred language — including applications, Medicare comparisons and claims paperwork. An alternate Spanish-language line is available at +1-407-555-0187.

How do I know if I qualify for ACA subsidies in Florida?

Eligibility depends on household income relative to the federal poverty level, household size and whether you have an affordable employer offer. As HealthCare.gov-certified agents, we run the calculation for you and verify whether you also qualify for cost-sharing reductions that lower copays and deductibles.

When should I enroll in Medicare if I'm turning 65 in Orlando?

Your Initial Enrollment Period is the seven-month window that begins three months before your 65th birthday month and ends three months after it. Enrolling early avoids Part B and Part D late-enrollment penalties. We run free Turning-65 workshops twice monthly at our East Orlando and Kissimmee locations.

What happens if my claim gets denied?

Your assigned agent personally assists with the claim — you never get handed off to a 1-800 call center. We help gather records, file the appeal, and work directly with the carrier's claims examiner. That claims-help guarantee is included with every policy at no additional cost, forever.

Do you offer evening or in-home appointments?

Yes. Monday through Thursday we hold evening appointments until 8:00 PM, Saturday we meet by appointment from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM, and in-home visits are available throughout Orange, Seminole, Osceola and Lake counties. Virtual appointments by Zoom or phone are offered statewide in Florida.

I'm self-employed or drive for a gig app — can you still help me?

Absolutely. Self-employed workers, 1099 contractors and gig-economy drivers in Orlando are a core part of our practice. We routinely place ACA Marketplace plans with subsidy calculations, short-term medical, disability income and term life coverage built around irregular monthly income.

How does your 30-day policy review guarantee work?

If a new policy isn't the fit we expected — the rate class came back different, a prescription isn't covered, or your doctor left the network — we re-shop it at no cost to you within 30 days. We also honor Florida's free-look period on every policy, typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product, with a full premium refund.

What are your hours, and are walk-ins welcome?

We're open Monday through Friday 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM and Saturday 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM by appointment. Walk-ins are welcome at 1214 E. Robinson Street, Suite 210, Orlando, FL 32801, though appointments are preferred. Calls after hours are returned within one business day.
