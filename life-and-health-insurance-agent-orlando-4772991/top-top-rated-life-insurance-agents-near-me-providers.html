Frequently Asked Questions About Orlando Life and Health Insurance Agents

Does it cost anything to work with a life and health insurance agent in Orlando?

No. At independent agencies such as Cypress Ridge Life & Health Insurance, consultations, quotes, needs analyses, applications, and annual reviews are free to you. Agents are paid commissions directly by the insurance carriers, and there are no broker fees, premium mark-ups, or monthly service charges.

How much does term life insurance cost in Orlando?

As a rough estimate only, a healthy 35-year-old may see 20-year term quotes around $18–$25 per month for $250,000 of coverage. Final expense policies typically range from $35 to $90 per month depending on age and health. These are illustrations — your actual rate requires a quote and, in many cases, underwriting.

What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents one insurance company and can only quote that company's products. An independent agent holds appointments with multiple carriers — often 20 or more — and can compare underwriting guidelines and pricing across companies. For the top top rated life insurance agents near me providers Life and Health Insurance agent searches, independent agencies generally give you more options at no extra cost.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. No-medical-exam and simplified-issue options are available for ages 0–85. Approval usually relies on a health questionnaire and database checks instead of bloodwork and a paramedical exam. Coverage amounts may be lower and premiums slightly higher than fully underwritten policies, but the process is considerably faster.

Do you offer appointments outside normal business hours in Orlando?

Evening and in-home appointments are available Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM, Saturday appointments run 9:00 AM–2:00 PM by request, and virtual appointments are offered statewide in Florida by Zoom or phone. During the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 – December 7), extended hours run Monday–Saturday 8:00 AM–8:00 PM plus Sunday 12:00 PM–5:00 PM.

I already have coverage from another agency. Can you review it?

Yes — a free 15-minute policy review is available to anyone who already owns life or health insurance from another agency. Many Orlando policyholders discover they are paying for duplicate coverage, an outdated term that no longer fits, or a Medigap plan priced above current market rates. There is no obligation to change anything.

What happens if my policy is not the right fit?

Cypress Ridge offers a 30-day policy review guarantee: if a newly issued policy is not the right fit, the agency re-shops it at no cost. Florida's free-look period is also honored on every policy — typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product — with a full refund of premium if you cancel within that window.

Do you help file a claim, or do I call the insurance company myself?

Your assigned agent personally assists with filing the claim — you are not routed to a 1-800 call center. That single difference is why many Central Florida families stay with the same agent for life and health coverage over multiple decades and multiple moves within Orange, Seminole, and Osceola counties.
