What to Look For: How to Judge a Final Expense Agent

Final expense insurance — also called burial insurance or funeral insurance — is a small whole life policy, usually $5,000 to $40,000, designed to cover funeral costs, medical bills left behind, and small debts. The product is fairly simple. The agent is the variable. Here is what separates a genuinely good one from a fast-talking one.

1. Independence vs. Captivity

A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only sell that company's rates. An independent agent holds appointments with many carriers and can shop your health profile across all of them. If you have diabetes, COPD, a past heart attack, or take multiple medications, an independent agent may literally save you hundreds of dollars a year on identical coverage.

2. Reviews That Mention Specifics

Genuine reviews tend to mention concrete details: a claim that was paid, a premium reduced after a re-shop, a specific agent by name, a time of day. Reviews that say only "great service, very professional!" with no detail are often incentivized. On Google, look for review histories that stretch back several years rather than a burst of 20 five-star ratings in one month.

3. Willingness to Show Written Quotes

Any reputable agent will email you a carrier illustration showing the premium, the death benefit, the graded-benefit period, and the effective date. If an agent resists putting numbers in writing, that is your cue to walk away.

4. Claims Help, Not a Call Center

Ask directly: "When I die, who does my family call?" The answer you want is a named agent, not a 1-800 number. At Cypress Ridge, the assigned agent personally assists with the claim paperwork — that is written into our service promise.

5. Licensing You Can Verify in 60 Seconds

Florida makes this easy. Every licensed agent has a public record through the Florida Department of Financial Services. Search the agent's name or license number at the state's licensee lookup. Ours is Life & Health Agent License #W284917. Also confirm the agency carries Errors & Omissions (E&O) coverage — we are background-checked, bonded, and E&O insured.

What the Tiers Mean

Tier 1 — Preferred (about $35–$60/month). Best health, no major conditions, level death benefit from day one, full cash value growth. This is the cheapest money in final expense insurance.

Tier 2 — Simplified Issue (about $45–$90/month). A handful of health conditions that are well controlled. No medical exam, usually a short phone interview, approval in 24–72 hours. Still a level, day-one death benefit with most carriers.

Tier 3 — Guaranteed Issue (about $70–$130/month). No health questions at all, so approval is guaranteed for ages 50–85. The trade-off is a graded death benefit: if you pass away in the first two years, the beneficiary typically receives premiums paid plus interest rather than the full face amount.

What You Should Never Pay For

  • Consultation fees — consultations, quotes, needs analyses and annual reviews should cost you $0.
  • Broker fees or premium mark-ups — the carrier pays the agent's commission set by the state.
  • Monthly "service charges" — a red flag for an agency charging you to maintain a policy you already own.
  • Application or processing fees — none of these should appear on a legitimate final expense policy.

The one legitimate fee you might encounter is with a fee-only consultant (see Pick #5), and that is disclosed up front by definition.

Expert Tips: Mistakes to Avoid

  • Don't buy the first quote you hear. We routinely find $15–$40 a month in savings on the exact same coverage simply by checking two or three more carriers.
  • Don't lie about tobacco or health history. Two years of contestability apply to material misstatements. Claims get denied over a single undisclosed diagnosis.
  • Don't over-insure. Most Orlando funeral homes quote $8,000–$14,000 for a full traditional service with burial. Add outstanding medical debt and you have your realistic number — not the highest figure an agent suggests.
  • Don't replace an old policy without a side-by-side. Florida's free-look and replacement rules protect you, but a new two-year graded period could be worse than the policy you already own.
  • Don't skip the beneficiary details. Name a primary and a contingent beneficiary, and include dates of birth. Ambiguous beneficiary forms are the number-one cause of delayed claims.
  • Don't disappear after the sale. Ask for an annual review. At Cypress Ridge, every client is offered a free yearly coverage review — and we will re-shop a policy at no cost within 30 days if it turns out not to be the right fit.

3. Can I get final expense coverage if I have diabetes, COPD or a past heart attack?

Usually yes. Many carriers accept well-controlled diabetes and other conditions at simplified-issue rates. More serious histories may qualify for guaranteed issue, where you cannot be turned down — though the death benefit is typically graded for the first two years.

4. Do I need a medical exam?

Rarely. Most final expense policies are simplified issue: a short health questionnaire and sometimes a brief phone interview, with approval in 24–72 hours. Guaranteed-issue policies for ages 50–85 require no health questions at all.

5. How much coverage do I actually need in Orlando?

Start with your realistic funeral cost — commonly $8,000–$14,000 in Central Florida for a traditional service with burial — then add outstanding medical bills, credit card balances or any debt you would not want to leave behind. Many Orlando clients settle between $10,000 and $20,000.

6. How fast are claims paid?

For a level, day-one policy that is past the contestability period, most carriers pay within 5–10 business days once the death certificate and claim form are received. Our clients' families call their assigned agent directly — not a 1-800 call center — and we help complete the paperwork.

7. What if I change my mind after signing?

You are protected. Florida's free-look period lets you cancel for a full refund of premium, typically within 10–30 days depending on the product. On top of that, we offer a 30-day policy review guarantee: if a new policy is not the right fit, we re-shop it at no cost to you.
