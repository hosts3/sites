Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Orlando

These are the questions our Orlando agents answer every week. If yours is not here, call +1-540-352-6249 — a licensed agent, not a script reader, will pick up.

How much does it cost to use a Life and Health Insurance agent in Orlando?
Nothing. There is no fee for consultations, quotes, needs analyses, applications or annual reviews. We are paid commissions directly by the insurance carriers, and there is no mark-up on your premium, no broker fee and no monthly service charge. You pay the same carrier rate whether you work with us or apply directly.
How fast can I get a quote or an appointment?
Most quote requests are returned the same business day, and our commitment is a response within one business day. Same-week appointments are usually available, including evening appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM and in-home visits. No-medical-exam life policies can be approved in as little as 24–48 hours.
Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance?
Not always. We place no-medical-exam and simplified-issue products for applicants from ages 0 to 85. Fully underwritten policies may require a paramedical exam and typically offer the lowest rates for healthy applicants. During your free analysis we will show you both paths side by side with real premiums so you can decide.
What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap?
Medicare Advantage (HMO/PPO) replaces Original Medicare with a network-based plan that often has a $0 monthly premium but carries copays and prior authorizations. Medigap supplements Original Medicare, letting you see any provider nationwide who accepts Medicare, usually for a higher monthly premium. We compare both, plus Part D drug plans, and verify your doctors and prescriptions first.
Can you help if I already have coverage from another agency?
Yes — that is one of our most requested services. We offer a free 15-minute policy review and will tell you honestly whether you are overpaying or underinsured. If your current policy is competitive, we will say so. If not, we re-shop it across 25+ carriers at no cost and you decide whether to switch.
Are your agents licensed and how are they certified?
Every agent holds a Florida Department of Financial Services Life & Health license (agency license #W284917). Our Medicare-writing agents are AHIP certified and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified annually, and we are ACA Marketplace certified through HealthCare.gov for each plan year. The agency is bonded with errors-and-omissions coverage.
I'm self-employed or a gig worker in Orlando — what are my options?
You have three realistic paths: an ACA Marketplace plan with premium tax credits (often $0–$50 per month for Central Florida households), a short-term medical policy if you need a bridge, or a private small-group plan if you have at least one W-2 employee. We run your subsidy numbers before recommending anything, at no charge.
Do you offer bilingual appointments in Orlando?
Yes. Our staff is fluent in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and bilingual agents are available for in-home, office and virtual appointments. We also host financial literacy workshops through the Hispanic Chamber of Commerce of Metro Orlando and Turning-65 Medicare workshops twice monthly at our East Orlando and Kissimmee locations.
What happens if I need to file a claim?
Your assigned agent personally assists with the claim — you are not routed to a 1-800 call center. We help gather the death certificate or medical documentation, submit to the carrier, follow up until the claim is paid, and can coordinate with beneficiaries who live out of state. Claims help is included at no charge for the life of your policy.
What is a free-look period and does it apply to me?
Yes. Florida law gives you a free-look period on every policy — typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product — during which you can cancel for a full refund of premium. In addition, our own 30-day policy review guarantee means that if a new policy is not the right fit, we will re-shop it at no cost to you.
When is Medicare Annual Enrollment, and can you help outside of it?
Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7 each year; we extend hours to Monday–Saturday 8:00 AM–8:00 PM plus Sunday 12:00–5:00 PM during that window. Outside enrollment you can still change plans for qualifying life events, and we handle year-round Part D reviews and Medigap applications.
Do you serve clients outside Orlando or outside Central Florida?
In person, we serve Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, Polk and Brevard counties — including Winter Park, Kissimmee, Sanford, Clermont, Oviedo, Celebration and Melbourne. Statewide, we meet by Zoom or phone. We are licensed in Florida only, so we cannot write policies for other states.
Is there any pressure to buy on the first call?
None. Our zero-pressure guarantee means no obligation to purchase during any consultation. You can take the quotes, think about it, talk it over with family and come back. Many clients call us in January and enroll in March — that is completely fine with us.
