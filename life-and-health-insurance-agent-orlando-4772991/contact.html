Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance agent in Orlando

These are the questions our Orlando agents hear most often, answered plainly. Have a question that is not listed? Call +1-540-352-6249 and we will answer it in person — no obligation, no pressure.

How much does it cost to contact a life and health insurance agent in Orlando?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, needs analyses, applications and annual reviews are completely free to you. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carriers, so there are no broker fees, no mark-ups on your premium and no monthly service charges — ever.

How fast can I get a life insurance policy issued in Orlando?

Simplified-issue and no-medical-exam policies are frequently approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term policies with labs and medical records typically take two to four weeks. We tell you the expected timeline for your specific age, health history and carrier before you apply.

Do you charge for a policy review if I already have coverage?

No. Our 15-minute policy review is complimentary, even if your current policy was written by a different agency. We compare your existing plan against 25+ carriers and tell you honestly whether switching or keeping what you have is the better financial decision.

Are your agents licensed to sell insurance in Florida?

Yes. Cypress Ridge Life & Health Insurance holds Florida Department of Financial Services Life & Health Agent License #W284917. All Medicare-writing agents complete AHIP and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certification annually, and we are ACA Marketplace certified through HealthCare.gov each plan year.

What happens if I change my mind after the policy is issued?

Florida law gives you a free-look period — typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product. If you cancel in writing during that window, you receive a full refund of premium. We also offer a 30-day policy re-shop guarantee at no cost if the plan simply is not the right fit.

Can you help me choose Medicare Advantage, Medigap or Part D in Orlando?

Absolutely. We compare Medicare Advantage HMO and PPO plans, Medicare Supplement Plan G and Plan N, and standalone Part D drug plans from multiple carriers. Before recommending anything, we check your physicians, your hospital and every prescription against each plan's network and formulary.

Who helps me if I need to file a claim?

Your assigned agent does — not a 1-800 call center. Claims support is part of our written service promise. Call the same number you used to enroll and the licensed agent who wrote your policy walks the claim through the paperwork and carrier follow-up with you personally.

Do you offer evening or weekend appointments in Orlando?

Yes. We hold evening appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM, in-home visits across Orange, Seminole and Osceola counties, Saturday appointments by request from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM, and virtual Zoom or phone appointments statewide in Florida. Same-week availability is typical.

Can you write small-group health benefits for my Orlando business?

Yes. For groups of 2 to 50 employees we provide a complimentary benefits benchmark report comparing your current group plan against three alternatives, including dental, vision, short and long-term disability, critical illness and hospital indemnity coverage. There is no consulting fee for the report.

Do you speak Spanish or Portuguese?

Sí. Our downtown Orlando office is staffed in English, Spanish and Portuguese. Call our Spanish-language line at +1-407-555-0187 and a bilingual licensed agent will assist with any life insurance, ACA Marketplace or Medicare question you have.

What is the difference between an independent agent and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents one insurance company. We are independent with 25+ carrier appointments, so we can compare pricing, underwriting flexibility and benefits side by side — and if your health or needs change, we can re-shop your coverage rather than cancel and start over.
