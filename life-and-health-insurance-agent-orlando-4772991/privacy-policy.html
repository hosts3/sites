Does it cost anything to work with an independent insurance agent in Orlando?

No. Consultations, quotes, needs analysis, applications, and annual policy reviews are all free to you. Our agents are paid commissions directly by the insurance carriers, and there are no broker fees, no mark-up on premiums, and no monthly service charges. You pay the same premium you would pay buying direct from the carrier — you just get comparison shopping and claim help on top of it.

How much does life insurance cost in Orlando in 2026?

As an estimate that requires a personalized quote: a healthy 35-year-old commonly pays about $18–$25 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage. Final expense and burial policies typically run $35–$90 per month depending on age and health. Permanent whole life and indexed universal life cost significantly more per dollar of coverage. Health history, tobacco use, and coverage amount move the rate more than anything else.

What does your privacy policy mean as a Life and Health Insurance agent?

As a licensed Life and Health Insurance agent we collect only the personal information needed to quote and underwrite coverage — name, address, date of birth, health history, and prescription information. We do not sell your data. Information is shared only with the carriers you authorize us to shop, and each carrier issues its own HIPAA notice. You may request a copy of our full written privacy policy at any time by calling +1-540-352-6249 or emailing hello@cypressridgelifehealth.com.

How fast can I get covered if I need health insurance right now?

ACA Marketplace applications submitted during Open Enrollment or a qualifying Special Enrollment Period can often be effective the first of the following month. Short-term medical plans can start in as little as 24–48 hours. Term life insurance with a no-medical-exam option can be approved in 24–72 hours, while fully underwritten policies typically take 2–4 weeks including the medical exam. During Medicare Annual Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) we run extended hours to move faster.

Are you a captive agent tied to one insurance company?

No — we are an independent agency with more than 25 carrier appointments. That means we can place your coverage with the carrier that fits your health, budget, and doctors rather than the one that pays us the most. If you already own a policy from another agency, our free 15-minute policy review will tell you honestly whether you are overpaying or under-covered. Read more on our about page.
