Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Orlando

Straight answers to the questions our Orlando clients ask most. Don't see yours? Call +1-540-352-6249 and we will answer it in plain English.

How much does life insurance cost in Orlando right now?

As of 2026, a healthy 35-year-old Orlando resident typically sees a 20-year, $250,000 term policy quoted between $18 and $25 per month. Final expense and burial policies generally run $35 to $90 per month depending on age and health. Those are estimates only — your real number requires a quote based on age, tobacco use, medical history and the coverage amount you choose.

Do I pay anything at all for your help as an insurance agent?

No. Consultations, quotes, needs analysis, applications, claim assistance and annual reviews are always free to you. Cypress Ridge is compensated directly by the insurance carriers. There are no broker fees, no mark-ups on premiums and no monthly service charges — the rate you get through us matches the carrier's direct rate.

How fast can I actually get covered?

ACA Marketplace and Medicare Advantage enrollments are usually effective the first of the following month. Simplified-issue life policies with no medical exam are often approved within 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten life policies, which require labs and medical records, typically take two to four weeks from application to approval.

Can you review a policy I already own from another agency?

Yes — and it costs you nothing. Our free 15-minute policy review compares your existing life or health coverage against options from more than 25 carriers. If your current plan is genuinely better, we will tell you to keep it. If it is not, we will show you exactly where the difference is, dollar for dollar.

What's the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a network-based plan, often at a $0 monthly premium with copays and a maximum out-of-pocket limit. Medigap supplements Original Medicare, lets you see any provider nationwide who accepts Medicare, and costs more monthly but leaves very little out-of-pocket. We compare both against your doctors and prescriptions.

Do you offer no-medical-exam life insurance options?

Yes. We write simplified-issue and no-exam products for ages 0 through 85, including coverage for applicants with controlled diabetes, hypertension, sleep apnea or a prior cancer diagnosis. Approval is usually based on a short health questionnaire rather than blood work, which is ideal for people who want protection quickly or who have had trouble qualifying elsewhere.

Are you licensed, bonded and properly insured?

We operate under Florida Department of Financial Services Life & Health Agent License #W284917. Every agent is background-checked and bonded, and the agency carries errors and omissions (E&O) coverage. Our Medicare-writing agents complete AHIP Medicare and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certification each year, and we are certified with HealthCare.gov (FFM) every plan year.

Can you help me file a claim, or do I have to call the carrier myself?

Your assigned agent personally helps you file. That means gathering the death certificate or medical documentation, submitting the forms to the right department, and following up with the carrier until the claim is paid — not handing you a phone number. Our claims help guarantee is a core reason clients stay with us for years.

Do you offer appointments in Spanish or Portuguese?

Yes. Our staff is bilingual in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and we serve many families throughout Orlando, Kissimmee and the Hispanic Chamber of Commerce of Metro Orlando business community. For Spanish-language assistance, call our dedicated line at +1-407-555-0187.

What if I don't qualify for an ACA subsidy?

Even without a premium tax credit, we can often find savings through off-exchange ACA-compliant plans, short-term medical coverage, hospital indemnity, critical illness or accident policies that fill specific gaps at a lower premium. We will show you the honest trade-offs of each option so you can decide what level of protection fits your budget.

Do you work with small businesses in the Orlando area?

Absolutely. We build group medical, dental, vision and disability benefits for employers with 2 to 50 employees, and business owners receive a complimentary benefits benchmark report comparing their current group plan to three alternatives, including level-funded and HSA-qualified high-deductible designs. We also write key-person and buy-sell life coverage for owners.
