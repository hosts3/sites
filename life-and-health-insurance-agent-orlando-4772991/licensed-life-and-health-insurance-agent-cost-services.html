Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent Services in Orlando

Straight answers to what Orlando clients ask us most — pricing, timelines, credentials, guarantees, and how the process really works.

How much does it cost to work with a licensed life and health insurance agent in Orlando?

Nothing. Cypress Ridge charges no consultation, quote, needs-analysis, application, or annual review fees. Our agents are paid by the insurance carriers after a policy is issued, and that commission is already built into the carrier's published rate — so your premium is identical whether you buy through us or directly from the insurer.

Do you charge a broker fee or mark up my premium?

No. There are no broker fees, no mark-ups on premiums, and no monthly service charges. Because we are an independent agency with 25+ carrier appointments, we are paid the same whether you choose carrier A or carrier B — which means the recommendation is based on fit, not commission.

How much does term life insurance cost in Orlando in 2026?

A healthy 35-year-old typically sees $18–$25 per month for a 20-year, $250,000 term policy. A healthy 45-year-old often pays $45–$70 for the same coverage. Final expense policies generally range $35–$90 per month for ages 50–80. Every figure is an estimate until we run a real quote with your age, health, and nicotine status.

Can I really get ACA Marketplace health coverage for $0 per month?

Many Central Florida households do. Premium tax credits often bring a bronze or silver plan down to $0–$50 per month, and cost-sharing reductions can lower deductibles and copays on silver plans. Our agents are HealthCare.gov (FFM) certified every plan year and calculate your subsidy during a free appointment — there is no charge for the calculation.

How fast can I get covered?

Marketplace health plans can be effective the first of the next month if you enroll by the 15th. Simplified-issue and no-medical-exam life policies are frequently approved in 24–72 hours, while fully underwritten policies take two to four weeks. We return every call and email within one business day.

What if I have a health condition or have been declined before?

We write simplified-issue and no-medical-exam life products for ages 0 to 85, and many clients with diabetes, high blood pressure, or a prior decline still qualify — sometimes at standard rates. With 25+ carrier appointments we can shop your case instead of quoting one company and stopping there.

Do you help with Medicare during the Annual Enrollment Period?

Yes. Every Medicare-writing agent on our team is AHIP certified annually and completes Fraud, Waste & Abuse training. From October 15 through December 7 we run extended hours — Monday to Saturday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, plus Sundays 12:00 to 5:00 PM — and we host a free Turning 65 workshop twice a month in East Orlando and Kissimmee.

What happens if I need to file a claim?

Your assigned agent files it with you — you are never handed off to a 1-800 call center. We help gather medical records and documentation, submit the claim, and follow up with the carrier until it is paid. That claims help guarantee is part of our written service commitment to every client.

Are you really independent, and which carriers do you represent?

Yes — Cypress Ridge is a fully independent, non-captive agency with more than 25 carrier appointments across life, health, Medicare, and ancillary products. We are members of the National Association of Insurance and Financial Advisors (NAIFA) and the Florida Association of Insurance Agents (FAIA), and hold Florida Life & Health Agent License #W284917.
