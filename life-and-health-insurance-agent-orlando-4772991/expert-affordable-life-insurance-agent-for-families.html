Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Orlando

Straight answers to the questions Orlando families ask us most. Still unsure? Call +1-540-352-6249 — the consultation is free either way.

How much does it cost to work with an insurance agent in Orlando?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, needs analysis, applications and annual reviews are all free at Cypress Ridge Life & Health. We are paid by the insurance carriers directly, so there are no broker fees, no premium mark-ups and no monthly service charges. Your premium is the same whether you buy through us or online — you just get a local advocate for free.

How much does life insurance cost for a family in Orlando in 2026?

As of 2026, a healthy 35-year-old in Orlando commonly qualifies for a $250,000 20-year term policy starting around $18 to $25 per month. Final expense and burial policies typically range from $35 to $90 per month depending on age and health. Your exact rate always requires a real quote — health history, tobacco use and coverage amount all move the number.

How fast can my family get covered?

Simplified-issue and no-medical-exam policies can be approved in as little as 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten term policies usually take 2 to 4 weeks. For health insurance, ACA Marketplace coverage can begin the first of the following month if you enroll by the 15th, and Medicare Advantage enrollment during a valid election period is often effective the next month.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap in Orlando?

Yes. Every Medicare-writing agent on our team is AHIP certified and completes annual Fraud, Waste & Abuse training. We compare Medicare Advantage HMO and PPO plans, Medigap Plan G and N, and standalone Part D drug plans across 25+ carriers — then check your specific doctors and prescriptions before recommending anything.

Can you help if I already have a policy with another agency?

Absolutely. We offer a free 15-minute policy review with no obligation to switch. Bring your declaration page and we'll benchmark your premium, death benefit, riders and cash value against three alternatives. Many Orlando clients discover they're paying 20–40% more than they need to for identical coverage.

What is your 30-day policy review guarantee?

If a new policy we place doesn't turn out to be the right fit within the first 30 days, we re-shop it at no cost to you. You also keep every Florida free-look right — typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product — which allows a full refund of premium if you change your mind.

Do you offer evening and in-home appointments?

Yes. Evening and in-home appointments are available Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM across Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, Polk and Brevard counties. Saturday appointments run 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM by request, and our downtown Orlando office at 1214 E. Robinson Street, Suite 210 welcomes walk-ins Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Which languages does your Orlando insurance agency speak?

Our team is bilingual in English and Spanish, with Portuguese-speaking support available for Central Florida's Brazilian community. Our alternate line, +1-407-555-0187, connects Spanish-speaking clients directly to a licensed agent — no hold music, no translation delays.

Can you help small-business owners with group health benefits?

Yes. For businesses with 2 to 50 employees we provide a complimentary benefits benchmark report comparing your current group plan with three alternatives. We also quote dental, vision, short- and long-term disability, critical illness, accident and hospital indemnity coverage, plus key-person and buy-sell life policies for owners.

What happens if I need to file a claim?

Your assigned agent personally assists with the claim — you're never routed to a 1-800 call center. We prepare the paperwork, submit it to the carrier, follow up on your behalf and stay on the file until it's paid. That's our claims help guarantee, and it applies for the entire life of your policy.

Are you licensed and insured in Florida?

Yes. Cypress Ridge Life & Health holds Florida Department of Financial Services Life & Health Agent License #W284917, carries errors and omissions insurance, and all staff are background-checked and bonded. We are members of NAIFA and the Florida Association of Insurance Agents, and we are licensed in Florida only.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Often, yes. Simplified-issue and no-medical-exam products are available for applicants from age 0 to 85. Approval can be based on health questions alone instead of bloodwork and a paramedical exam — which is ideal for busy Orlando families, self-employed workers and anyone managing a chronic condition.

How is an independent agent different from a captive agent?

A captive agent works for one insurance company and can only sell that company's products. As an independent insurance agency in Orlando, we hold appointments with 25+ carriers, so we can place your case with the underwriter most likely to approve it at the best rate class. That is the single biggest reason our clients save money.
