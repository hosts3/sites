Frequently Asked Questions

Does using an insurance agent cost me more than buying direct?

No. Premiums are filed with the state and are identical whether you buy direct or through a licensed agent. Cypress Ridge charges no broker fees, no mark-ups, and no monthly service charges — agents are paid commissions by the carriers. You simply get professional guidance at the same price.

What makes an agent "independent," and why does it matter?

An independent agent contracts with many insurance companies rather than being employed by one. Cypress Ridge holds 25+ carrier appointments, so your health history, budget, and goals can be matched across multiple underwriters instead of being squeezed into a single company's product line.

How much does term life insurance cost in Orlando right now?

As an estimate, healthy 35-year-olds commonly see 20-year term policies near $18–$25 per month for $250,000 of coverage, and final expense policies typically run $35–$90 per month depending on age and health. Actual pricing requires a personalized quote because underwriting classes, tobacco use, and medical history all move the number.

Can I really get an ACA Marketplace plan for $0–$50 a month?

Many Central Florida households qualify for premium tax credits that reduce Marketplace premiums dramatically — sometimes to $0 monthly — and Silver plan cost-sharing reductions can lower deductibles and copays further. Eligibility depends on projected household income and family size, so a certified agent should run the calculation before you enroll.

Do I have to buy anything during the first appointment?

Never. Every consultation is covered by a zero-pressure guarantee — there is no obligation to purchase and no decision has to be made on the first call. Serious illness, pregnancy, and job changes can also qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period, so we only move forward when the timing and the plan are right for you.

What if I already have coverage from another agency?

Bring your current policy and ask for the free 15-minute policy review. It is genuinely free and there is no obligation to switch. If a comparable policy fits better — or if your existing one is already solid — we tell you that plainly. Cypress Ridge also offers a 30-day policy review guarantee and honors Florida's free-look period on every policy.
