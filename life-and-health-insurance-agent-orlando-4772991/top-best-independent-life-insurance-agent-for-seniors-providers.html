Frequently Asked Questions

What does an independent life insurance agent for seniors actually do differently?

An independent agent holds appointments with many carriers instead of one. That means one application conversation can produce several quotes, and if your health history gets declined by one company, the same agent can submit to another. A captive agent can only offer their employer's product, so a decline often ends the conversation.

How much does it cost to work with an independent agent in Orlando?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, needs analysis, applications, and annual reviews are free to the client. Commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, not by you, and there are no broker fees, premium mark-ups, or monthly service charges. As a general estimate, term coverage for a healthy 35-year-old at $250,000 often starts around $18–$25 per month, while final expense policies typically run $35–$90 per month depending on age and health. Your actual rate requires a quote.

What if I have health conditions like diabetes, COPD, or a past heart attack?

You still have options. Simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue final expense policies for ages 50–85 frequently approve applicants with managed diabetes, COPD, and prior cardiac events. Guaranteed-issue plans include a graded benefit period — usually two years — during which the death benefit is limited to premiums paid plus interest. A good agent will explain that trade-off before you apply rather than after.

Can one agent handle both my life insurance and my Medicare or ACA coverage?

Yes, and it is often better that way. One agent who knows your prescriptions, doctors, and budget can avoid duplicating coverage or creating gaps. During Medicare's Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 – December 7), having a single point of contact also means one call instead of three when your drug plan formulary changes.

Do I have to buy on the first call?

No. Any reputable agency operates on a zero-pressure basis, and no decision has to be made on the first call. Look for a written 30-day policy review guarantee and confirmation that your Florida free-look period is honored — typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product — with a full refund of premium if you change your mind.

How quickly can I get an appointment in the Orlando area?

Same-week appointments are usually available, including in-home and evening slots Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM. Virtual appointments via Zoom or phone are offered statewide in Florida. During the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period, extended hours run Monday through Saturday 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM with Sunday hours from 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM.

What should I ask an agency before I share my health information?

Ask three things: what license number they hold, how many carriers they are appointed with, and how they are compensated. A licensed Florida Life & Health agent number (for example, #W284917) is verifiable through the Florida Department of Financial Services. If an agent will not answer those three questions plainly, keep shopping.
