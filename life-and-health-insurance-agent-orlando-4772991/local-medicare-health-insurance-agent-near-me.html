Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Orlando

These are the questions our Orlando agents hear most often. Don't see yours? Call +1-540-352-6249 or email hello@cypressridgelifehealth.com — every message gets a reply within one business day.

How much does it cost to use a local Medicare health insurance agent near me in Orlando?

Nothing. Consultations, needs analyses, plan comparisons, applications and annual reviews are completely free to you. Insurance carriers pay our commission directly, and we never add broker fees, mark-ups on premiums or monthly service charges. You pay the same premium whether you enroll through us or directly with the carrier.

What is the difference between an independent insurance agency and a captive agent?

A captive agent represents one insurance company and can only sell that company's products. Cypress Ridge is an independent Orlando insurance agency with more than 25 carrier appointments, so we can compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ACA Marketplace and life insurance plans side by side and recommend what genuinely fits — not what a single carrier happens to offer this quarter.

When should I start shopping for Medicare if I turn 65 this year?

Start about three months before your 65th birthday month. Your Initial Enrollment Period runs seven months total — three before, your birthday month, and three after — and enrolling on time protects you from late-enrollment penalties. We hold a free Turning-65 workshop twice monthly at our East Orlando and Kissimmee locations; registration is required and seating is limited.

Can you really get my ACA Marketplace plan down to $0 per month?

Many Central Florida households do qualify for premium tax credits that bring Marketplace plans to $0–$50 per month, but it depends on household size, income and the second-lowest-cost silver plan in your ZIP code. We are HealthCare.gov (FFM) certified every plan year and will calculate your exact subsidy during a free appointment — no guesswork.

How fast can I get covered?

ACA Marketplace enrollments usually take 20–40 minutes and can be effective the first of the following month. Medicare Advantage and Part D enrollments are typically confirmed within 24–48 hours. Term life policies with a medical exam typically issue in 7–14 days, while simplified-issue and final expense policies can be approved the same day.

What happens if a new policy isn't the right fit?

You're protected twice. First, our 30-day policy review guarantee means we re-shop the policy at no cost to you within 30 days of issue. Second, Florida's free-look period — typically 10 to 30 days depending on the product — lets you cancel for a full refund of premium. And nothing has to be decided on your first call; that's our zero-pressure guarantee.

Do you help with claims, or just with sales?

Both. Your assigned agent personally assists with claims filing — we sit on the phone with the carrier with you rather than handing you a 1-800 number. We also send a free annual coverage review every year to check whether your doctors, prescriptions or premiums have changed enough to warrant switching plans.

I already have life or health insurance through another agency. Can you still help?

Yes. We offer a free 15-minute policy review for anyone who already owns life or health coverage through a different agency in Orlando. We'll compare your current plan against three alternatives and tell you honestly if you should stay put. There is no obligation and we won't ask you to cancel anything on the first call.

Do you offer in-home or evening appointments?

Yes. Our office is at 1214 E. Robinson Street, Suite 210, Orlando, FL 32801, and walk-ins are welcome — though appointments are preferred. We also offer in-home appointments and evening slots Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM, plus Zoom or phone appointments anywhere in Florida for clients who prefer virtual meetings.

How much does term life insurance cost for a healthy person in Orlando?

As an estimate that requires a personalized quote, a healthy 35-year-old often sees $250,000 of 20-year term coverage start around $18–$25 per month. Final expense policies typically run $35–$90 per month depending on age and health.
