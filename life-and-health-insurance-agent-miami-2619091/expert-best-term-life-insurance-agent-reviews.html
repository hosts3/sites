Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Miami

Straight answers to the questions our Miami clients ask most often. If your question is not here, call +1-540-352-6249 — we answer in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

How much does term life insurance cost in Miami in 2026?
A healthy 30-year-old in Miami can typically get $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old usually pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use, coverage amount and term length — and because we shop 18+ carriers, we frequently beat single-carrier quotes.
Do you charge a fee for quotes, consultations or policy reviews?
No. Every consultation, quote, policy review and enrollment appointment is 100% free with no obligation and no cancellation fees from us. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carriers, not by you, so your premium is the same whether you work with us or apply directly.
How fast can I get approved for life insurance?
With accelerated underwriting, many healthy applicants are approved within 24 to 72 hours and no medical exam is required. Fully underwritten policies with labs and medical records typically take 2 to 4 weeks. Guaranteed issue final expense policies for ages 50–85 can sometimes be issued the same day with no health questions.
Do you also write auto insurance and home insurance in Miami?
Our Florida licenses and specialty focus are life, health, Medicare and annuities. For auto insurance quotes and home insurance in Miami, we refer clients to trusted independent property and casualty agents in our network — and we will review your life and health coverage at the same appointment so nothing gets overlooked.
How do you get paid if consultations are free?
Carriers pay our agency a commission after a policy is issued, and that commission is already built into the carrier's published rate. You pay the same premium as buying direct, but you gain independent comparison, bilingual education and claims advocacy at no extra cost.
What is included in the free policy review?
We review policies you already own — even ones we did not sell. That includes death benefit adequacy, premium competitiveness, cash value performance, loan exposure, rider value and beneficiary designations. We will also check whether a different carrier would approve you at a lower rate today. Bring your policy documents or just the carrier name and policy number.
Can you help if I have been declined for life insurance before?
Yes — this is one of our specialties. We work with Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, Gerber Life and other carriers that underwrite diabetic, heart condition and cancer-survivor cases, plus guaranteed issue coverage for ages 50–85 with no health questions and no medical exam. Declined cases are often approved with the right carrier.
Do you offer bilingual service in Spanish or Haitian Creole?
Sí — and wi. Our Little Havana office provides full service in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole by phone, in person or by video appointment. Policy explanations, beneficiary forms and plan comparisons are all available in your preferred language, and bilingual phone support covers every department.
What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap?
Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with network-based coverage, often at a $0 monthly premium and frequently including dental, vision, hearing and prescription coverage. Medigap supplements Original Medicare with no networks and no referrals required; Plan G rates in Miami-Dade generally run $115–$210 per month depending on age and tobacco use.
What enrollment deadlines should I know about in 2026?
Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 – December 7 each year. ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 – January 15. Life insurance, final expense and annuities have no enrollment window and can be applied for year-round. Outside open enrollment, a Special Enrollment Period may apply after a qualifying life event such as marriage, a move or job loss.
How quickly do you respond to quote requests and urgent questions?
Our written 24-hour response guarantee covers every quote request and client question. In practice, our average first response is under 45 minutes during business hours. Voicemail and text messages are monitored for urgent claims and enrollment questions, and extended evening video appointments run Monday–Thursday until 8:00 PM during Medicare and ACA enrollment periods.
Do you help beneficiaries file claims and handle paperwork?
Yes. Claims advocacy is included with every policy we place, at no charge. We contact the carrier, request and complete the required forms, track the claim through underwriting review and follow up until payment is issued. One recent final expense claim was paid to a client's mother in 11 days with zero paperwork handled by the family.
Are your agents properly licensed and accredited?
Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — our agency operates under License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). Our Medicare team completes annual AHIP certification plus Fraud, Waste & Abuse training and follows CMS-compliant marketing standards. The agency is a NABIP member and has been A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015.
