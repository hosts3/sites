Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Port St. Lucie, Florida

Updated for 2026 — the questions Treasure Coast clients ask us most often, answered honestly.

Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in Port St. Lucie, Florida?

Base rates are filed with the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation, so the premium for the same carrier, product and underwriting class is normally identical either way. The difference is service and accuracy: an independent agent compares eight carriers at once, pre-screens your health profile so you land in the right class, and handles the paperwork for free. Clients who skip that screening often end up paying more because they were placed in a worse rate class — or declined entirely — at an online checkout.

How much does life insurance cost in Port St. Lucie, Florida in 2026?

A healthy 30-year-old can typically secure $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for about $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 ranges from $32 to $95 per month, depending on age and coverage amount between $5,000 and $25,000. Your exact rate depends on age, tobacco use, build and medical history.

How long does the life insurance approval process take in Florida?

Simplified-issue policies with no medical exam can be approved in 24 to 72 hours. Fully underwritten policies — which usually produce the lowest rates — generally take 2 to 6 weeks depending on how quickly your physician's office returns records. Guaranteed issue policies for ages 50–85 with no health questions usually issue within 7 to 10 business days.

Do I pay anything to work with your insurance agency?

No. Consultations, comparisons, applications and annual policy reviews are completely free with no obligation. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carriers, not by clients.
