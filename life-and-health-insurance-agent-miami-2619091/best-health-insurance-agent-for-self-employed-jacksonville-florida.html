What makes you the best health insurance agent for self employed Jacksonville Florida workers?

How much does term life insurance cost for a healthy 40-year-old in Jacksonville?

Do you help small Jacksonville businesses with employee benefits?

Yes. We design group health, dental, vision, life and disability packages for Jacksonville businesses with 5 to 50 employees, coordinate carrier quotes, and run on-site or virtual enrollment meetings so your staff understands their options. We also build Section 125 documentation and handle ongoing employee changes at no extra cost.

What if I cannot qualify for coverage because of my health history?

We have guaranteed-issue life insurance for ages 50 to 85 with no health questions and no medical exam, plus several carriers that accept controlled conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol and sleep apnea. On the health side, ACA Marketplace plans cannot deny you or charge more for pre-existing conditions.

How is an independent insurance agency different from a captive agent or a website?

A captive agent can only sell one company's products, and a comparison website cannot explain why a plan looks cheap but excludes your cardiologist. An independent agency is appointed with 18+ carriers, is paid the same regardless of which plan you choose, and is legally obligated to document the advice given. That is the difference between shopping and being advised.
