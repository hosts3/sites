Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance in Jacksonville, Florida

Real questions our licensed agents answer every week from Duval, Clay and St. Johns County clients.

Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in Jacksonville, Florida?

Almost never cheaper online. Life insurance premiums are set by the carrier based on your health class and policy details — not on how you applied. A 20-year, $500,000 term policy for a healthy 45-year-old runs roughly $58–$75 per month whether you buy direct or through us. Working with an agent simply means someone compares 2–4 carriers for you at no extra cost, because the commission is paid by the insurer.

Do I pay a fee when I work with a life and health insurance agent?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free with zero obligation. Agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier out of the premium you already owe. We also offer a rate-shopping guarantee: find a lower rate for identical coverage and we will match it or explain exactly why the difference exists.

How fast can I get approved for life insurance in Jacksonville?

No-medical-exam policies using accelerated underwriting can issue in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Fully underwritten term coverage typically takes 3 to 10 business days, depending on how quickly the paramedical exam and medical records arrive. We schedule exams at your home or office anywhere in the Jacksonville area, including evenings and Saturdays.

Do I need a medical exam to buy life insurance in Florida?

Not always. Applicants 18–60 in good health frequently qualify for accelerated underwriting with no exam and no blood draw. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 usually use a short health questionnaire instead of an exam, and guaranteed-issue plans for ages 50–85 require no health questions at all, though they carry a two-year graded benefit period.

Should I buy life insurance online or talk to an agent first?

Do both, in that order. Get online quotes to establish a baseline, then have an agent verify them. Online engines rarely show which carrier will approve your specific health history, whether a living-benefits rider is included, or how the beneficiary designation should be written. Those three details cause the majority of denied and delayed claims we see.

How much does term life insurance cost for a 35-year-old in Jacksonville?

As of 2026, a healthy 35-year-old non-smoker can typically secure $250,000 of 20-year term coverage in the mid-$20s per month, and $500,000 for roughly double that. Rate depends on build, nicotine use, blood pressure, family history and driving record. Call +1-540-352-6249 for a firm quote on your exact profile.

Can you help me compare Medicare Advantage and Medigap plans in Jacksonville?

Yes. We are AHIP Medicare certified annually and CMS-compliant. We check whether your physicians, specialists and hospital — including Baptist Health, Mayo Clinic Florida and UF Health Jacksonville — are in-network, then compare your 2026 drug formulary tiers. Many Medicare Advantage plans are available at $0 monthly premium, while Medigap Plan G typically runs $115–$210 per month in this market.

What if I get denied for health insurance because of a pre-existing condition?

You still have options. ACA Marketplace plans cannot deny you or charge more for pre-existing conditions, and most Northeast Florida households qualify for premium tax credits that bring many plans under $50 per month. If you missed Open Enrollment, we screen you for a Special Enrollment Period or point you to Florida KidCare and short-term alternatives.

What exactly is included in the free policy review?

We read your current policies line by line — including ones you did not buy from us — and check the death benefit versus your current income and mortgage, beneficiary designations, riders, cash-value performance on permanent policies, and whether a cheaper identical product exists. Last year, these reviews uncovered unnecessary coverage and rate gaps for hundreds of Florida households, at no cost.

Yes. You call our office first. We contact the carrier, confirm the correct claim forms, help gather the certified death certificate and supporting documents, and follow the file until funds are released to your beneficiary. That claims advocacy service is included for the life of your policy — and it is the single biggest thing an online checkout page cannot do for you.
