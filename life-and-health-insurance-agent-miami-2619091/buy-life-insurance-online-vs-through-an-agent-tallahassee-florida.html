Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Tallahassee, Florida

Straight answers to the questions Tallahassee families ask us most — including the honest pros and cons of buying online versus through an agent.

Is it cheaper to buy life insurance online or through an agent in Tallahassee, Florida?
Life insurance premiums are filed with the state, so the base rate for the same policy with the same carrier is identical whether you apply online or through a licensed agent. Where it changes is your underwriting class. An agent pre-screens your health profile and matches you to the carrier most likely to approve you at the best class — often saving 20–40% versus applying blind online. You also never pay an agent fee; the carrier pays the commission.
How much does term life insurance cost in Tallahassee, Florida in 2026?
As a benchmark, a healthy 30-year-old can usually place $250,000 of 20-year term coverage from approximately $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old can typically place $500,000 of 20-year term from about $58–$75 per month. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally range from $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of coverage. Your exact rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use and coverage amount.
How long does it take to get approved and covered?
Fully underwritten term and whole life policies typically issue in 3 to 10 business days, though some carriers with accelerated underwriting can finish in 24–72 hours. Simplified-issue final expense coverage is frequently approved within 48 hours with no medical exam. Guaranteed issue policies (ages 50–85, no health questions) usually issue in about a week, with a graded benefit period for the first two years.
What exactly does a free consultation include — is there any cost to me?
There is never a fee to you. Our consultation includes a full needs analysis, pre-screening against multiple carriers' underwriting guidelines, two to four written side-by-side quotes, and a plain-language explanation of riders, exclusions and beneficiary rules. Commissions are paid entirely by the insurance carrier. You are also never asked to commit during your first appointment.
Can I compare buying life insurance online vs through an agent in Tallahassee, Florida using my own online quotes?
Absolutely — bring them. Our rate-shopping guarantee says that if you find a lower rate for identical coverage, we will either match it or explain exactly why the difference exists (usually a different underwriting class, a shorter rate-lock period, or a missing rider). Side-by-side comparison is how we earn trust, and it costs you nothing but about 20 minutes.
What credentials and licenses do you hold?
Our agency operates under Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only). We are AHIP Medicare and Fraud, Waste & Abuse certified annually, CMS-compliant for Medicare marketing and enrollment, members of the National Association of Benefits and Insurance Professionals (NABIP), and A+ Better Business Bureau accredited since 2015. All 8 agents hold active Florida licenses and complete continuing education every year.
Can I get coverage if I have health conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure or a previous decline?
In most cases, yes. Several carriers we represent specialize in controlled diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol and prior declines. We assess which carrier treats your specific condition most favorably — sometimes the difference between a table-rated approval and a preferred class is thousands of dollars over the life of the policy. For ages 50–85 we also have guaranteed issue options with no health questions at all.
Can you help me with Medicare or ACA Marketplace coverage too?
Yes. We are AHIP-certified for Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D drug plans, and we provide $0-cost enrollment help for ACA Marketplace plans and Florida KidCare. Medicare Advantage plans in Leon County are frequently available at $0 monthly premium, and most Marketplace clients qualify for premium tax credits that bring net premiums under $50 per month.
What happens if I need to file a claim — do you actually help?
Yes, and this is the single biggest difference between online purchasing and working with an agent. Our claims advocacy service means we contact the carrier, confirm the required documents, submit the death certificate and beneficiary paperwork, and follow up until the claim is paid. Your family never has to navigate a phone tree alone during an already difficult time.
