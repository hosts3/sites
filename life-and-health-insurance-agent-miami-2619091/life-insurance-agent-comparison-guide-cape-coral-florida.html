How much does life insurance cost in Cape Coral, Florida in 2026?

Rates depend on age, health and term length. As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Cape Coral resident typically qualifies for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage for approximately $18–$24 per month. A healthy 45-year-old applying for $500,000 of 20-year term usually sees $58–$75 per month. Final expense coverage for ages 45–85 generally runs $32–$95 per month for $5,000–$25,000 of protection. We confirm your exact rate with a soft-check quote in under 24 hours.

Do you charge a fee for comparing insurance carriers or reviewing my policy?

Never. Every consultation, carrier comparison, needs analysis and annual policy review is completely free, with no obligation and no hidden fees. Our agents are compensated directly by the insurance carrier only after a policy is issued. You can review coverage we did not sell you — many clients bring us an existing policy for a free second opinion.

How quickly can I get a quote, and how long until my policy is issued?

Quotes are returned within 24 hours and usually within 4 business hours. Fully underwritten term life policies typically issue in 2–4 weeks. Simplified-issue and final expense products can be approved in as little as 24–72 hours, and guaranteed issue policies for ages 50–85 require no medical exam or health questions at all.

What if I have diabetes, high blood pressure or a past decline?

You still have options. We are appointed with 8 life carriers, including several that specialize in impaired-risk underwriting. Clients with controlled diabetes, hypertension, elevated cholesterol or a tobacco history frequently qualify for standard or table-rated coverage. If a fully underwritten policy is not realistic, we pivot to simplified-issue or guaranteed-issue products rather than leaving you uninsured.
