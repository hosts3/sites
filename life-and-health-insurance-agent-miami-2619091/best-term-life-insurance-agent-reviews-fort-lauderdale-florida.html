Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agent in Fort Lauderdale, Florida

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from Broward County residents. Still unsure? Call +1-540-352-6249 — no obligation.

How much does term life insurance cost in Fort Lauderdale, Florida?

In 2026, a healthy non-smoking 30-year-old in Fort Lauderdale typically pays about $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage, while a healthy 45-year-old pays roughly $58–$75 per month for $500,000. Your final rate depends on age, health history, tobacco use and the carrier — which is exactly why we compare 8 carriers before quoting you.

How long does it take to get approved for life insurance?

Most fully underwritten policies are issued in 2–4 weeks, and many accelerated-underwriting products are approved in 24–72 hours with no medical exam. Application submission typically happens within 48 hours of your free consultation, and we track underwriting status daily so nothing stalls.

Do you charge a fee for your insurance agent services?

No. Consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are completely free, with no obligation. Our agents are compensated by the insurance carrier, not by you, so the premium you see is the premium you pay. There are no broker fees, service charges or hidden costs of any kind.

Can I get life insurance with no medical exam?

Yes. We offer simplified-issue and guaranteed-issue products — including final expense coverage for ages 50–85 with no health questions at all. Guaranteed-issue policies carry a graded benefit for the first two years, and we explain exactly how that works before you apply so there are no surprises.

What if I already have a life insurance policy from another agency?

Bring it in. Our free policy review covers policies you did not buy from us — we check your rate against 2026 carrier pricing, confirm your beneficiaries are current, and look for gaps like missing living benefits or an expiring term. If your existing policy is better, we will tell you to keep it.

Do you help with Medicare when I turn 65?

Absolutely. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant, and we compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap and Part D drug plans from Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna and Cigna. We also host a free bilingual Medicare workshop at our Little Havana office on the first Wednesday of every month.

Can you help me get ACA Marketplace coverage with a subsidy?

Yes, at zero cost to you. Most Broward County households we assist qualify for premium tax credits, and many pay under $50 per month after subsidies. We handle the application, income documentation and plan comparison, and we also provide free Florida KidCare enrollment help for families.

How fast do you respond to quote requests and client questions?

Our average first response time is under 4 hours, and every quote request or client question is answered within one business day under our written 24-hour response guarantee. Active clients with urgent claims or billing questions can reach us by phone at +1-540-352-6249 during and outside business hours.

What licenses and credentials do your agents hold?

Every agent holds an active Florida Department of Financial Services license — 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) or 2-40 (Health) — plus annual continuing education and yearly AHIP Medicare certification. Our agency has held A+ Better Business Bureau accreditation since 2015 and is a NABIP member.

Do you help beneficiaries file claims?

Yes. Claims advocacy is included with every policy we place. Our licensed staff contacts the carrier, requests the correct forms, submits the death certificate and required documentation, and follows the claim through to payment. Your family never has to navigate a call center alone during a difficult time.

Is life insurance through an independent agent cheaper than buying direct?

In most cases, yes — because carrier pricing for identical coverage is the same whether you buy direct or through an agent, but an independent agency like ours can show you the lowest of 8 carriers instead of one. If you find a lower published rate for identical coverage, we will match it or explain the difference in writing.

What areas of Fort Lauderdale and Broward County do you serve?

We serve all of Broward County — including downtown Fort Lauderdale, Las Olas, Victoria Park, Coral Ridge, Wilton Manors, Oakland Park, Hollywood, Plantation, Sunrise, Pompano Beach, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Davie and Coral Springs — plus Miami-Dade, Monroe County and statewide video appointments for life insurance.
