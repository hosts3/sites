How much does it cost to work with a health insurance agent in Hialeah, Florida?

Nothing. Consultations, quotes, plan comparisons and annual policy reviews are 100% free with no fees, no obligation and no hidden charges. Our commissions are paid directly by the insurance carrier, not by you, so the premium you pay is identical whether you enroll through us or on your own. You simply get expert help for the same price.

I'm self-employed with no employer benefits — what are my health insurance options in Hialeah?

You have three main paths: an ACA Marketplace plan with premium tax credits (usually the best value if your 1099 income is moderate), a private short-term or association plan (typically faster approval but fewer consumer protections), or coverage through a spouse's employer plan. Most self-employed Hialeah clients we help qualify for subsidies that bring premiums to under $50 per month.

When is open enrollment for 2026, and can I enroll outside that window?

ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15 for 2026 coverage. Outside that window you may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period if you've had a qualifying life event — marriage, divorce, a new baby, loss of employer coverage, a move, or a change in income. Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7. Call us and we'll confirm your eligibility in minutes.

How quickly can I get a quote and get covered?

We respond to every quote request within 24 hours, and usually within 2 hours during business hours. ACA Marketplace applications can be submitted the same day we speak, and coverage often starts the first of the following month. Life insurance quotes are typically delivered within one business day; policies with a medical exam generally issue in two to five weeks depending on the carrier.

Do you help if I already have a policy I bought somewhere else?

Yes — and it's one of our most popular services. Our free policy review covers policies you didn't purchase through us. We check your coverage amounts, beneficiary designations, premium versus current market rates, and whether your doctors and prescriptions are still in network. There's no cost and no obligation to change anything. Many clients discover they were overpaying by $100 or more per month.

Can I get life insurance if I have diabetes, high blood pressure or a past decline?

In most cases, yes. We work with carriers like Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo and Gerber Life that offer simplified issue and guaranteed issue products with few or no health questions. Guaranteed issue coverage is available for ages 50 to 85 with no medical exam and no health questions. We'll run several carriers to find your best available underwriting class.

How do ACA subsidies actually work for someone with 1099 income?

Your premium tax credit is based on your projected household income for the coverage year, compared with the federal poverty level for your household size. Because 1099 income fluctuates, we help you estimate conservatively and report changes during the year to avoid repayment surprises at tax time. This is the single most common mistake self-employed Hialeah residents make when they enroll alone.
