How much does it cost to work with a life and health insurance agent in Miami, Florida?

Nothing, ever. Every consultation, quote, needs analysis and annual policy review from Biscayne Bay Life & Health Insurance Group is 100% free with no obligation. Our commissions are paid by the insurance carriers, not by you, which is exactly why we can stay independent and recommend the plan that fits you rather than the plan that pays us the most.

How fast can I get a life insurance quote in Miami?

Most quote requests receive a response within 24 hours and many are answered the same business day. Fully underwritten term life policies usually take two to four weeks to issue, while final expense and guaranteed issue policies can be approved in 24 to 72 hours because there is often no medical exam required.

Do you help with Medicare Advantage and Medigap plans in Miami-Dade?

Yes. Our agents are AHIP certified and CMS-compliant every year and represent Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Cigna, Simply Healthcare, Molina and Doctor's Health Plan. Many Medicare Advantage plans in Miami-Dade cost $0 per month, and Medicare Supplement Plan G typically ranges from $115 to $210 monthly depending on your age at enrollment.

Can you help me qualify for ACA Marketplace subsidies or Florida KidCare?

Absolutely, and at no cost to you. Most Miami-Dade households qualify for premium tax credits, and many of our clients end up paying under $50 per month for Marketplace coverage. We also complete Florida KidCare applications for families at zero charge — bring your income estimate and household information and we will handle the rest.

What if I already have a life insurance policy from another agent?

We will still review it for free. Since 2011 our agency has reviewed more than 5,000 policies purchased elsewhere and frequently finds outdated beneficiaries, missing living benefits, or identical coverage at a lower premium. If your current policy is genuinely the best fit for your family, we will tell you that honestly and send you home with no changes.

Do you speak Spanish or Haitian Creole?

Sí, completamente. Our 8 licensed agents and 3 support staff serve clients in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. You can meet in person at our Little Havana office on SW 8th Street or our Kendall office on SW 117th Avenue, by phone, or by video appointment if that is easier for your schedule.

How much does term life insurance cost for a healthy 30-year-old in Miami?

Our rate-shopping guarantee covers that.
