How much does life insurance cost in Cape Coral, Florida?

For a healthy 30-year-old, $250,000 of 20-year term coverage typically runs $18–$24 per month; a healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term for about $58–$75 monthly. Final expense policies for ages 45–85 generally fall between $32 and $95 per month depending on age and coverage amount. Your personalized quote is free.

Do I have to take a medical exam to get life insurance?

Not always. Many carriers now offer accelerated underwriting with no exam for healthy applicants under 60, using prescription and motor vehicle records instead. Guaranteed-issue final expense plans for ages 50–85 require no medical exam and no health questions at all. We pre-screen you first so you are not declined unnecessarily.

How long does it take to get covered?

Term life applications with no medical exam frequently issue in 24–48 hours. Traditional fully underwritten policies take two to four weeks. ACA Marketplace health plans can be effective the first of the next month if you enroll before the monthly cutoff, and Medicare Advantage or Part D enrollments typically take effect the first of the following month.

Is your consultation really free, and how do you get paid?

Yes — consultations, quotes, policy reviews and enrollment help are 100% free with no obligation. Our commission is paid directly by the insurance carrier you choose, not by you. That means your premium is identical whether you work with us or apply online, but you get a licensed agent advocating for you at no extra cost.

Do you only sell life insurance, or health and Medicare too?

We are licensed in both life and health, so you can handle everything in one place: term life, whole life, IUL, final expense, mortgage protection, Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement (Medigap), Part D drug plans, ACA Marketplace coverage, Florida KidCare, small group benefits and annuities. One agent, one relationship.

Can you review the policy I already own?

You call us before you call the carrier. Premium tax credits are calculated from your projected household income and family size, then applied directly to your monthly Marketplace bill.
