Frequently Asked Questions About Life and Health Insurance Agents in Tampa, Florida

Updated for 2026 — the questions our Tampa clients ask most often before their first appointment.

What is the difference between an independent insurance agent and a captive agent in Tampa, Florida?

A captive agent works for one insurance carrier and may only sell that company's products — their job is to place you with their employer. An independent insurance agent is appointed with many carriers, so we can quote Mutual of Omaha, Transamerica, John Hancock, Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare and more in one sitting, then recommend whichever fits your health, budget and family situation best. That structural difference is the whole point of the independent insurance agent vs captive agent Tampa Florida comparison.

Do independent insurance agents cost more than captive agents in Tampa?

No — it is usually the opposite. In Florida, agent commissions are paid by the insurance carrier, never by the client. Our consultations, quotes, enrollments and annual policy reviews are 100% free with zero obligation. Because independent agents are not locked into one rate card, clients frequently save several hundred dollars a year on identical coverage.

How much does term life insurance cost in Tampa, Florida in 2026?

As of 2026, a healthy 30-year-old Tampa resident typically pays about $18–$24 per month for $250,000 of 20-year term coverage. A healthy 45-year-old generally pays $58–$75 per month for $500,000 of 20-year term. Final rates depend on your age, tobacco use, height/weight and family medical history — that is why we quote several carriers at once.

How fast can I get a quote and have coverage in force?

We respond to every quote request within 24 hours, and most Tampa clients hear back the same business day — often within 45 minutes during office hours. Many life policies are approved in 24 to 72 hours through accelerated underwriting. Simplified-issue final expense plans can be approved the same week, and some require no medical exam at all.

Can you help me enroll in Medicare in Tampa, Florida?

Yes. We are AHIP-certified and CMS-compliant for Medicare Advantage (Part C), Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Medicare Part D prescription drug plans. Many Medicare Advantage plans available in Hillsborough County carry a $0 monthly premium, and Medigap Plan G rates in Florida typically range from $115 to $210 per month depending on age and gender. Our Medicare Annual Enrollment support runs October 15 through December 7.

Do you charge any fee for consultations, quotes or policy reviews?

Never. Every consultation, quote, enrollment and annual policy review is completely free with no obligation. We will also review a policy you purchased from another agent or a captive carrier at no cost. If we cannot improve your coverage or your premium, we will tell you so honestly instead of pushing a product.

Is your agency licensed to sell insurance in Tampa and Hillsborough County?

Yes. We hold Florida Department of Financial Services License 2-15 (Life, Health & Annuity) and License 2-40 (Health Insurance Only), and we complete annual continuing education plus AHIP Medicare certification. Life insurance is licensed and available statewide across Florida, so Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel and Clearwater clients can be served in person, by phone or by secure video.

What if I have health conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure or a past cancer diagnosis?

You still have strong options. We work with carriers such as Mutual of Omaha, Americo, Gerber Life and Foresters Financial that specialize in simplified-issue underwriting for managed diabetes, controlled hypertension and many other conditions. Guaranteed-issue life insurance for ages 50–85 asks no health questions whatsoever — coverage is available to nearly everyone who applies.

Do you help self-employed workers and gig-economy drivers in Tampa get health coverage?

Absolutely — 1099 contractors, rideshare and delivery drivers, restaurant, salon and construction workers make up a large share of our Tampa client base. We handle ACA Marketplace enrollment, calculate your premium tax credit and compare networks from Florida Blue, Ambetter, Molina, Oscar and Cigna. Many Hillsborough County households pay under $50 per month after subsidies, and Florida KidCare help is always free.

Does your agency help beneficiaries file claims when someone passes away?

Yes, and it costs nothing. Claims advocacy is included with every policy we place. Our team calls the carrier, requests the correct forms, gathers the death certificate and required documents, and follows the claim through to payment — so your family does not have to navigate a complicated process during an already difficult time.

Should I buy life insurance through my employer or through an independent agent?

Group life through an employer is usually a good starting point, but it is typically capped at one or two times your salary and it disappears when you change jobs. Most Tampa clients pair employer coverage with an individually owned term policy that stays with them regardless of employment. We will look at your group certificate and show you exactly where the gaps are — no charge.

What happens during the free annual policy review?

Once a year we revisit your coverage amount, beneficiaries, carrier rating and premium against current 2026 rates. We check that your beneficiary designations still match your wishes, confirm your Medicare drug plan still covers your prescriptions, and look for any new savings. The review takes about 20 minutes by phone or video and is free for every client, every year.

Why Choosing the Right Insurance Agent Matters in Tampa, Florida

Tampa Bay is one of the most competitive insurance markets in the country — and also one of the trickiest to shop. Hillsborough County homeowners deal with hurricane deductibles, flood-zone designations near Bayshore Boulevard and the Hillsborough River, and wind mitigation credits that change premiums dramatically. Renters in Channelside, Westshore and Seminole Heights need renters coverage that does not assume a homeowner policy exists. And a growing number of Tampa residents — rideshare drivers, restaurant staff, salon professionals and 1099 contractors — have no employer benefits at all, which makes individual life and health coverage their only protection.

That is exactly where the independent insurance agent vs captive agent Tampa Florida distinction becomes practical rather than theoretical. A captive agent representing a single carrier can only tell you what that one company will charge. An independent agent can place your case with the carrier whose underwriting actually fits your health profile — which is why two Tampa neighbors with identical coverage amounts can pay wildly different premiums.

We also understand the local calendar. Tampa's Medicare population is large and growing, so Annual Enrollment (October 15 – December 7) is a busy, confusing window. ACA Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15, and Florida's subsidy landscape shifts every year. During those periods we extend evening video appointments Monday through Thursday until 8:00 PM so working families in Brandon, Riverview and Wesley Chapel can get help after their shift ends.
